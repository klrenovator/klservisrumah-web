## 4. PHOTO REQUEST

### 4.1 Standard request
**EN** — Could you send 2–3 photos?
1. A close-up of the problem
2. A wider shot of the whole area
3. Something for scale if possible
That helps our team price it accurately without a wasted trip.

**BM** — Boleh hantar 2–3 gambar?
1. Gambar dekat masalah tersebut
2. Gambar lebih luas keseluruhan kawasan
3. Sesuatu untuk skala jika boleh
Ini membantu pasukan kami beri harga tepat tanpa lawatan sia-sia.

**ZH** — 可以发 2–3 张照片吗？
1. 问题部位的近拍
2. 整体区域的全景
3. 如果可以，放个参照物看比例
这能帮助我们的团队准确报价，避免白跑一趟。

### 4.2 Leak-specific
**EN** — For a leak, could you send: the wet area close-up, the wider room, and the suspected source (bathroom above, roof, or balcony) if you can reach it safely?
**BM** — Untuk kebocoran, boleh hantar: gambar dekat kawasan basah, gambar bilik lebih luas, dan punca disyaki (bilik air di atas, bumbung, atau balkoni) jika selamat dicapai?
**ZH** — 漏水问题，可以发：湿处近拍、房间全景，以及可疑源头（楼上浴室、屋顶或阳台）——如果能安全拍到的话。

### 4.3 Painting-specific
**EN** — Could you send a wide photo of each room to be painted, plus close-ups of any cracks, peeling or damp patches? That helps us scope the prep work.
**BM** — Boleh hantar gambar luas setiap bilik yang hendak dicat, serta gambar dekat sebarang retak, kelupasan atau tompok lembap? Ini bantu kami skop kerja persediaan.
**ZH** — 可以发每个待粉刷房间的全景照片，以及裂缝、剥落或潮湿部位的近拍吗？这有助于我们确定基层处理范围。

### 4.4 Chasing photos
**EN** — Whenever you get a chance, those photos would really help move things along. No rush.
**BM** — Bila-bila anda ada masa, gambar-gambar itu sangat membantu mempercepat proses. Tiada tergesa-gesa.
**ZH** — 您方便的时候发一下照片，会大大加快进度。不着急。

### 4.5 After receiving photos
**EN** — Thanks — got them. I can see {specific visible observation}. I'm passing these to our team so they can prepare an accurate quote for you.
**BM** — Terima kasih — sudah terima. Saya nampak {specific visible observation}. Saya hantar kepada pasukan kami untuk sediakan sebut harga tepat.
**ZH** — 谢谢，已收到。我看到{specific visible observation}。我会转给团队，让他们为您准备准确的报价。
> ⚠️ Describe only what is visible. **Never diagnose the cause.**

### 4.6 Customer can't send photos
**EN** — No problem at all. In that case a free site visit is the better route — our team will measure and assess on site. Which day suits you?
**BM** — Tidak mengapa langsung. Dalam kes ini, lawatan tapak percuma lebih sesuai — pasukan kami akan ukur dan nilai di tapak. Hari mana sesuai?
**ZH** — 完全没问题。这种情况下，免费上门勘查更合适——我们的团队会现场测量评估。您哪天方便？

### 8.1 Step 1 — acknowledge (always send this first)
**EN** — I'm sorry — that's not the standard we aim for. Let me get this to our supervisor right away.

**EN** — So they have the full picture, can you tell me roughly when the work was done, and what's happened since? A photo would help too, if you have one.

### 8.3 Step 3 — escalate and set expectation
**EN** — Thanks — I've passed everything to our supervisor. They'll reply here personally during our hours, 9 AM–6 PM daily.
**BM** — Terima kasih — saya sudah hantar semuanya kepada penyelia kami. Mereka akan balas di sini secara peribadi dalam waktu operasi, 9 pagi–6 petang setiap hari.

### 8.4 Very angry / abusive customer
**EN** — I understand you're frustrated. I've escalated this to our supervisor and they'll respond to you personally.
**BM** — Saya faham anda kecewa. Saya telah naikkan perkara ini kepada penyelia kami dan mereka akan balas kepada anda secara peribadi.
**ZH** — 我理解您的不满。我已将此事上报给我们的主管，他们会亲自回复您。

> **Never:** defend, explain the cause, blame anyone, say "that's not covered", or offer a refund, discount, compensation or free re-do. Escalate on the **first** complaint message.
