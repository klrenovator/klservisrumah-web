## SECTION H — SERVICE-SPECIFIC

### Painting
**Q: How long to paint a 3-bedroom apartment?**
A: A standard 3-bedroom, 1,000 sq ft apartment usually takes 2–3 days — including wall prep, drying between coats and final cleaning. A single room is often done in a day.

**Q: Is wall repair included?**
A: Basic hairline crack patching, plaster touch-ups and nail-hole filling are included in the standard painting package. Major structural cracking, heavy peeling or water-damaged plaster is quoted separately and transparently.

**Q: Will you protect my furniture?**
A: Yes — furniture, flooring, switches and windows are fully covered with heavy-duty drop sheets and masking tape, and we vacuum and sweep before handover.

### Plumbing
**Q: Can you find a leak without breaking my tiles?**
A: Usually, yes. We use acoustic leak detectors and thermal imaging to trace the exact point, so often only 1–2 tiles need removing rather than the whole wall.

**Q: How fast for a burst pipe?**
A: I'm treating this as urgent and sending it to our dispatch team now. Can you confirm your full address and best contact number? → **P1 EMERGENCY**

**Q: Why is my water pressure low?**
A: It can be several things — supply drops, partially closed valves, internal pipe clogging in older galvanised pipes, or a leak. Our team inspects the main lines and can install a booster pump if needed.

**Q: Do you supply the fixtures?**
A: Both ways work. Our plumbers carry standard quality valves, pipes, faucets and siphons — or we can install a toilet, sink or mixer you've bought.

### Ceiling
**Q: Why is my ceiling cracking or sagging?**
A: Common causes are water leaks from above, weak timber frames instead of galvanised steel, or building settlement. We reinforce the hangers, replace weak boards with moisture- or fire-rated gypsum, and seal joints with fibreglass mesh. ⚠️ *If it's actively sagging → P1 safety escalation.*

**Q: Will there be a lot of dust?**
A: Plaster sanding does create fine dust, but we seal the area with plastic containment, cover furniture and use dust-extraction sanders — cutting dust by around 80% — then vacuum and sweep thoroughly.

**Q: Can you cut holes for downlights and fans?**
A: Yes — clean circular holes for downlights, reinforced timber fan hooks behind the ceiling so fans are safe, and neat openings for aircond cassettes.

**Q: What's the difference between plaster ceiling and gypsum board?**
A: They're the same thing — "plaster ceiling" is the local term for gypsum plasterboard on a metal frame, finished with skim coat. We always use galvanised iron frames rather than wood.

### Waterproofing
**Q: What is PU grouting?**
A: A non-destructive method: we drill small ports and inject liquid polyurethane, which expands and cures into a flexible, permanent seal — filling the cracks without hacking up your bathroom floor.

**Q: How long does waterproofing last?**
A: A professional multi-layer membrane with a UV-resistant topcoat typically lasts 5–10 years in Malaysian weather. We give up to a 5-year written guarantee on complete re-waterproofing works.

**Q: Do you test after waterproofing?**
A: Yes — for bathrooms, balconies and flat roofs we block the drains, flood the floor with about 2 inches of water for 24–48 hours, and check the ceiling below for zero moisture transfer before tiling.

**Q: Is my damp lower wall a waterproofing issue?**
A: Usually yes — that's rising damp or seepage. Our team scrapes the peeling paint, treats the wall with salt-neutralising chemicals and applies a waterproofing barrier before repainting.

**Q: How many injection points will I need?**
A: That's determined on site — it depends on the leak severity and access. It starts from RM 300 per point, and the final quotation comes after inspection.

### Electrical
**Q: Are your electricians licensed?**
A: Yes — our wiremen are Suruhanjaya Tenaga (ST) registered and competent up to 415 V for residential and light commercial. Anything above 100 A or three-phase is signed off with an ST-registered Competent Electrical Engineer.

**Q: Does my old house need rewiring?**
A: Common signs are rubber-insulated cables over 25 years old, frequent main breaker tripping, scorch marks on sockets, a fuse-based DB box without RCCB, or two-pin outlets with no earth. We do an on-site inspection with a written photographic report for RM 150.

**Q: Can you install an EV charger?**
A: Yes — Level 2 AC chargers (7 kW–22 kW) for all major brands. Standard installation includes a dedicated 32 A circuit, weatherproof isolator, RCCB protection where required and a 3-metre cable run. Longer runs are quoted on site. → *No total price; site visit.*

**Q: How long is the power off during a DB box upgrade?**
A: Typically 4–8 hours depending on scope. We coordinate with TNB for temporary disconnection if needed and restore power the same day.

### Water Heater
**Q: Why does my water heater trip the RCCB?**
A: Most often moisture getting into the heating element or wiring, or a scaled-up element leaking current to earth. Our team tests it properly and advises repair or replacement based on the heater's age and condition.

**Q: How often should I descale?**
A: Every 12–18 months in hard-water areas like parts of Klang and Shah Alam. Descaling restores efficiency and extends lifespan — we also check the anode rod.

**Q: Can I install one myself?**
A: We'd strongly recommend a licensed contractor — DIY can void the manufacturer's warranty, breach condo management rules and create a safety hazard. We handle management submissions and provide a Certificate of Completion.

### Ceiling Fan
**Q: Can you install a fan on a plaster ceiling?**
A: Yes — the key is a properly anchored fan hook fixed to the metal frame or a reinforced timber block, not just the ceiling board. For existing ceilings we cut a small inspection opening or fit a heavy-duty retrofit bracket.

**Q: Why is my fan wobbling?**
A: Usually loose blade screws, unbalanced blades or a weak fan hook. We torque-check the screws, balance the blades and verify the hook is anchored into solid structure.

**Q: How long does installation take?**
A: A straight replacement on an existing hook is about 30–45 minutes. A new installation with wiring, regulator and fan hook is typically 1.5–2 hours.

### Handyman
**Q: Can you mount a heavy TV on a partition wall?**
A: Yes. We use heavy-duty steel spring-toggle anchors that grip behind the gypsum board, and scan for the metal studs to bolt directly where possible — never plastic plugs.

**Q: Do you charge hourly or per task?**
A: Flat rate per task, so you know exactly what you'll pay before we arrive. For a list of jobs we can bundle them into one consolidated package.

**Q: Do you supply the TV bracket?**
A: We can supply a heavy-duty fixed or full-motion bracket for an additional fee, or install one you've already bought.

### Tiling
**Q: Why are my tiles hollow or loose?**
A: Usually poor adhesive coverage, substrate movement, water damage from failed waterproofing, or impact. Our team tap-tests to find the hollow tiles, then re-beds them with fresh adhesive and proper substrate prep.

**Q: Can you tile over a timber floor?**
A: Not directly — timber movement cracks the grout. We install a decoupling membrane first, which absorbs the movement. It adds to the substrate prep cost but is essential for a lasting finish.

**Q: Do you do marble and granite?**
A: Yes — marble, granite, travertine and limestone with specialist stone adhesive and grout, plus sealing or polishing to your chosen finish.

### Cleaning
**Q: What's the difference between deep cleaning and post-renovation cleaning?**
A: Deep cleaning is for lived-in homes. Post-renovation cleaning is much more aggressive — specialist solvents for paint, silicone and grout haze, scrapers, wet-vacuum extraction and floor buffers. It costs more because of the equipment and time involved.

**Q: When should I book post-renovation cleaning?**
A: 3–7 days after your contractor finishes, so paint, silicone and grout can cure. Too early risks damaging uncured finishes; too late and the dust settles deeper.

**Q: Do you bring your own equipment?**
A: Yes — all cleaning products, microfibre cloths, mops, HEPA-filtered vacuums and specialty equipment. You just need to provide access to water and electricity.

### CCTV
**Q: How many cameras do I need?**
A: Most landed houses use 6–8; a typical 2-storey terrace often needs 4–6. We do a free site survey and propose a plan based on your actual layout rather than a fixed package.

**Q: Wired or wireless?**
A: Wired PoE is more reliable, records 24/7 to a local NVR with no cloud subscription, and suits landed houses. Wireless is easier to retrofit and suits condos and renters.

**Q: Can I view the cameras from overseas?**
A: Yes — all our systems support remote viewing on iOS and Android. We set up the app and walk you through it before handover.

**Q: Is CCTV allowed in condos?**
A: Inside your own unit, yes. Common areas need management approval — we help submit the application with camera specs and a position plan.

### Auto Gate
**Q: Will my gate work in a power outage?**
A: Not unless a battery backup is fitted — we strongly recommend one. Every motor installation also includes a manual release key so you can open the gate from inside.

**Q: Can you automate my existing gate?**
A: Usually yes, if the gate isn't warped or heavily rusted and the hinges are solid. We assess on site and tell you upfront if it's suitable.

**Q: How often does an autogate need servicing?**
A: Every 6 months. Our maintenance visit is RM 180 and includes lubrication, motor and sensor checks, battery check and a written condition report.

### Renovation
**Q: How long does a full renovation take?**
A: A 1,500–2,000 sq ft terrace is typically 8–12 weeks; a 1,000 sq ft condo interior about 6–8 weeks. It depends on demolition complexity, carpentry scope and material lead times.

**Q: Do you handle permits?**
A: Yes — DBKL/MBSA submissions for landed structural work, and condo management forms, lift bookings, insurance certificates and work-hour compliance.

**Q: Can I stay in the house during renovation?**
A: For partial scope like a kitchen or bathroom we can isolate the area with containment so you can stay. For full-house work we strongly recommend vacating for safety, dust control and speed.

**Q: What's the payment schedule?**
A: Milestone-based: 10% booking, 20% on demolition, 30% on rough-in, 30% on carpentry and tiling, 10% on final handover. Every change is quoted and approved by you in writing first. → *Then handover — all renovation goes to the project team.*
