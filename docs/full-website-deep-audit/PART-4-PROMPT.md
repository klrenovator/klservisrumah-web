# PART 4 — SXO + LOCAL SEO + INTERNAL LINKING + CRO + TRUST

## 4.1 SXO — SEARCH EXPERIENCE OPTIMIZATION

Evaluate the complete journey: search result, landing page, first impression,
answer, trust, service understanding, price understanding, proof, CTA, contact,
booking. Determine where users may abandon.

## 4.2 MOBILE UX

Audit navigation, font size, buttons, CTA visibility, WhatsApp button, phone
CTA, forms, sticky CTA, page speed, popups, intrusive elements, spacing,
readability.

## 4.3 CONVERSION RATE OPTIMIZATION

Determine whether every commercial page answers: What service? Where? How much?
Why choose us? How does it work? How long? What is included? What is excluded?
Warranty? How to book? How to contact? Identify missing conversion elements.

## 4.4 CTA AUDIT

Evaluate Call Now, WhatsApp, Get Quote, Book Service, Request Inspection, Get
Estimate. Determine CTA placement, CTA clarity, CTA consistency, mobile
visibility, page-specific CTA. Recommend ideal CTA strategy.

## 4.5 TRUST AUDIT

Evaluate reviews, testimonials, project photos, before/after, warranty,
guarantee, service process, team, business information, policies, contact
information. Identify trust gaps.

## 4.6 LOCAL SEO

Audit service areas, Kuala Lumpur, Selangor, neighbourhoods, location pages,
local intent, NAP consistency, Google Business Profile alignment, local schema,
reviews, local proof, local entities, map relevance, local backlinks, citations.
IMPORTANT: Do not recommend creating thousands of city/location pages simply for
keywords. Determine which locations deserve dedicated pages based on real service
coverage, search demand, business relevance, unique local value, actual customer
intent.

## 4.7 INTERNAL LINKING

Perform deep internal-link analysis. Identify orphan pages, underlinked pages,
overlinked pages, irrelevant links, generic anchor text, keyword-stuffed anchor
text, weak pillar pages, weak cluster relationships, poor hierarchy. Create an
ideal internal-linking model — but do not force a structure if another
architecture is more appropriate.

## 4.8 ANCHOR TEXT

Analyze exact match, partial match, branded, generic, URL, natural language.
Identify over-optimization.

## 4.9 CRO PAGE-BY-PAGE TEMPLATE

For every major page template recommend hero, value proposition, service
explanation, benefits, process, pricing/estimate, proof, reviews, FAQs, service
areas, CTA, contact. Determine which sections should be present.

## 4.10 COMPETITOR GAP

Identify major competitors in Kuala Lumpur/Selangor. Compare content, services,
location coverage, backlinks, topical authority, reviews, trust, UX, schema,
FAQs, AI visibility, content depth, internal linking. Do NOT invent competitor
metrics. Only report verified data.
