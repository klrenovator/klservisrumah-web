# PART 3 — AEO + GEO + LLMO + AIO / AI SEARCH AUDIT

This section is extremely important. The goal is not merely ranking in
traditional blue links. The goal is making the business/page easy for AI systems
to understand, retrieve, summarize and cite.

## 3.1 AEO — ANSWER ENGINE OPTIMIZATION

Determine whether pages directly answer questions users ask. Audit FAQs,
question-based headings, concise answers, definitions, step-by-step
explanations, comparison answers, pricing answers, "how much" questions,
"how long" questions, "which is better" questions, "what causes" questions,
"when should I" questions. Find missing question opportunities. Create a list of
high-value question clusters.

## 3.2 FEATURED SNIPPET / DIRECT ANSWER OPTIMIZATION

Identify opportunities for paragraph snippets, lists, tables, definitions,
steps, comparison blocks. Explain how content should be reformatted.

## 3.3 GEO — GENERATIVE ENGINE OPTIMIZATION

Audit whether AI systems can understand who the company is, what it does, where
it operates, what services it provides, who it serves, what makes it different,
how to contact it, what evidence supports its claims. Evaluate content for AI
retrieval and summarization.

## 3.4 LLMO — LARGE LANGUAGE MODEL OPTIMIZATION

Evaluate whether pages are semantically clear, factually explicit, entity-rich,
internally consistent, citation-worthy, easy to summarize, well structured,
authoritative, context-rich. Look for vague marketing language. Replace vague
claims with verifiable information where possible. Example: BAD = "We provide
the best renovation services." BETTER = "KL Servis Rumah provides residential
renovation and home-maintenance services in Kuala Lumpur and Selangor." Only
recommend factual statements that can be verified.

## 3.5 AI CITATION POTENTIAL

Evaluate — Could ChatGPT cite this page? Could Google AI Overview use this page?
Could Perplexity cite this page? Could Gemini use this information? Could Bing
Copilot use it? For major service pages, give an **AI Citation Potential Score:
/100** and explain exactly why.

## 3.6 AI-READABLE CONTENT

Audit whether critical information is hidden inside JavaScript, images, sliders,
tabs, accordions, interactive widgets, buttons, forms. Determine whether
important facts are available as crawlable text.

## 3.7 AI SEARCH ENTITY CONSISTENCY

Check whether brand name, services, service locations, phone, email, website,
business description, company information are consistent. Inconsistencies can
reduce entity confidence.

## 3.8 AI FAQ STRATEGY

Do not create generic FAQ spam. Create FAQs based on genuine customer intent.
For each major service identify beginner questions, pricing questions, problem
questions, comparison questions, process questions, time questions, material
questions, warranty questions, location questions, emergency questions.
