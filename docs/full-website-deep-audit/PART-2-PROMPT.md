# PART 2 — ON-PAGE SEO + CONTENT + SEMANTIC SEO + ENTITY SEO

Perform a deep content and relevance audit.

## 2.1 PAGE TITLE AUDIT

Check duplicate titles, missing titles, overly long titles, overly short
titles, keyword stuffing, location stuffing, templated titles, weak commercial
intent, brand placement, service intent, search intent alignment. Create ideal
title formulas for different page types.

## 2.2 META DESCRIPTION

Audit duplicate descriptions, missing descriptions, generic descriptions,
keyword stuffing, poor CTR potential, weak CTA, mismatch with search intent.
Provide recommended formulas.

## 2.3 H1 / H2 / H3

Audit missing H1, multiple H1s, duplicate H1, weak H1, keyword stuffing, poor
hierarchy, headings used only for styling. Create recommended heading structures
for service pages, location pages, service + location pages, problem pages,
blog/informational pages, commercial landing pages.

## 2.4 SEARCH INTENT

For each major page type determine whether intent is Informational, Commercial
investigation, Transactional, Local, or Navigational. Identify pages where
content does not satisfy the intent.

## 2.5 CONTENT QUALITY

Evaluate originality, usefulness, depth, accuracy, specificity, first-hand
experience, practical advice, local relevance, pricing transparency, process
explanation, trust, evidence, FAQs, images, examples, before/after evidence,
warranties, service limitations, customer expectations. Determine whether pages
provide actual value or simply variations of templated content.

## 2.6 PROGRAMMATIC SEO AUDIT

This is CRITICAL because the site has 4,000+ pages. Detect patterns such as
"Service + Location", "Service + Area", "Problem + Location", "Keyword +
Location". Determine whether these pages are genuinely useful or simply
generated variations. For every programmatic page template evaluate unique
introduction, unique service information, unique local context, unique FAQs,
unique examples, unique pricing/context, unique internal links, unique entities,
unique images, unique customer intent, local proof, service availability. Give
the template a quality score from 0–100.

## 2.7 SEMANTIC SEO

Build a semantic map for the business. Identify entities related to air
conditioning, renovation, painting, plumbing, waterproofing, ceiling,
partition, electrical, handyman, tiling, carpentry, kitchen, bathroom, roofing,
drainage, maintenance, repair, installation. Identify supporting concepts,
attributes, processes, tools, materials, problems, solutions and customer
questions. Determine missing entities, weak entity relationships, missing
supporting topics, semantic gaps, topical gaps, entity ambiguity.

## 2.8 TOPICAL AUTHORITY

Determine whether KL Servis Rumah has enough content depth to establish
authority around its major services. Build a topical map: Pillar, Cluster,
Subcluster, Supporting content, FAQ, Problem, Solution, Location. Identify
missing clusters.

## 2.9 ENTITY SEO

Audit whether the website clearly communicates WHO, WHAT, WHERE, SERVICES,
EXPERIENCE, EXPERTISE, BRAND, CONTACT, SERVICE AREAS. Check entity consistency
across Website, Google Business Profile, Social profiles, Directories, About
page, Contact page, Footer, Schema, Organization data. Identify entity
inconsistencies.

## 2.10 E-E-A-T

Evaluate Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness. Look for
company identity, team information, experience, project examples, testimonials,
reviews, warranties, guarantees, contact details, physical/service presence,
credentials, policies, author information, editorial standards, real
photographs, case studies. Identify trust gaps.
