# Cracked or Displaced Roof Tiles After a Storm

**Question:** I can see cracked, slipped or missing roof tiles from the ground, often after a heavy storm. Do I replace a few tiles, or the whole roof?

**Answer:** Most homes need only spot replacement of colour-matched tiles plus ridge re-bedding. Replace the whole roof only if about 30% or more of the tiles are damaged, or the underlay is compromised. Typical causes are storm impact, fallen branches, foot traffic from a previous contractor, age-related brittleness after 20+ years, or failed mortar on ridge tiles.

This is **tile damage you can see**. Seasonal leak behaviour during a storm is [roof leak in the rainy season](/problems/roof-leak-rainy-season). Indoor stains after rain are [ceiling leak after rain](/problems/ceiling-leak-after-rain).

## Why Tiles Crack or Slip

- Storm impact or fallen branches.
- Foot traffic damage from a previous contractor.
- Brittleness after 20+ years.
- Failed mortar bedding on ridge tiles.

## Spot Repair or Full Roof?

| Finding | Typical path |
|---|---|
| A few cracked or slipped tiles | Colour-matched replacement |
| Loose ridge caps | Re-bed with polymer mortar |
| Hairline cracks only | UV-stable sealant where appropriate |
| 30%+ damaged, or underlay failed | Discuss full replacement |

A standard roof inspection (drone + ground survey + written report) is published at **RM 250 to RM 450** depending on access and size.

## What the Work Involves

1. Locate cracked and displaced tiles (drone survey where useful).
2. Replace individual tiles, colour-matched from common Malaysian brands such as Monier and Malaysian Mosaics. Very old tiles may be a close match rather than exact.
3. Re-bed loose ridge caps with fresh polymer mortar.
4. Seal hairline cracks with UV-stable sealant where that is the right detail.
