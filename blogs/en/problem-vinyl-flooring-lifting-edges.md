# Vinyl or SPC Flooring Lifting at the Edges

**Question:** My vinyl or SPC planks are curling, lifting or gapping at the edges — usually near doors, windows or wet areas. Do I need a whole new floor?

**Answer:** Usually not. Most cases are edge-lift only and are fixable in two to four hours if the planks are not heat-warped or waterlogged. Typical causes are an expansion gap under 10 mm at the walls, direct sun overheating the material, water under the floor, or a click-lock that never fully engaged. The repair is to lift the affected planks, trim the gap, dry the substrate and re-click with full engagement.

This is **edges lifting or gapping**. A wear layer that is peeling or delaminating is [SPC flooring peeling at the edges](/problems/spc-flooring-peeling-edges). Compare materials in [SPC vs vinyl vs laminate](/blog/spc-vs-vinyl-vs-laminate-flooring-malaysia).

## Why Edges Lift in Malaysian Rooms

- Insufficient expansion gap (needs about 10 mm at walls).
- Direct west sun overheating the planks.
- Water seepage under the floor.
- Original click-lock not fully engaged.

## Reuse or Replace?

| Condition | Typical decision |
|---|---|
| Edges up, planks otherwise sound | Lift, dry, trim gap, re-click |
| Heat-warped or waterlogged | Replace those planks |
| Movement when you walk | Relock the run; check underlay |
| Water visible under edges | Dry the substrate before any reinstall |

## What the Repair Involves

1. Lift affected planks.
2. Trim the expansion gap at walls.
3. Dry the substrate fully.
4. Re-click with full engagement.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can lifting vinyl be reused?
Usually yes if it is not heat-damaged. Lift, dry, trim the gap and re-click the same planks.

### Do I need to replace the whole floor?
Only if planks are heat-warped or waterlogged. Most cases are edge-lift only.

### How do I prevent it happening again?
Keep a 10 mm expansion gap at walls, shade west-facing sun, and use rugs at wet entryways.
