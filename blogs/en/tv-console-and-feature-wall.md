# TV Console & Feature Wall Carpentry in KL & Selangor

A TV wall in a Malaysian living room has to do three jobs: hold the screen safely, hide the decoder, fibre and plug cluster, and still look calm when the lights are on. A shop console rarely hits the wall width or hides the cables. A built-in console and feature wall can.

**Direct answer:** KL Servis Rumah builds custom TV consoles and matching feature walls from **RM 1,500**, with storage drawers, floating shelves, a cable void, and panelling. The TV itself can be mounted as part of the same visit or through our [heavy TV wall mounting](/services/handyman/heavy-tv-wall-mounting) service.

## What this service includes

A typical TV-wall quotation covers:

- A low console (drawers and/or open cubbies) built to the wall width.
- A cable-management void at the back so HDMI and power are not draped on the skirting.
- Floating or fixed shelves for devices and décor.
- Feature-wall panelling (slat, shaker, or flat laminate) above or around the TV.
- Site install, levelling and handover.

It does not automatically include the TV, the wall bracket, extra power points, or a full room [carpentry](/services/carpentry) package. Those are itemised.

## Cable management that actually works

The usual KL living-room cluster is: Astro / Unifi, a soundbar, a game console, and a 13A socket that was never centred on the sofa wall.

A clean install needs:

- A **rear void** at least deep enough for plugs, not just the cable diameter.
- A vertical path from console to TV height — trunking inside the panel, or a new [electrical](/services/electrical) point at TV height.
- Ventilation for a closed decoder cupboard. A fully sealed box cooks the device.
- Access: a removable back or an open bay so you can change HDMI later.

We do not hide a live extension block inside a sealed cavity.

## Feature-wall materials

- **Laminate plywood panels** — durable, wipeable, good behind a sofa wall that gets hands.
- **Painted MDF slats or shaker frames** — the common “feature” look; keep them off wet or sun-blasted walls.
- **Mixed open shelves** — useful, but dust shows; plan fewer, deeper shelves rather than a grid of tiny ones.

Humidity and afternoon sun on a west-facing condo glass wall will move thin MDF. We say so on site if the wall is a problem.

## How the job is done

1. **Measure** — wall width, TV size, socket positions, window glare, sofa distance.
2. **Design** — console height (usually so the TV centre sits at seated eye level), panel layout, open vs closed storage.
3. **Workshop** — typically 2–3 weeks after design lock.
4. **Install** — 1 day for a simple console; longer if the full wall is panelled.
5. **Mount and test** — TV bracket on a solid fixing (stud, brick or a hidden ply pad), cables dressed, devices powered.

If the wall is gypsum, the TV is never hung on hollow board alone. We locate studs or add a hidden timber/ply pad. That is the same safety rule as our handyman TV-mounting work.

## What we check before building

- Wall type: brick, concrete or gypsum partition.
- Whether water pipes run in the wall (common on condo party walls).
- Glare from the balcony door at 4–6 pm.
- Sofa distance — a 75-inch screen on a 2.4 m wall can be too close.
- If you also want a [built-in wardrobe](/services/carpentry/built-in-wardrobe) in the same unit, we can sequence both.

A TV that has already fallen is a fixing problem, not a carpentry style problem. See [TV fell off the wall](/problems/tv-fell-off-wall).

## Common mistakes

- Building the console first, then realising the TV is off-centre to the sofa.
- No rear void — cables exit under the skirting in a bundle.
- Closed decoder cupboard with no vents.
- Hanging a heavy OLED on feature slats that are only glued.
- Choosing high-gloss panels opposite a west window.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you hide all the wires?

We can hide the run from console to TV inside the carcass or panel. The building’s fibre point still has to reach the console — sometimes that needs a short surface trunk or a new point.

### Do you supply the TV bracket?

We can, or we install yours. The bracket must be rated for the TV weight and the wall type.

### How long does install take?

A simple console is often one day after workshop lead time. A full-height feature wall may take longer on site.

### Can this be done in a rented condo?

Only if the landlord allows drilling and you accept that panels are fixed to the wall. Many tenants should use a freestanding console plus a proper mount instead.
