This guide explains the benefits of SPC flooring, how it is installed, and what it costs.

## What Is SPC Click-Lock Flooring?

SPC flooring is a rigid core flooring made from stone and plastic composite. The planks lock together with a click mechanism — no glue needed — and are highly water-resistant, making them ideal for Malaysian homes.

## Why Choose SPC Flooring?

- **Waterproof** — resists moisture, making it suitable for kitchens and wet areas.
- **Durable** — rigid core resists dents and wear.
- **Easy to maintain** — simple to clean and care for.
- **Realistic look** — available in many timber and stone finishes.
- **Fast installation** — the click-lock system installs cleanly and quickly.

## The Installation Process

1. **Measurement** — we measure the area and calculate the flooring required.
2. **Subfloor preparation** — the floor is cleaned, levelled, and dried; a moisture barrier or underlay is used as needed.
3. **Acclimatisation** — the flooring is allowed to adjust to the room temperature and humidity.
4. **Installation** — planks are laid and clicked together with proper expansion gaps.
5. **Finishing** — skirting and transition strips are fitted for a neat finish.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is SPC flooring suitable for kitchens?

Yes. SPC flooring is highly water-resistant, making it an excellent choice for kitchens, dining areas, and even balconies.

### Can SPC flooring be installed over tiles?

In many cases, yes, if the existing tiles are level and sound. An underlay or moisture barrier is used as appropriate. We will assess your floor and advise.

### Does SPC flooring require glue?

No. SPC click-lock planks lock together without glue, which makes installation clean and quick.

### How long does installation take?

A standard 3-bedroom apartment can usually be floored within a few days, depending on area and subfloor preparation.
