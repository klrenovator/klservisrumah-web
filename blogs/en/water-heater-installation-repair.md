A water heater is one of the few appliances in a home that combines mains electricity with running water, which is why installation must include proper earthing, an RCCB-protected circuit, and waterproof isolation. This guide explains the types of heaters available, what installation involves, and when repair makes more sense than replacement.

## Types of Water Heaters for Malaysian Homes

### Instant (single-point) water heaters

The most common type in Malaysia, mounted directly above the shower or basin tap. Water is heated as it flows through the unit.

- Heats water instantly — no waiting for a tank.
- Compact, wall-mounted, ideal for condominiums and single bathrooms.
- Good for one shower point; running multiple outlets at once reduces temperature.
- Power-hungry (3.5kW–4.5kW typical), so it needs a dedicated circuit.

### Storage (tank) water heaters

A 15L–50L insulated tank heats and stores water, feeding multiple points in a home.

- Consistent hot water for two or more bathrooms, or a kitchen.
- Takes time to reheat after the tank is used.
- Requires secure wall mounting — a full 50L tank is heavy when full.
- Runs on a lower load than instant units, so it suits homes with older wiring — but still needs proper circuits.

### Heat pump water heaters

An energy-efficient system for landed homes: the heat pump draws heat from outdoor air to warm the water, using roughly a third of the electricity of a resistance heater.

- Higher upfront cost, much lower running cost.
- Requires outdoor or well-ventilated space for the condenser unit.
- Best for families that use a lot of hot water daily.

## Installation: What a Professional Job Includes

Installing a water heater is electrical and plumbing work at the same time. A proper installation includes:

1. **Site check** — confirming wall type (the unit must be mounted securely), the nearest power point, and water supply pressure.
2. **Dedicated circuit** — for most heaters, a dedicated 20A–32A point with isolator is required so the heater never shares a circuit with other appliances.
3. **RCCB protection** — the circuit must be protected by an earth-leakage device; this is non-negotiable for an appliance that uses water.
4. **Water-tight installation** — anti-splash mounting and SIRIM-compliant pipework, so water never reaches live electrical parts.
5. **Testing** — earth continuity, polarity, and RCCB function are verified before the unit is handed over.

All wiring is done by ST-registered wiremen, and installation carries a 12-month workmanship warranty.

## Water Heater Repair & Maintenance

Common problems we fix:

- **No hot water or slow heating** — often a faulty heating element, thermostat, or thermal cut-out. See our guide on [water heaters that won't heat](/problems/water-heater-not-heating).
- **RCCB trips when the heater switches on** — usually a sign of a leaking element or damaged wiring; this must be fixed immediately.
- **Leaking from the unit** — tank corrosion in storage heaters, or a cracked heating chamber in instant units.
- **Scalding or inconsistent temperature** — a failing thermostat or a mineral deposit issue.
- **Noisy heating** — rumbling or hissing usually means scale buildup in the tank.

**Descaling** — Malaysian water is hard in many areas (Klang, Shah Alam, and parts of KL), and scale builds up inside heaters and shower heads. Descaling every 1–2 years extends the life of the element and restores heating speed. Storage heaters should also have their anode rod checked, as it protects the tank from corrosion.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which water heater brand is best in Malaysia?

The best brand depends on your needs. Joven and Alpha are popular, affordable choices widely available in Malaysia. Rheem and Rinnai are strong mid-range options with reliable elements, and Mitsubishi Electric offers efficient heat-pump systems for landed homes. We install and service all of these.

### Is it safe to install a water heater myself?

No. Water heater installation combines electrical work with plumbing. Without proper earthing, RCCB protection, and water-tight mounting, a DIY installation can be lethal. Malaysian regulations require the electrical work to be done by a registered wireman.

### How long does a water heater last?

A typical instant water heater lasts 5–8 years; a storage tank heater with a maintained anode rod can last 8–12 years. Descaling and periodic checks extend both.

### Can I use hot water in the kitchen from the same heater?

Instant heaters are single-point — they serve one shower or basin. For multiple points (kitchen + bathrooms), a storage tank or heat-pump system is the right choice.
