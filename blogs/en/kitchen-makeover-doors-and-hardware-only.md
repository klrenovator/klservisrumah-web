# Kitchen Makeover: Doors and Hardware Only in Kuala Lumpur and Selangor

**Direct answer:** Replacing doors, drawer fronts, handles, hinges and runners is sensible only when the existing cabinet carcasses are dry, square and firmly fixed. Swollen boards, active sink leaks, termite damage or badly distorted boxes require repair or replacement before refacing.

This guide explains the [Kitchen Makeover: Doors and Hardware Only](/services/kitchen-cabinet/kitchen-makeover-doors-and-hardware-only) scope under [kitchen cabinet services](/services/kitchen-cabinet). The published starting price is **RM 4,200**. A site-specific quotation should confirm measurements, preparation, materials, access and related repairs rather than treating the starting rate as a fixed total.

## When This Service Is the Right Choice

The service is appropriate when inspection confirms that the existing property and surrounding construction can support the proposed work. Visible symptoms alone do not prove the cause. In Kuala Lumpur and Selangor, occupied areas, parking or loading access, condominium management rules and permitted working hours may affect planning and scheduling.

## What Should Be Checked Before Work

- **Swelling, delamination and musty odour below the sink.**
- **Cabinet boxes are square and securely fixed.**
- **Hinge cup positions and compatibility with new doors.**
- **Drawer box condition, runner length and clearances.**

## Compare the Available Approaches

| Approach | When it makes sense |
|---|---|
| **Reface doors/hardware** | carcasses are sound and layout already works. |
| **Repair then reface** | damage is isolated and can be structurally restored. |
| **Replace cabinets** | swelling, pests or distortion is widespread. |

Choosing the least expensive option without checking its limits can cause repeat work. The quotation should explain why the selected approach suits the substrate, dimensions and intended use.

## Typical Work Process

1. **Inventory every door, drawer and hardware type**.
2. **Confirm carcass condition and stop moisture sources**.
3. **Approve door sample, colour, edge and handle position**.
4. **Fabricate and label fronts before removing the old set**.
5. **Install, align gaps and test hinges and runners under load**.

Preparation and sequencing matter. Existing defects, unstable supports, incompatible materials or damaged substrates should be corrected before they are concealed. Protect occupied rooms, common corridors and completed finishes throughout the work. At handover, test operation and inspect edges, fixings, joints and access points—not appearance alone.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Refacing over water-damaged particleboard.
- Reusing worn hinges with heavier new doors.
- Choosing colour from a phone screen instead of a physical sample.
- Changing handles without checking old hole spacing.

A competent inspection should distinguish a serviceable component from a system that has reached the point of replacement. Ask for assumptions and exclusions to be written down, especially where concealed conditions cannot be confirmed until work opens the area.

## Safety, Access and Maintenance

Do not bypass manufacturer instructions, building management requirements or safe access arrangements to save time. Keep product references and care instructions after handover. Routine cleaning and early attention to movement, moisture, corrosion or loose hardware usually make local repairs simpler.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can the countertop stay?
Usually yes when it and the base cabinets are sound; the survey must confirm that door and drawer work will not disturb its support.

### Will new doors hide swollen cabinets?
They may hide the appearance briefly but will not restore strength or stop moisture. The cause and damaged board need attention.

### Can soft-close be added?
Often yes if hinge geometry and drawer clearances are compatible; it should be specified after measuring the existing boxes.
