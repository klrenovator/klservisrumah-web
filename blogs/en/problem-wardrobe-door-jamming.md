# Wardrobe Door Jamming or Not Closing? Causes and Repair

**Question:** Why is my wardrobe door sticking, scraping, or refusing to close flush?

**Answer:** Wardrobe doors jam when the hinge bends or loosens, a sliding door drops on its track, the roller wears out, the wardrobe is over-packed, or moisture warps the panel. Early hinge or roller adjustment is cheap; a bent track, cracked door or swollen board usually needs part replacement.

This article covers **wardrobe doors**. For kitchen cabinet doors, see [kitchen cabinet doors sagging or misaligned](/problems/cabinet-door-sagging). For entry doors that scrape the floor, see [door scraping the floor](/problems/door-scraping-floor).

## Hinged vs Sliding Wardrobe Doors

| Door type | Common jam cause | Usual fix |
|---|---|---|
| Hinged swing door | Loose/bent hinge or over-packed shelf | Adjust hinge, clear contents |
| Sliding door | Dirty/bent track or worn roller | Clean track, replace roller |
| Full-height mirror door | Heavy door pulling hinge | Reinforce or upgrade hinges |
| Damp wardrobe | Swollen board or warped door | Fix damp source, repair panel |

## Why Hinged Wardrobe Doors Jam

### Loose or bent hinges
A wardrobe door that leans can rub against the carcass or another door. Soft-close hinges have adjustment screws, but if the hinge arm is bent it should be replaced.

### Clothes or storage pushing against the door
Over-stuffed shelves and hanging clothes can prevent the door from closing. This is not a hardware fault, but repeated forcing can bend hinges.

### Settlement or an uneven floor
Wardrobes can twist slightly on an uneven floor. Packers under the base may square the carcass and help the doors align.

## Why Sliding Wardrobe Doors Jam

Sliding doors rely on the track and rollers. Dust, carpet fluff and grit collect in the bottom track; rollers then skip or seize. A door may also drop if its top roller screw loosens.

Professional repair usually includes:

1. Vacuuming and cleaning the track.
2. Checking for bent track sections.
3. Adjusting roller height.
4. Replacing broken nylon or metal rollers.
5. Testing the door so it slides without force.

## Moisture and Warping

Wardrobes on external walls or in rooms with poor ventilation can absorb moisture. Signs include:

- A door that rubs in the centre during humid weather.
- Swollen laminate edges.
- Mould on the back panel or wall.
- A wardrobe that is no longer square.

If the wall behind the wardrobe is damp, solve the moisture source first — otherwise new doors will warp again. Relevant issues include [damp walls and paint bubbling](/problems/damp-walls-paint-bubbling) and [condensation and ceiling mould](/problems/condensation-ceiling-mold).

## When to Repair vs Replace

Repair makes sense when the track is straight, the board is solid, and only hinges or rollers have failed. Replacement is more sensible when:

- The wardrobe is badly water-damaged.
- Multiple panels are swollen or cracked.
- The track is bent and doors cannot run true.
- You need more hanging space, drawers or a different layout.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why does my sliding wardrobe door keep coming off the track?
Usually because the track is dirty, bent or the roller height has dropped. Cleaning the track and adjusting or replacing the roller normally fixes it.

### Can a warped wardrobe door be fixed?
Slight movement may be corrected by adjustment and ventilation. A badly swollen or delaminated door usually needs replacement after the moisture source is fixed.

### How much does wardrobe door repair cost?
The published problem range is **RM 180 to RM 650+**, depending on whether it needs adjustment, rollers, hinges or panel work.
