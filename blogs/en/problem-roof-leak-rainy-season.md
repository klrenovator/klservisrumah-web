# Roof Leaks in the Monsoon: What to Do During the Storm and After

**Question:** My roof leaks during monsoon storms but seems fine the rest of the year. What should I do while it is raining, and when can it actually be repaired?

**Answer:** During a storm, the priority is containment and electrical safety, not repair. Move valuables, catch water in containers, turn off lighting circuits in the affected area at the distribution board if water is near any fitting, and photograph where the water enters. Permanent repair — replacing tiles, re-bedding ridges, resealing flashing, or recoating a flat roof — requires a dry, safe surface. Working on a wet tiled roof during rain is dangerous and any sealant applied to a wet substrate will fail. The right sequence is: contain now, inspect once dry, repair before the next rain band.

This article covers the seasonal side of roof leaks: what to do in the moment, how monsoon conditions differ from ordinary rain, and how to prepare a roof before the wet season. If you want the diagnostic breakdown of where rainwater enters a ceiling, read [ceiling leak after rain](/problems/ceiling-leak-after-rain), which covers the entry points in detail.

## Why Monsoon Rain Finds Leaks That Ordinary Rain Does Not

Peninsular Malaysia experiences two monsoon seasons — the Southwest Monsoon roughly from May to September, and the Northeast Monsoon roughly from November to March, with inter-monsoon periods bringing intense convective thunderstorms. The Malaysian Meteorological Department publishes seasonal forecasts and heavy rain warnings that are worth watching if your roof has a known weak point.

Three things change during monsoon conditions:

- **Wind direction and force.** Wind-driven rain travels horizontally and upward under tile laps. A roof that sheds vertical rain perfectly can leak badly when rain arrives sideways at 60 km/h.
- **Rainfall intensity.** Gutters and valleys are sized for a rate of flow. In a tropical downpour, a partially blocked gutter overflows backwards into the eaves rather than draining.
- **Duration.** Sustained rain saturates. Small capillary paths that shed a five-minute shower will pass water during a two-hour storm.

This is why a leak can appear for the first time in years during a single severe storm.

## During the Storm: A Safe Sequence

1. **Deal with electrical risk first.** If water is near a downlight, ceiling fan, or any wiring, switch off that lighting circuit at the DB. Do not touch a wet fitting that is still live.
2. **Contain the water.** Buckets under the drip, towels around the base, and plastic sheeting over furniture. A bin with a cloth in it reduces splashing noise and spread.
3. **Relieve a bulging ceiling.** If a plasterboard ceiling is visibly bulging with trapped water, water is pooling above it and the board can fail suddenly. Clear the area beneath it. Piercing a small drain hole releases the load in a controlled way, but only do this if you can do it safely from a stable position — otherwise stay clear and wait.
4. **Photograph and note timing.** Where the water enters, how heavy, and which wind direction. This information significantly shortens the later inspection.
5. **Do not go onto the roof.** Wet concrete and clay tiles are extremely slippery, and tiles crack under foot load. Most serious roof injuries in Malaysia happen to people trying to fix a leak in the rain.

## After the Rain: Inspection and Repair

Once the roof is dry, a proper inspection covers:

| Area | What is checked |
|---|---|
| Roof tiles | Cracked, slipped, chipped or wind-lifted tiles; broken laps |
| Ridge and hip lines | Cement pointing condition, loose ridge caps, flexible ridge tape |
| Valleys and gutters | Debris, moss, corrosion, standing water, fall direction |
| Flashing | Wall abutments, chimney and skylight junctions, dried sealant |
| Underlay / sisalation | Tears, sagging, previous patch repairs |
| Roof structure | Damp or soft timber battens and trusses, rust on metal members |
| Flat roof / balcony slab | Membrane condition, ponding, cracking at upstands |
| Ceiling void | Water tracking, stained battens, insulation condition |

Repairs are then matched to what is found. ## Pre-Monsoon Preparation: The Highest-Value Maintenance You Can Do

Most monsoon leaks are the predictable result of deferred maintenance. Before the wet season:

- **Clear gutters and downpipes.** The single most common preventable cause. See [clogged gutter causing roof leak](/problems/clogged-gutter-leaking).
- **Check ridge pointing.** Cement pointing shrinks and cracks under years of UV. Cracked pointing is a direct wind-driven rain path.
- **Look for slipped or cracked tiles** from ground level with binoculars, or from an accessible window.
- **Check flat roof and balcony membranes** for ponding, blistering and cracked upstands — see [balcony or pool deck leak](/problems/swimming-pool-leak-balcony).
- **Trim overhanging branches** that drop leaves into gutters and abrade tiles in wind.

## Temporary Measures That Are Acceptable — and Ones That Are Not

**Reasonable as a stopgap:** a tarpaulin properly weighted and secured over a damaged roof section by someone competent working in dry conditions; buckets and containment inside; switching off affected circuits.

**Not acceptable as a fix:** sealing the leak from inside the ceiling. This traps water in the roof void where it rots timber battens, rusts metal, and eventually breaks out somewhere else. Water must always be stopped at the outer surface.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a roof leak be repaired while it is still raining?
Emergency containment such as a secured tarpaulin can sometimes be done, but permanent repair cannot. Sealants and mortar will not bond to a wet surface, and a wet tiled roof is genuinely dangerous to walk on.

### Why does my roof only leak in heavy rain?
Because wind-driven rain and high rainfall intensity overcome defences that handle ordinary rain — water is pushed up under tile laps, and gutters overflow rather than drain.

### Is a leak from the roof or from a pipe?
Rain-only leaks point to the roof, gutters, balcony or exterior walls. A leak that also occurs in dry weather points to plumbing — use the [leak triage tool](/tools/leak-triage) or read [hidden water leak detection](/blog/hidden-water-leak-detection-kl).

### How soon after a storm can the roof be inspected?
As soon as the roof surface is dry and safe. Booking during a dry window between rain bands is normal practice during the monsoon.

### Should I claim on insurance?
That depends on your policy and whether the damage is storm damage or the result of wear. Dated photos of the water entry and a written inspection report support any claim you decide to make.
