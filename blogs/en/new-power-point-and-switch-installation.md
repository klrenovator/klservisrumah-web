# New Power Point & Switch Installation in KL & Selangor

Adding a new power point or switch in a Kuala Lumpur or Selangor home typically starts from RM 150 per point, including cable, surface or recessed mounting, and labour. The work must be done by a wireman registered with Suruhanjaya Tenaga, because extending an electrical circuit is regulated fixed wiring work.

Most Malaysian homes eventually run short of sockets — especially kitchens, home offices, and bedrooms in terrace houses and condominiums across the Klang Valley. This guide covers what you can install, what the job involves, and how to get it done safely.

## What Can Be Installed

A licensed electrician can add:

- **13A sockets** — the standard power point for general appliances.
- **USB outlets** — sockets with built-in USB-A or USB-C charging ports, popular for bedside and desk locations.
- **Double-pole switches** — switches that disconnect both live and neutral, required for high-load appliances like water heaters and air conditioners.
- **Dimmer controls** — for dimmable LED lighting circuits.
- **Weatherproof sockets** — for balconies, yards, and outdoor areas exposed to rain.

## Why You Shouldn't DIY a New Power Point

In Malaysia, adding or altering fixed wiring must be carried out by a registered wireman under the Electricity Regulations. DIY socket additions create several risks:

- **Overloaded circuits** — a new socket on an already loaded circuit trips breakers and heats cables.
- **No earth connection** — older homes may lack proper grounding, which turns a socket into a shock hazard.
- **Fire risk** — loose connections behind the wall can start fires without visible warning.
- **Insurance problems** — unapproved electrical alterations can void coverage after a fire or accident.

If a circuit trips frequently when you plug in appliances, read our guide on [RCCB tripping in KL homes](/problems/rccb-tripping-kl) before adding more points — the fix may start at the DB box, not the socket.

## What the Installation Involves

A typical new power point job follows this process:

1. **Site check** — the electrician identifies the nearest circuit, checks cable condition, and confirms wall type (concrete, brick, or gypsum partition).
2. **Quote** — you receive an itemised price for the point, cable, trunking, and labour.
3. **Safe isolation** — the circuit is isolated and verified at zero voltage.
4. **Installation** — the electrician runs the cable from the nearest point or DB box, fits the socket or switch, and mounts it flush or surface-mounted.
5. **Testing** — polarity, earth continuity, and insulation are tested before power is restored.

In condominiums, building management may require an electrical approval form and scheduled work hours — part of the service when we work in high-rise buildings in Mont Kiara, Bangsar, and elsewhere in KL.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Adding a point to a circuit already at capacity** — your electrician should check the load first.
- **Using an adapter hub instead of a new point** — adapters overload sockets and are a leading cause of electrical fires in Malaysian homes.
- **Fitting outdoor sockets without weatherproofing** — in Malaysia's rainy climate, outdoor points need IP-rated covers.
- **Forgetting about the switch rating** — dimmers and double-pole switches must be rated for the connected load.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I convert an existing single socket to a double socket?

Yes, in most cases a single 13A socket can be replaced with a double socket on the same circuit, provided the circuit can handle the load. This is a common request in kitchens and bedrooms.

### Is a USB socket worth installing?

For bedside tables, desks, and kitchen counters, USB sockets reduce the need for bulky chargers. USB-C sockets also support fast charging for modern phones and tablets.

### How long does installing a new socket take?

A straightforward new point takes about one to two hours. Multiple points or long cable runs can take longer.

### Do condominiums allow new power points?

Most condominium management offices allow new points as long as the work is done by a registered electrician and approved in advance. We handle the coordination for you.
