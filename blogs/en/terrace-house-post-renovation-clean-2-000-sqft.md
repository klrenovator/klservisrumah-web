# Terrace House Post-Renovation Clean in KL (2,000 sqft)

A full terrace house renovation leaves residue everywhere: cement dust on two floors, grout haze across new tiling, paint on skirting and grilles, silicone around every new window, and a porch or garage that has been the contractor's mixing area for weeks.

**Direct answer:** KL Servis Rumah cleans 2,000 sqft double-storey terrace houses after renovation from **RM 1,400** across Kuala Lumpur and Selangor. Book **3–7 days after the contractor finishes**, before furniture arrives. Work carries a **7-day spot-check free re-clean guarantee**.

## When to book

- **After a full-house renovation** — rewiring, replumbing, retiling, repainting, new carpentry.
- **After an extension or a kitchen rebuild** — the dust reaches the whole house regardless of where the work was.
- **Before furniture delivery and move-in.**
- **Before listing or handover photos.**
- **3–7 days after completion**, once paint, silicone and grout have cured.

## What is covered

**Interior, both floors**
- Debris, offcuts, packaging and protective film bagged and removed
- Industrial HEPA vacuuming of floors, staircase, skirting, cabinet interiors and window tracks
- Paint splatter removed from floors, skirting, doors, grilles and glass
- Silicone smears scraped and dissolved
- Grout haze on new tiles treated with pH-balanced acidic cleaner
- Adhesive and sticker residue removed from glass, aluminium and new fittings
- Kitchen and wet kitchen: cabinet interiors, worktops, backsplash, sink, tapware
- All bathrooms: new tiles, glass screens, sanitary ware, fittings, floor traps
- Switches, sockets, light fixtures, ceiling fans and air-conditioning fascias
- New aluminium windows, sliding doors, tracks, grilles and reachable frames
- Staircase, handrail and balustrade
- Floors buffed by material, then walkthrough with you

**Outdoor**
- Porch, car park and entrance swept and washed down where reachable
- Garage cleared of dust, offcuts and packaging
- Back yard and drying area cleared of debris
- Outdoor drains cleared of cement slurry and tile offcuts

**Quoted separately:** skip bin rental (typically RM 350–500 for a one-week bin in KL/Selangor), driveway pressure washing, facade and high-level exterior glass, roof and gutter work.

## Sequence for a two-storey site

1. **Site walk and scope.** We note the finishes, the residue level and any damage worth photographing before we touch it.
2. **Debris out first.** Nothing else is efficient until the offcuts and packaging are gone.
3. **Rough clean top-down.** Upstairs, then staircase, then ground floor, HEPA vacuum and damp wipe.
4. **Detail work.** Paint, silicone, grout haze and adhesive, each with the correct solvent for the surface.
5. **Wet kitchen and outdoor.** Usually the dirtiest zones, done after interior detailing so dust does not travel back in.
6. **Final polish and handover.** Floors buffed, glass and aluminium polished, walkthrough with you.

## Common mistakes

- **Treating it as a bigger deep clean.** Construction residue needs solvents and scrapers, not a longer mop session — see [terrace house deep clean](/services/deep-cleaning/terrace-house-deep-clean-2-000-sqft) for what a deep clean actually covers.
- **Leaving cement slurry in outdoor drains.** It sets hard and causes standing water, then damp walls — see [wall dampness and efflorescence repair](/services/waterproofing/wall-dampness-and-efflorescence-repair).
- **Cleaning before the contractor's snag list is closed.**
- **Letting anyone dry-scrape new aluminium or glass.** Damage is permanent; see our [glass and aluminium service](/services/glass-aluminium).
- **Ignoring dust that keeps coming back.** Usually it was never removed from high surfaces and ceiling voids — see [construction dust that keeps returning](/problems/construction-dust-returning).
- **Assuming new grout stains will lift later.** Haze and staining are easiest to remove early — see [stubborn grout stains](/problems/stubborn-grout-stains-after-deep-clean).

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does a 2,000 sqft terrace house take?

Usually a full day with a larger crew, and a second day if there is heavy cement residue, extensive floor buffing or a lot of debris.

### Do you clean the garage and porch?

Yes, clearing and washing down reachable outdoor surfaces is included. Driveway pressure washing is separate.

### Can you coordinate with my contractor?

Yes. If your finishes are under supplier warranty, we align the cleaning method with what the contractor and supplier allow.

### What about renovation debris in the garden?

Normal volumes are bagged and disposed of. Large volumes need a skip bin, quoted separately.

### Do you clean the exterior windows?

We clean windows, tracks and frames from inside and any reachable exterior surfaces at ground level. Upper-floor exterior glass needs access equipment and is quoted separately.
