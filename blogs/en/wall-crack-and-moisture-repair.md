# Wall Crack & Moisture Repair in KL and Selangor

**Question:** Can I just paint over cracked or damp-stained walls?

**Answer:** No. Paint applied over an active moisture problem or an untreated crack will bubble, peel and stain again within months. The correct sequence is: find and fix the moisture source, repair the crack or damaged plaster properly, apply a sealer or primer, and only then repaint. Skipping the repair stage is the most common reason a repainted wall fails early.

This article covers the **wall crack & moisture repair** sub-service of [house painting services](/services/painting), which prepares damaged walls for a lasting paint finish. If the moisture comes from outside or from the ground, a waterproofing solution is needed — see [wall dampness & efflorescence repair](/services/waterproofing/wall-dampness-and-efflorescence-repair). ## Know Your Crack

Not all wall cracks are equal, and the repair method depends on the type:

| Crack type | What it looks like | Usual cause | Repair approach |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hairline plaster crack | Fine, thin line on the paint/plaster surface | Plaster shrinkage or paint ageing | Scrape, fill, sand, repaint |
| Movement or settlement crack | Reopening crack at corners, joints or along blockwork | Building movement or thermal expansion | Flexible filler, joint treatment; monitor |
| Structural crack | Wide, diagonal, or visibly growing | Structural movement | Investigate the cause before any patching |
| Paint film cracking | Crazed or alligator-skin pattern in the paint layer | Old paint, wrong paint system, no primer | Sand back, re-prime, repaint |

A hairline crack that stays stable for months is usually cosmetic. A crack that widens, runs diagonally across corners, or makes door frames stick deserves investigation before cosmetic repair.

## Where Wall Moisture Really Comes From

Moisture stains, bubbling paint and white salt deposits on a wall have only a few possible sources:

- **A leaking pipe or bathroom** behind or above the wall — see [water leakage diagnosis & repair](/services/plumbing/water-leakage-diagnosis-and-repair).
- **Rising damp** from the ground on ground-floor or boundary walls.
- **Rain penetration** through external walls, parapets or around window frames.
- **Condensation** on cold walls in air-conditioned rooms.

Treating the surface without addressing the source is why stains keep returning. A professional checks with visual inspection and moisture readings before recommending the repair scope.

## What a Crack & Moisture Repair Includes

The published scope of this sub-service starts at **RM 180** and typically covers:

1. **Scraping** loose paint and debonded plaster from the affected area.
2. **Widening and filling** cracks with suitable filler; flexible systems for joints that move.
3. **Sanding or spot-skimming** the repaired area level with the surrounding wall.
4. **Moisture-blocking sealer or primer** on damp-exposed areas — this is what stops stains bleeding through new paint.
5. **Topcoats** to blend the repaired area into the rest of the wall, usually done as part of an [interior painting job](/services/painting/interior-house-painting).

## When Wall Repair Alone Is Not Enough

Some walls need more than fill-and-paint:

- **Active leak:** the pipe, roof or bathroom leak must be repaired first; otherwise the repair will fail within weeks.
- **External damp or groundwater:** a moisture barrier or injection treatment is needed on the source side — that is the [wall dampness & efflorescence repair](/services/waterproofing/wall-dampness-and-efflorescence-repair) scope under waterproofing.
- **Extensive plaster damage:** when plaster is hollow or crumbling across large areas, a [skim coating service](/services/skim-coat) for the full wall gives a flatter, longer-lasting result than patching dozens of spots.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Filling a structural or movement crack with ordinary wall putty and declaring it fixed.
- Painting over a wall that is still damp at its source.
- Skipping sealer on patched areas — the patches then show through as dull or shiny spots ("flashing").
- Sanding and repainting over mould without treating it.
- Using interior-grade filler in a bathroom or splash zone.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I know if a crack is structural?
Warning signs are cracks wider than a few millimetres, diagonal cracks running from door or window corners, cracks that visibly lengthen or reopen, and doors that suddenly stick. Cosmetic fillers will not stop these — have the cause assessed.

### Can the repair and repainting be done on the same visit?
Small hairline filling included in a painting job is normally done in sequence on the same project. Damp-affected areas need drying time between repair, sealer and paint, so they may stretch the schedule by a day or two.

### Will the cracks come back?
Stable hairline cracks that are properly filled usually stay closed. Movement cracks can recur; flexible filler systems reduce the risk but structural movement cannot be hidden by cosmetic repair — it needs monitoring and, if required, professional assessment.
