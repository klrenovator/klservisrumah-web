# Double Swing Autogate Installation in KL and Selangor

**Question:** Is a double swing autogate right for a wide landed-house entrance?

**Answer:** A double swing autogate works well for wide entrances where both gate leaves can swing safely inward or outward without blocking drains, slopes, parked cars or footpaths. Before buying, check gate width and weight, swing direction, pillar strength, power supply, motor type and safety features. A site measurement prevents the common problem of installing a motor that is too small or a gate that swings onto a public path.

## What Is a Double Swing Autogate?

A double swing autogate has two hinged leaves that open from the centre, each driven by its own motor or arm. It is common at landed homes in Kuala Lumpur and Selangor because it gives a wide, clear driveway opening and suits traditional wrought-iron or aluminium gate designs.

## Site Checks Before Installation

| Check | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Total gate opening | Determines leaf width and motor load |
| Gate weight | Motor and hinges must support the material |
| Swing direction | Must not hit public path, drain, slope or parked car |
| Pillar size and strength | Motors and brackets need solid mounting |
| Power supply | Control panel needs protected power nearby |
| Ground clearance | Sloping driveways can cause leaves to scrape |
| Autogate track/stopper | Leaves need correct open/close limits |
| Safety sensors | Prevents closing on vehicles or people |

## Motor Types

### Articulated arm motors
Visible arms are common on many Malaysian homes. They are easier to install on standard pillars and are practical for wider leaves.

### Underground/ram motors
These are hidden beneath the hinges for a cleaner look. They require proper drainage and stronger hinge preparation, making them more sensitive to flooding and poor installation.

### Linear arm motors
A piston-style arm can give a neat appearance and strong closing force, but suitability depends on pillar distance and geometry.

The correct choice depends on gate weight, leaf width, pillar dimensions and exposure. KL Servis Rumah confirms the motor model after site measurement rather than assuming a one-size-fits-all kit.

## Safety Features to Consider

- Obstacle detection so the gate stops or reverses if blocked.
- Photocell/safety beam across the opening.
- Adjustable auto-close timing.
- Manual release for power failure.
- Warning light or siren where useful.
- Remotes with secure coding.
- Backup power or battery option where appropriate.

A gate is heavy machinery. Children and pets should be kept away during operation, and the manual release should be understood by all household members.

## Installation Process

1. **Site measurement:** measure opening, pillars, gate weight and swing clearance.
2. **Motor selection:** choose arm type and motor capacity.
3. **Power preparation:** run protected wiring to the control panel location.
4. **Bracket and motor fitting:** mount securely to solid pillars.
5. **Limit setup:** set open/close positions and obstacle sensitivity.
6. **Safety testing:** test reversal, remotes and manual release.
7. **Handover:** demonstrate operation, maintenance and troubleshooting.

## Maintenance Tips

- Keep hinges lubricated and free of rust.
- Keep the swing area clear of stones, plant pots and parked cars.
- Test obstacle detection periodically.
- Check remotes and batteries.
- Have the motor serviced if the gate moves slowly or makes new noises.
- Do not keep forcing a gate that is binding; check the hinges and alignment first.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a double swing autogate open outward?
It can, but it must not obstruct a public road, footpath or drain. Many homes choose inward opening when space allows.

### What happens during a power cut?
Most autogates have a manual release. The location and operation should be explained during handover.

### Is a double swing or sliding gate better?
A swing gate needs clear side space for the leaves; a sliding gate needs side run space along the wall. The driveway layout usually decides.
