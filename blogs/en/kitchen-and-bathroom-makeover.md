# Kitchen & Bathroom Makeover in KL & Selangor

The kitchen and bathroom are the most-used rooms in a home — and often the most dated. A makeover can transform them without a full house renovation. KL Servis Rumah provides kitchen and bathroom makeovers across Kuala Lumpur and Selangor, from RM 10,000.

This guide explains what a makeover includes, what it costs, and how it differs from a full renovation.

## What a Kitchen Makeover Includes

- **Cabinetry** — new or resurfaced cabinets and doors.
- **Countertops** — new worktops (see our [kitchen cabinet & countertop service](/services/kitchen-cabinet) for options).
- **Sink and taps** — new or upgraded fittings.
- **Tiling and backsplash** — refreshed walls and splash areas.
- **Lighting and fittings** — updated lighting and fixtures.

## What a Bathroom Makeover Includes

- **New fixtures** — toilet, basin, and shower fittings.
- **Tiling** — refreshed floor and wall tiles.
- **Waterproofing** — ensuring wet areas are properly waterproofed.
- **Cabinetry and storage** — new vanity or storage units.
- **Lighting and mirrors** — updated fixtures.

## Makeover vs. Full Renovation

A makeover focuses on replacing and refreshing finishes and fixtures — cabinets, countertops, tiles, and fittings. A full renovation also includes structural changes, replumbing, and rewiring. If the underlying structure is sound, a makeover gives you a new look for less.

## The Makeover Process

1. **Assessment** — we inspect the current kitchen or bathroom and discuss your goals.
2. **Quote** — you receive an itemised quote for the makeover scope.
3. **Preparation** — removal of old fixtures and surfaces.
4. **Installation** — new cabinetry, countertops, tiling, and fittings are installed.
5. **Finishing and cleanup** — a clean, finished handover.

## How Much Does a Kitchen & Bathroom Makeover Cost?

A kitchen & bathroom makeover starts from **RM 10,000**. The final cost depends on:
