# Quartz or Granite Kitchen Countertop in Kuala Lumpur and Selangor

**Direct answer:** Quartz offers consistent colour and low routine sealing needs; granite offers natural variation and good heat tolerance but may need periodic sealing. The right countertop also depends on cabinet support, slab thickness, cut-outs, joint locations and how the kitchen is used.

## Compare the Available Approaches

| Approach | When it makes sense |
|---|---|
| **Engineered quartz** | consistent appearance and simple daily care. |
| **Natural granite** | unique stone pattern and greater direct-heat tolerance. |
| **Repair existing top** | minor chips or failed silicone with a sound slab. |

Choosing the least expensive option without checking its limits can cause repeat work. The quotation should explain why the selected approach suits the substrate, dimensions and intended use.

## Typical Work Process

1. **Template only after cabinets are fixed and level**.
2. **Confirm slab selection, thickness, edge and joint drawing**.
3. **Factory-cut and finish holes using appliance templates**.
4. **Dry-fit, support joints and bond the top without stressing cabinets**.
5. **Install sink support, seal edges and inspect level and joints**.

Preparation and sequencing matter. Existing defects, unstable supports, incompatible materials or damaged substrates should be corrected before they are concealed. Protect occupied rooms, common corridors and completed finishes throughout the work. At handover, test operation and inspect edges, fixings, joints and access points—not appearance alone.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Measuring before cabinets are level and final.
- Cutting a large sink opening without adequate rails or brackets.
- Assuming quartz cannot be damaged by hot pans.
- Hiding a long seam at a weak or visually obvious location.

A competent inspection should distinguish a serviceable component from a system that has reached the point of replacement. Ask for assumptions and exclusions to be written down, especially where concealed conditions cannot be confirmed until work opens the area.

## Safety, Access and Maintenance

Do not bypass manufacturer instructions, building management requirements or safe access arrangements to save time. Keep product references and care instructions after handover. Routine cleaning and early attention to movement, moisture, corrosion or loose hardware usually make local repairs simpler.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does quartz stain?
It is low-porosity, not indestructible. Wipe coloured spills promptly and use products approved by the slab manufacturer.

### Can an undermount sink hang from the stone alone?
It should have a designed support method; adhesive alone should not be treated as permanent structural support.

### When is sealing needed?
Natural granite may need sealing depending on porosity and product guidance. Engineered quartz normally follows different care instructions.
