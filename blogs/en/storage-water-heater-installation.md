A storage heater is the right choice when more than one bathroom — or a bathroom plus kitchen — needs hot water. This guide explains how storage heaters work, what installation involves, and how to keep the tank lasting longer.

## How a Storage Tank Heater Works

A storage heater is an insulated tank (usually 15L, 25L, 30L, 40L or 50L) that heats water to a set temperature and stores it, feeding several outlets. When a tap opens, the stored hot water is pushed out and cold water refills the tank, triggering the element to reheat.

### Advantages

- Hot water at multiple points — two bathrooms, or bathroom and kitchen.
- Steadier temperature than an instant heater.
- Runs at a lower electrical load, which suits homes with limited electrical capacity.
- Can be positioned in a bathroom, storeroom, or kitchen cabinet (space permitting).

### Limitations

- Limited volume — after heavy use the tank needs time to reheat.
- Takes up wall or floor space; a full 50L tank is heavy and needs a solid wall.
- Slightly higher standby heat loss (modern units use better insulation).

If you only need hot water in one shower, an [instant heater](/services/water-heater/instant-water-heater-installation) is cheaper and smaller.

## What Installation Includes

A professional storage heater installation covers:

1. **Sizing & positioning** — we confirm the tank size to match your usage, and a position with solid wall support (concrete or brick). For floor-standing units, the base must be level and able to take the full weight.
2. **Wall mounting** — the tank is secured with appropriate anchors. A full 50L tank weighs roughly 60kg–70kg when full, so mounting is not a job for DIY hooks.
3. **Pressure relief valve** — fitted to release excess pressure and protect the tank.
4. **Pipework** — SIRIM-compliant piping connects the tank to the required outlets, with isolation valves so the tank can be serviced without draining the whole system.
5. **Electrical connection** — a dedicated circuit with isolator and RCCB protection, installed by ST-registered wiremen.
6. **Testing** — the tank is filled, checked for leaks, and the temperature settings and safety devices are verified.

## Choosing the Right Tank Size

| Household | Recommended size |
|---|---|
| 1–2 people, one bathroom | 15L–25L |
| 3–4 people, one bathroom + kitchen | 30L–40L |
| 4+ people, two bathrooms | 40L–50L |

Remember that a tank serves stored water first — if two people shower back-to-back, allow reheat time between uses. For families with high hot-water demand, ask us about heat-pump systems, which heat water more efficiently over time.

## Maintenance That Extends Tank Life

- **Check the anode rod** — the sacrificial anode inside the tank protects it from corrosion. On Malaysian water, it should be inspected every 1–2 years and replaced when worn.
- **Descaling** — hard water areas (parts of Klang, Shah Alam and KL) build scale that slows heating. Descaling restores performance.
- **Test the pressure relief valve** — lift the lever occasionally to confirm it releases and reseats.
- **Watch for leaks** — water around the tank or a continuously running refill usually means a failing valve or a corroded tank.

### Where can a storage tank heater be installed?

On a solid wall in a bathroom, storeroom, balcony (weather-protected), or kitchen cabinet area — provided there is clearance for the tank, access for servicing, and a suitable water supply. Our installer will advise on the best position at the site check.

### How long does installation take?

A typical storage heater installation takes half a day, including pipework to the outlets. If new plumbing or a new circuit is required, allow more time.

### Do storage heaters work during a water supply interruption?

No — they need mains water pressure to feed the tank. They do, however, keep water hot during a brief power interruption until the stored heat is used.

### Can a storage heater be installed in a condominium?

Yes, where the management allows and wall space permits. We coordinate approval and work-hour requirements with building management for condominium installations across KL and Selangor.
