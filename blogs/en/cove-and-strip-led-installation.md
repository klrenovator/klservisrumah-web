Sequence matters when several trades share the area. ## How the Work Is Carried Out

1. **Mock** — mock up brightness and colour. Record the finding before materials are ordered.

2. **Prepare** — prepare clean continuous profile. Protect adjacent finishes and keep the work area controlled.

3. **Size** — size feeds and drivers. Check alignment, coverage, support or connections as the work progresses.

4. **Install** — install and connect. Do not conceal the stage until the responsible person has inspected it.

5. **Test** — test dimming heat and dark spots. Test under normal use and record any care or cure-time restriction.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Sticking bare strip to hot dusty plaster

- Running too long from one feed and accepting dim ends

- Sealing drivers where they cannot be replaced

- Mixing batches with visibly different colour


Do not bypass structural, electrical, waterproofing or product requirements to save preparation time. Stop work if hidden moisture, unsafe wiring, weak support or moving cracks are discovered; diagnose these conditions before covering them.

## Frequently Asked Questions


### Why does an LED strip look darker at one end?
Voltage drop increases along an undersized or overly long run. Correct cable sizing and additional feed points help.


### Is an aluminium profile necessary?
It improves heat dissipation, alignment and protection; it is especially useful for higher-output strips.


### Can cove lighting be the only room light?
Sometimes for ambience, but task areas often need downlights or dedicated fixtures as well.
