# Window Glass Replacement in KL & Selangor

You do not need a new window because one pane cracked. If the frame is still square and the gasket channel is intact, the glass can be replaced on its own.

**Direct answer:** KL Servis Rumah replaces broken, cracked or failed window glass from **RM 200**, matching the existing size and thickness, or upgrading to tinted, frosted or tempered glass in the same frame.

Related repairs: [window seal replacement](/services/window-repair/window-seal-replacement) if rain still enters after the pane is sound, and the main [window repair](/services/window-repair) service for handles, hinges and rollers.

## When glass replacement is the right job

Replace the pane when you have:

- A visible crack, chip or shatter.
- A hole from impact (ball, ladder, branch).
- Fog or condensation **between** the panes of a double-glazed unit — the unit seal has failed.
- Glass that is badly scratched or has an old film that cannot be removed.

Do **not** start with new glass if the only issue is a draught with an intact pane — that is usually the [rubber seal](/services/window-repair/window-seal-replacement).

## Clear, tinted, frosted or tempered?

| Glass | Typical use | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Clear annealed 4–5 mm | Standard house windows | Breaks in shards — not for wet-area doors |
| Tinted (grey / bronze / green) | West-facing rooms, glare | Cuts some heat; still a single pane unless specified |
| Frosted / obscure | Bathroom, stair, neighbour sightline | Privacy while keeping light |
| Tempered | Doors, low sills, wet areas, large panels | Breaks into small granules; lead time 2–5 working days |
| Laminated | Security / overhead / some shopfronts | Holds together if broken |

We do not put ordinary annealed glass in a shower screen or a door panel. Shower work is under [glass and aluminium](/services/glass-aluminium).

## Same-day vs made-to-order

- **Same-day (landed, common sizes):** 4 mm and 5 mm clear or tinted that we can cut to the measured opening.
- **Made-to-order:** tempered, laminated, unusual thickness, or double-glazed units. We measure, board the opening if it is not weather-tight, and return when the factory pane is ready (typically 2–5 working days).

High-rise condo glass that must be handled from the outside may need access equipment. That is quoted after we see the opening — we do not guess a facade price.

## How glass is replaced

1. Measure thickness, type (annealed vs tempered — a tempered logo is often in the corner), and the rebate.
2. Tape the old pane, remove beads or gaskets, lift the glass out.
3. Clean the rebate; fit setting blocks so the new pane is not sitting on aluminium.
4. Set the new glass; refit beads; renew the gasket if it has hardened.
5. Clean and check the sash still closes without stressing the new pane.

A new pane in an old, rock-hard gasket will leak. We will say if the seal must be replaced at the same time.

## Safety while you wait

- Keep people and pets away from a starred pane.
- Do not slam the sash.
- If the pane is gone, we can board it so rain and mosquitoes stay out.
- Upper-floor openings should not be left open overnight.

## Common mistakes

- Taping a long crack and forgetting it through thunderstorm season.
- Ordering glass from a photo without a thickness measurement.
- Forcing a tempered offcut into a smaller frame (tempered cannot be recut).
- Replacing glass but leaving blocked weep holes, so the next rain still wets the sill.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you match tinted glass to the other panes?

We match the common grey and bronze tints as closely as stock allows. Old sun-faded tint may not match a new piece perfectly; we will show a sample.

### The crack is only in the corner. Can I leave it?

A crack grows. On a high window or a door, replace it before the next storm.

### Do you replace glass in aluminium and timber frames?

Yes. The bead and gasket method differs; the idea is the same.

### Is this the same as a new aluminium window?

No. New frames are [glass and aluminium fabrication](/services/glass-aluminium). This service reuses your frame.
