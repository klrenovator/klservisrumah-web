# Fire-Rated Door Installation in Kuala Lumpur and Selangor

**Direct answer:** A fire-rated door is a tested door-set, not merely a heavy door leaf. The leaf, frame, intumescent and smoke seals, hinges, closer, latch, glazing and installation details must match the required rating and the approved product documentation.

This guide explains the [Fire-Rated Door Installation](/services/door/fire-rated-door-installation) scope under [door services](/services/door). The published starting price is **RM 950**. ## What Should Be Checked Before Work

- **Required fire rating and building or management requirement.**
- **Opening size, wall construction and frame fixing substrate.**
- **Complete compatible door-set labels and documentation.**
- **Swing direction, escape clearance, closer and latch operation.**

## Compare the Available Approaches

| Approach | When it makes sense |
|---|---|
| **1-hour set** | where the approved requirement specifies that rating. |
| **2-hour set** | where a higher tested rating is required. |
| **Ordinary door** | only where no rated separation is required. |

Choosing the least expensive option without checking its limits can cause repeat work. The quotation should explain why the selected approach suits the substrate, dimensions and intended use.

## Typical Work Process

1. **Confirm requirement and select a documented compatible door-set**.
2. **Survey opening, wall and all interfaces before ordering**.
3. **Prepare the opening and fix the frame by the tested method**.
4. **Hang the leaf and install seals, hardware, closer and latch**.
5. **Adjust self-closing, inspect gaps and retain product records**.

Preparation and sequencing matter. Existing defects, unstable supports, incompatible materials or damaged substrates should be corrected before they are concealed. Protect occupied rooms, common corridors and completed finishes throughout the work. At handover, test operation and inspect edges, fixings, joints and access points—not appearance alone.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Mixing an approved leaf with an unapproved frame or hardware.
- Cutting the leaf excessively for floor clearance.
- Removing the closer because the door is inconvenient.
- Painting over or damaging intumescent seals and labels.

A competent inspection should distinguish a serviceable component from a system that has reached the point of replacement. Ask for assumptions and exclusions to be written down, especially where concealed conditions cannot be confirmed until work opens the area.

## Safety, Access and Maintenance

Do not bypass manufacturer instructions, building management requirements or safe access arrangements to save time. Keep product references and care instructions after handover. Routine cleaning and early attention to movement, moisture, corrosion or loose hardware usually make local repairs simpler.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can an existing frame be reused?
Only if the approved door-set documentation permits that frame and it meets the required condition and fixing details. Do not assume compatibility.

### Must the door close by itself?
Fire doors protecting rated openings commonly rely on a functioning closer and positive latch. Follow the approved design and building requirement.

### Can the bottom be trimmed later?
Only within the manufacturer-approved allowance. Unauthorised trimming can invalidate the tested assembly.
