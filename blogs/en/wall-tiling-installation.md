# Wall Tiling Installation in KL & Selangor

Wall tiling installation in Kuala Lumpur or Selangor starts from RM 16 per square foot, covering kitchen splashbacks, bathroom walls, and feature walls. KL Servis Rumah installs ceramic, porcelain, mosaic, glass, and natural stone wall tiles with straight or brick-bond patterns.

Wall tiles protect walls from water, grease, and stains while defining a room's look. Bathroom and kitchen walls in particular need the right preparation — including waterproofing in wet areas — or the tiles will eventually hollow and drop. This guide explains the process, design options, and costs.

## Wall Tiling Applications

- **Kitchen splashback** — the wall between worktop and cabinets, protecting it from splashes and grease. Usually one or two rows of tiles in a band, or full height behind the hob.
- **Bathroom walls** — full or half-height tiling around showers, baths, and basins.
- **Feature walls** — a tiled accent wall in a living room, bedroom, or entryway.
- **Commercial cladding** — shop counters, restaurant frontages, and office reception walls.

## The Wall Tiling Process

1. **Substrate check** — the installer verifies the wall is flat, sound, and clean. Plastered walls may need skimming; painted walls need the right key for adhesion.
2. **Waterproofing (wet areas)** — for shower and bath areas, a waterproofing membrane is applied before tiling so water cannot migrate behind the tiles.
3. **Layout planning** — the tile pattern is planned (straight, brick-bond, diagonal, or herringbone), starting from a level line so cuts sit neatly at the bottom or least visible edge.
4. **Adhesive** — the correct wall adhesive is applied with a notched trowel; large or heavy tiles are back-buttered as well.
5. **Fixing** — tiles are set, tapped level, and spaced; tile spacers keep joints even and level.
6. **Grouting** — joints are grouted and finished; mould-resistant grout is used in wet areas.
7. **Sealing and finishing** — porous tiles and natural stone are sealed; edges and corners are finished cleanly (for example, with silicone at tub and basin junctions).

## Design Options

| Pattern | Effect |
|---|---|
| Straight (grid) | Clean, classic, suits most rooms |
| Brick-bond (offset) | Adds texture and movement, very popular for splashbacks |
| Diagonal | Creates a larger-feeling space, more cuts |
| Herringbone / chevron | Distinctive feature look for accents and splashbacks |
| Mosaic sheets | Fine detail on splashbacks; needs a very flat substrate |

**Size trends in KL & Selangor:** large-format wall slabs (600×1200mm) are popular for a seamless modern look in bathrooms; subway tiles (75×300mm) and mosaics suit classic and feature styles.

## What Affects the Cost

| Factor | Effect on price |
|---|---|
| Tile size | Large-format and tiny mosaic both cost more per square foot |
| Pattern complexity | Diagonal and herringbone need more cuts and labour |
| Wet-area waterproofing | Membrane application adds to the scope in showers |
| Wall condition | Uneven walls need skimming or levelling first |
| Height of tiling | Full-height walls use more tile and labour than a splashback band |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does it cost to tile a bathroom wall in Malaysia?

Installation starts from RM 16 per square foot, plus tiles and waterproofing. A typical master bathroom wall area of 400–500 sq ft is roughly RM 6,500–8,500 in labour and membrane work before tiles.

### Can wall tiles be installed over old tiles?

It is possible but rarely recommended on walls — the old tiles must be perfectly sound and the extra thickness affects corners and trims. Removing old wall tiles is usually safer, especially in wet areas where the substrate needs checking.

### Do bathroom walls need waterproofing before tiling?

Yes, in shower and wet areas. Without a membrane, water seeps through grout lines and travels behind tiles, causing damp, mould, and eventually hollow tiles in the room below — see our waterproofing services for details.

### How long does wall tiling take?

A kitchen splashback can be done in a day. A full bathroom wall installation typically takes 2–3 days including waterproofing, tiling, and grouting.

### Which wall tile is easiest to maintain?

Porcelain is the most durable and easiest to clean for walls. Glazed ceramic is also low-maintenance. Natural stone looks premium but needs regular sealing.
