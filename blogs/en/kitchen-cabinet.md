## Modular vs custom plywood — which kitchen is right?

| | Modular | Custom plywood |
|---|---|---|
| Best for | Standard L, U or straight kitchens | Odd corners, low beams, full-height pantry, unusual pipe positions |
| Carcass | 18 mm melamine boxes | Moisture-resistant plywood, cut to the wall |
| Lead time | Shorter once the design is locked | Longer — typically 3–4 weeks in the workshop |
| Install | Often about 1 day for a standard run | 1–2 days plus countertop templating |
| Starting price | From RM 7,500 (10 ft L-shape) | From RM 11,000 |

Neither option is “cheap kitchen” versus “luxury kitchen”. A well-specified modular kitchen with Blum hinges and a quartz top will outlast a poorly built plywood kitchen with no edge banding and no soft-close. The decision is about fit, access and how long you plan to stay.

## What lasts in a Malaysian kitchen

Klang Valley kitchens fail in predictable ways: swollen carcass edges near the sink, peeling door film next to the hob, sagging hinges, and countertops that stain or crack at the sink cutout.

Professionals normally specify:

- **Moisture-resistant carcass** near the sink, dishwasher and hob — not raw particleboard with exposed edges.
- **Soft-close hinges and runners** (Häfele or Blum are the usual specification on our quotes) so doors do not slam and drawers stay aligned.
- **Proper edge banding** on every exposed board edge. Unbanded edges absorb steam first.
- **A heat gap and filler panel** beside the oven or hob so the door finish does not cook.
- **A countertop that matches how you cook** — granite for high heat and unique stone; engineered quartz for a non-porous, consistent colour.

If cabinet doors already sit crooked or will not close, that is usually hardware or a swollen box, not “the kitchen is finished”. See our guide on a [misaligned kitchen cabinet door](/problems/kitchen-cabinet-door-misaligned).

## When to replace the kitchen — and when a door makeover is enough

Replace the full kitchen when:

- The carcass is swollen, delaminated or infested.
- You need a new layout, taller units, or a full-height pantry.
- Plumbing or electrical points must move with the cabinets.
- You want a new countertop that the old boxes cannot support.

A **doors-and-hardware makeover** (from RM 4,200) is enough when the boxes are still square, dry and firmly fixed. New doors, soft-close hinges and runners can make a 10-year-old kitchen look current without a full demolition.

## How a kitchen cabinet job is done

1. **Site measurement and design brief** — we measure walls, ceiling height, window, pipes, gas point and electrical points. You confirm layout (L, U, island, straight), storage needs and finish.
2. **Design and quotation** — you receive an itemised quote: carcass, doors, hardware brand, countertop, sink cutout, and any plumbing or electrical work.
3. **Manufacturing** — modular boxes are ordered or assembled to the locked design. Custom plywood is cut and edged in the workshop (typically 3–4 weeks).
4. **Demolition and prep** — old units come out, services are isolated, and the wall line is checked. Waterproofing or tiling in the wet zone is completed first if it is in scope.
5. **Installation and handover** — cabinets are levelled, scribed to the wall, hardware is adjusted, the countertop is templated and fitted, and you walk through every door and drawer.

Typical design-to-handover time for a full kitchen is **6 to 10 weeks**, depending on countertop lead time and whether plumbing or electrical points move.

## Common mistakes we see

- Ordering cabinets from a photo without measuring the actual pipe and window positions.
- Choosing a high-gloss door next to a wok burner with no heat shield.
- Skipping a countertop template and cutting the sink hole on site “by eye”.
- Replacing doors on a swollen carcass — the new doors will still sit out of line.
- Leaving the old silicone line under a new top, which traps water at the back splash.

## Frequently asked questions

### Modular or custom plywood — which should I choose?

Choose modular if your kitchen is a standard L, U or straight run and you want a faster, lower starting price. Choose custom plywood if walls are out of square, you need full-height pantry units, or you want the carcass built around existing pipes and beams.

### What countertop is most common in Malaysian homes?

Local granite is still widely used because it handles heat well. Engineered quartz (Caesarstone, Silestone) is the usual premium choice when you want a non-porous, consistent colour and a cleaner undermount sink.

### Can you keep my existing boxes and only change the doors?

Yes, if the carcass is still dry and square. That is the door-and-hardware makeover, from RM 4,200.

### How long does a full kitchen take?

Usually 6 to 10 weeks from design lock to handover: design, manufacturing, demolition, cabinet install, then countertop template and fit.

### Do you work in condos?

Yes. We follow management work hours, protect the lift, and remove debris. Measurement and install are planned around your building’s booking slots.
