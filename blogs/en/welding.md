## On-Site vs Workshop Fabrication

1. **On-Site Mobile Welding:** Our technicians arrive equipped with portable inverter MIG and MMA arc welding machines, metal cut-off tools, and anti-rust coatings for rapid fixes to gates, railings, and security grilles.
2. **Workshop Fabrication:** Large projects like complete driveway gates, roof trusses, and staircase stringers are fabricated in a controlled workshop with laser cutting, precision jig alignment, and full-immersion hot-dip galvanising or high-bake powder coating before on-site installation.

## Anti-Corrosion Protection for Malaysian Weather

Raw steel exposed to rain and humidity in KL will rust within weeks. Every metal fabrication project we handle includes a 3-layer anti-corrosion protection standard:

- **Surface De-scaling & Grinding:** Mechanical wire-brushing and grinding of all weld spatter, scale, and surface oxidation.
- **Zinc Phosphate / Epoxy Primer:** Application of high-solid zinc chromate or 2-part epoxy anti-corrosion primer to chemically seal the steel.
- **Industrial Topcoat or Powder Coating:** Factory-cured architectural polyester powder coat (matte black, bronze, white, anthracite) or 2 coats of weather-resistant gloss enamel.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you weld broken gate hinges on the same day?
Yes, for emergency gate hinge repairs where the gate is detached or posing a safety hazard, we prioritize same-day mobile welding service across KL and Selangor.

### What is the difference between mild steel and stainless steel grilles?
Mild steel is cost-effective and highly customizable in color via powder coating, but requires proper anti-rust priming. Stainless steel (Grade 304) contains chromium and nickel, making it naturally impervious to rust without paint, though at a higher material cost.

### Do your custom window grilles include emergency fire escape hatches?
Yes, Malaysian fire safety guidelines recommend at least one hinged emergency escape hatch with a padlock bracket in each bedroom window grille. We include fire escape hatches in our window grille designs.
