# Modular Kitchen Cabinet (10 ft L-Shape) in KL & Selangor

A modular kitchen uses factory-sized cabinet boxes that are planned as a system, then installed as a run. For many terrace, condo and apartment kitchens in the Klang Valley, a **10 ft L-shape** is the standard layout: one wall for the hob and hood, the return wall for the sink and prep.

## What “modular 10 ft L-shape” actually includes

The published starting price is a **starting specification**, not a one-size kitchen for every home. A typical modular L-shape quote covers:

- Base and wall units planned to a 10 ft L (about 3 m + 3 m of run, adjusted to your walls).
- **18 mm melamine carcass** with edge banding.
- **Häfele or Blum** hinges and drawer runners (soft-close on the quoted spec).
- Door and drawer fronts in laminate, acrylic or spray-painted finish.
- Standard drawers, a sink base, and a hob/oven housing as designed.
- On-site levelling, scribing and hardware adjustment.

It does **not** automatically include a quartz or granite top, a sink, a hob, or moving water and electrical points. Those are itemised. Countertops are quoted separately — see [quartz and granite countertops](/services/kitchen-cabinet/quartz-granite-countertop) from RM 220 / linier ft.

## When a modular kitchen is the right choice

Modular is usually the better fit when:

- The kitchen is a normal L, U or straight run with reasonably square walls.
- You want a faster path from design lock to install.
- You want consistent factory boxes and a lower starting price than full custom.
- You are refreshing a condo or apartment kitchen without moving the sink or hob.

Choose custom plywood instead when the room has a low beam, an odd pipe chase, a full-height pantry that must hit the ceiling, or walls that are badly out of square. That is covered on the [custom plywood kitchen](/services/kitchen-cabinet/custom-plywood-kitchen) page.

## Door finishes that work in Malaysian kitchens

| Finish | Why people choose it | Watch-outs |
|---|---|---|
| Laminate | Durable, wide colours, easiest to live with near heat and oil | Cheap thin laminate peels at edges if banding is poor |
| Acrylic | High-gloss, modern, easy to wipe | Shows fingerprints; keep a heat gap beside the hob |
| Spray-painted | Colour-matched, furniture look | Needs a proper primer system; chips must be touched in |

Humidity and wok heat are harder on doors than on the boxes. A heat filler and the correct hood matter more than the colour you pick from a catalogue.

If you are still deciding carcass and door boards, the [kitchen cabinet material guide](/blog/kitchen-cabinet-material-guide-malaysia) explains how common Malaysian materials behave.

## How modular installation is done

1. **Measure** — walls, ceiling, window, existing sink, hob, hood and isolators.
2. **Lock the module plan** — box widths are planned so you do not end with a useless 80 mm leftover.
3. **Quote** — carcass, doors, hardware brand, extras (tall unit, inner drawers, LED), and any service relocation.
4. **Manufacture / supply** — boxes and doors are prepared to the locked drawing.
5. **Install** — old units out, new run levelled, scribed, connected at the L-corner, hardware tuned.
6. **Top and services** — countertop template after the boxes are fixed, then sink and hob cutouts.

A standard modular install is often completed in **about a day** once the old kitchen is out and the walls are ready. The longer wait is usually manufacturing and the stone top.

## What professionals check before they order boxes

- Whether the L-corner can take a carousel, magic corner, or two dead-end bases.
- Hood height versus the window lintel.
- Dishwasher or washing-machine gap if you want one later.
- Whether the existing floor tiles will stay (they must be level enough for a plinth).
- Condo lift size — tall units sometimes go up in two pieces.

Crooked doors on an old kitchen are not always a reason to replace everything. If the boxes are still sound, a [door and hardware makeover](/services/kitchen-cabinet) from RM 4,200 may be enough. If doors already hang out of line, read [kitchen cabinet door misaligned](/problems/kitchen-cabinet-door-misaligned).

## Common mistakes with modular kitchens

- Buying “10 ft” from a showroom without measuring the actual window and pipe.
- Forcing a leftover strip at the fridge end instead of resizing two boxes.
- Installing boxes before the splashback tiles are finished, then fighting the thickness.
- Skipping a countertop template and hoping the L-corner is 90 degrees.
