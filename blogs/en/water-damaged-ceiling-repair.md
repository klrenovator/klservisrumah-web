# Water-Damaged Ceiling Repair in KL & Selangor

A water-stained, sagging, or mouldy ceiling is one of the clearest signs of a water problem in your home. KL Servis Rumah provides professional water-damaged ceiling repair across Kuala Lumpur and Selangor, from RM 220.

The key to a lasting repair is simple: fix the source of the water first, then repair the ceiling. If you only repaint the stain, the leak will return and the damage will spread.

## Signs of a Water-Damaged Ceiling

- **Brown or yellow stains** — classic signs of a past or ongoing leak.
- **Sagging or bulging** — the plasterboard is soaking up water and weakening.
- **Peeling or bubbling paint** on the ceiling.
- **Mould or black spots** — from persistent moisture.
- **Cracks** along ceiling panels or joints.

## What Causes Ceiling Water Damage?

The most common sources are:

- **Roof leaks** — after heavy rain, especially where roof tiles are damaged.
- **Leaking pipes** — pipes running above the ceiling in bathrooms or kitchens.
- **Air-conditioner condensation** — a clogged drain pan overflowing.
- **Waterproofing failure** — water from a bathroom above seeping through the slab.

Finding the source is essential. If your leak appears after rain, the roof is often the culprit — see our [ceiling leak after rain guide](/problems/ceiling-leak-after-rain).

## The Correct Repair Process

1. **Find and fix the water source** — this is the most important step.
2. **Dry the area** — allow the ceiling and insulation to dry fully.
3. **Remove damaged material** — cut away water-soaked, weakened plasterboard.
4. **Treat mould** — clean and treat any mould growth before finishing.
5. **Patch and skim** — replace the board and skim the surface smooth.
6. **Prime and repaint** — apply a stain-sealing primer and repaint.

Skipping the source-fixing step is the most common reason the damage returns.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I just paint over a water stain?

Painting over a stain without fixing the leak will fail — the stain reappears as the moisture continues. The leak must be fixed and the stain sealed before repainting.

### How do I know if the ceiling is still leaking?

If the stain grows after rain or when you use a bathroom above, the leak is active. If it is stable and dry, the leak has stopped but the damage still needs repair.

### How long does ceiling repair take?

A small stained area can be repaired in a day. Larger damage with board replacement takes longer and depends on drying time.

### Is a sagging ceiling dangerous?

A sagging, waterlogged ceiling can eventually collapse. If it is bulging significantly or dripping, treat it as a priority and have it inspected.
