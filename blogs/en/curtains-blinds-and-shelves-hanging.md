# Curtain, Blind & Shelf Installation in KL and Selangor

**Question:** Why do curtain tracks and shelves come loose after a few months?

**Answer:** Almost always because the fixing does not match the wall. A plastic plug hammered into hollow gypsum board holds a picture frame, but not a track loaded with heavy curtains being pulled twice a day. Every wall in a Malaysian home — concrete beam, clay brick, cement block or gypsum partition — needs its own drill bit and its own anchor type, and load-bearing mounts also need level marking, correct bracket spacing and torqued fixings. Get the anchor wrong and the mount sags, tears out or falls.

## What Can Be Mounted

| Item | Key mounting considerations |
|---|---|
| Curtain tracks and poles | Bracket span, block-out for full coverage, constant daily pull forces |
| Roller and roman blinds | Exact width measurement, recess vs face mounting, control side |
| Floating shelves | Hidden brackets must seat into solid anchors; load rating matters |
| Mirrors and framed art | Weight-rated hooks; alignment across grouped frames |
| Wall shelves and racks | Stud or solid-wall fixing; coraline for heavy kitchen racks |
| Observers and notice boards | Cable management and accessibility |

## Match the Anchor to the Wall

| Wall type | What works | What fails |
|---|---|---|
| Concrete wall or beam | Masonry bit + expansion anchor or sleeve anchor | Nothing much — concrete holds the best |
| Solid clay brick | Masonry bit + expansion plug into solid brick face | Fixings that land in the mortar joint |
| Hollow block / lightweight block | Longer sleeve anchors or chemical anchors | Small plugs that grip only the thin shell |
| Gypsum board partition | Stud-found fixings or rated cavity anchors (toggle types) | Plain plastic plugs — they rip out under load |

Buying mounts labelled "suits all walls" is how [curtain tracks fall down](/problems/curtain-track-falling) months later. The same physics is why [TVs fall off walls](/problems/tv-fell-off-wall) — lightweight fixings under a heavy, daily-moved load.

## What Professional Installation Includes

1. **Measure and mark** — track/shelf positions levelled with a spirit level or laser; brackets spaced per the load, not per convenience.
2. **Wall check** — identify the wall type and tap-test for cavities before drilling; avoid concealed wiring and pipes in the fixing zone.
3. **Drill with the right bit** — masonry bits for hard surfaces, correct depth and diameter so anchors seat fully.
4. **Anchor selection** — anchor matched to wall type and expected load, including pull-out and shear.
5. **Mount and torque** — brackets and tracks fastened and checked firm; no over-tightening that crumbles the plug.
6. **Test load** — the mount is load-tested before handover: track pulled, shelf pressed, blind operated.
7. **Clean finish** — dust contained, holes level with the surface, packaging consolidated.

## Curtain Tracks vs Poles vs Blinds — Quick Guide

- **Double curtain tracks** suit day/night curtain pairs and take pelmets; they need firm, evenly-spaced brackets across the full span.
- **Curtain poles** are more decorative but depend on fewer brackets — each bracket carries more load, so wall fixing quality matters even more.
- **Roller and roman blinds** mount inside or outside the window recess; recess mounts make a room look tidier but need accurate width so the blind does not bind the sides.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you hang a track over a gypsum partition ceiling pelmet?
Yes — but the fixings must either land in the timber/metal structure inside or use rated cavity anchors sized for the load. Drilling raw into plain board is the most common cause of fallen tracks within the first year.

### How much weight can a floating shelf take?
It depends on the bracket system and wall, not the shelf. Properly anchored into solid wall, floating shelves take books and crockery well; on gypsum board the realistic safe load drops sharply unless the brackets find studs. State what you plan to load and the installer will fix to that.

### Do you supply curtains and blinds or install ours?
Both arrangements work. Customer-supplied tracks, poles and blinds are installed happily; if you prefer supply-and-install of mounting hardware such as brackets and anchors, that can be included in the quote.
