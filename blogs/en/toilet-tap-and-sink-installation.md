## What a Proper Installation Involves

1. **Site assessment** — we check the existing pipework, water pressure, and mounting surface.
2. **Sizing and fitting** — we confirm the right tap or fixture size and connection type.
3. **Water isolation** — the supply is isolated safely before removal or installation.
4. **Fitting and sealing** — the fixture is installed, levelled, and sealed properly.
5. **Leak testing** — connections are pressurised and checked for leaks.
6. **Clean handover** — the area is cleaned and we confirm everything works.

## Why Professional Installation Matters

- **Correct sealing** — a poor seal leaks water behind the fitting and into the wall.
- **Right connections** — mismatched hoses or fittings cause drips and damage.
- **No damage to surfaces** — professional handling avoids scratching tiles or basins.
- **Warranty** — professionally installed fittings come with workmanship protection.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I install a new tap myself?

Simple replacement of a similar tap is DIY-friendly if you can isolate the water supply. However, if pipework needs adjustment, the wall is hard-to-reach, or you want a guarantee, professional installation is safer.

### Do you install bidet spray fittings?

Yes. We install and connect bidet spray kits as part of our toilet work, including the water supply connection.

### What should I do if a new fitting leaks?

If a newly installed fitting leaks, the seal or connection was likely fitted incorrectly. Contact us and we will re-check and fix it under our workmanship warranty.

### Do you supply the toilet, tap, or sink?

We can install fittings you have purchased, or advise on suitable options. We do not push specific brands; we fit what suits your bathroom and budget.
