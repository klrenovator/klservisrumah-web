## How often should you book?

There is no universal answer, but these patterns hold in Klang Valley homes:

- **Weekly** — households with children, pets, or both working adults; condos where dust from nearby construction settles fast; homes with light-coloured floors that show everything.
- **Fortnightly** — couples, smaller households, homes where daily tidying already happens. This is the most common landed-home pattern.
- **Monthly** — low-occupancy units, holiday homes, or homes where the owner cleans but wants the heavy work done periodically.
- **One-off** — before Raya, Chinese New Year, Deepavali or Christmas, before guests arrive, or after a period of neglect.

Klang Valley adds two local factors: high humidity encourages mould in bathrooms and behind furniture, and roadside or construction dust returns quickly on high floors with open windows.

## What we ask from you

A clean goes faster and better when:

- Personal items and paperwork are cleared off the surfaces you want wiped.
- Someone can grant access, or you arrange a key or access card handover.
- Pets are secured if they are nervous around strangers or vacuums.
- Any product sensitivities (asthma, allergies, natural stone, timber floors) are stated upfront.
- Areas you want left alone are marked. Home offices and children's study desks are the usual ones.

## Common mistakes when hiring cleaners

- **Booking a different person every time.** A cleaner who knows your home works faster and misses less.
- **Expecting a standard clean to remove built-up grime.** Limescale, grout staining and grease need a deep clean first, then maintenance keeps it that way. See [stubborn bathroom limescale](/problems/stubborn-bathroom-limescale).
- **Assuming any product is safe on any floor.** Acidic cleaners damage natural stone; excess water damages timber and some laminates.
- **Booking a normal clean right after renovation.** Cement dust and grout haze need [post-renovation cleaning](/services/post-renovation-cleaning) — see [construction dust that keeps coming back](/problems/construction-dust-returning).
- **Not fixing the underlying issue.** If mould returns within days of every clean, the problem is ventilation or waterproofing, not the cleaning. See [mould in the bathroom after cleaning](/problems/mold-in-bathroom-after-cleaning).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need to be home during the clean?

No. Many clients arrange access with a key, access card or a family member. We confirm your preferred access arrangement before the first visit.

Yes, standard cleaning products and equipment are included. If you prefer we use your own products — for natural stone, timber or allergy reasons — just tell us.

That is the intention for recurring bookings. If your regular cleaner is unavailable, we arrange a replacement so your schedule is not disrupted.

Interior glass and reachable frames are included in a spring clean or deep clean. Exterior high-rise glass is not, for safety reasons.

### Do you clean small offices?

Yes, offices under about 3,000 sqft, typically after hours so your team is not disrupted.
