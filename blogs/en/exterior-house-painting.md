# Exterior House Painting in KL and Selangor

**Question:** What is involved in a proper exterior house painting job?

**Answer:** Exterior painting is more than applying colour to walls. It includes inspection for cracks and dampness, cleaning and scraping, repairing damaged surfaces, applying a suitable primer/sealer, and two topcoats of weatherproof exterior paint. In Malaysia's UV, rain and humidity, preparation and paint quality determine how long the finish lasts.

This sub-service article covers **exterior house painting**. For interiors, see [interior house painting](/services/painting/interior-house-painting). For walls that are already deteriorating, see [faded exterior paint in Malaysia](/problems/faded-exterior-paint-malaysia) and [damp walls and paint bubbling](/problems/damp-walls-paint-bubbling).

## When to Repaint the Exterior

- Faded or chalky walls after years of sun and rain.
- Peeling, flaking or blistering paint.
- Hairline cracks along walls, joints or parapets.
- Efflorescence — white powdery salt deposits.
- Mould or algae on shaded walls.
- New plaster that needs a complete protective coating system.

## Surface Preparation

A durable exterior finish starts with preparation:

1. **Inspection:** check cracks, hollow areas, damp patches and timber/metal trim.
2. **Cleaning:** remove dust, mould, algae and chalky residue.
3. **Scraping and sanding:** remove loose or flaking paint.
4. **Crack repair:** widen and fill cracks with flexible filler; use mesh for movement cracks.
5. **Sealer/primer:** apply alkali-resistant sealer on new plaster and a suitable primer on bare surfaces.
6. **Caulking:** seal gaps around window frames, pipes and joints where water can enter.

Painting over peeling or damp surfaces will fail quickly. If there is an active leak, it must be fixed before repainting.

## Paint System for Malaysian Weather

Exterior walls need UV-resistant, flexible and anti-fungal coatings. KL Servis Rumah commonly recommends premium exterior acrylic ranges such as Nippon Paint Weatherbond, Dulux Weathershield or Jotun Jotashield, subject to availability and project specification. The exact product is confirmed during quotation rather than assumed.

A standard system includes:

- One coat of sealer/primer.
- Two coats of weatherproof exterior paint.
- Separate metal primer and topcoat for railings/gutters where included.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Skipping the sealer on new plaster.
- Painting over mould without treating it.
- Using interior paint outdoors.
- Ignoring cracks that reopen every monsoon.
- Applying paint during rain or on a damp surface.
- Choosing colour without testing a sample in sunlight.

## Process

1. Protect plants, floors, windows and fixtures.
2. Wash, scrape and sand surfaces.
3. Repair cracks and defects.
4. Apply sealer/primer.
5. Apply two topcoats with proper drying time.
6. Inspect edges, touch up and clean up.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How often should a Malaysian house be repainted externally?
It depends on exposure and paint quality. A quality exterior system can last several years, while walls in heavy sun or rain may need earlier maintenance.

### Can you paint over efflorescence?
Not directly. The salt deposit should be removed, the source of moisture addressed, and an alkali-resistant sealer applied.

### Is exterior painting only for landed houses?
No. Cluster homes, townhouses, shoplots and low-rise commercial properties may also need exterior painting, subject to access and management rules.
