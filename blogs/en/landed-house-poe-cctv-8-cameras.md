# Landed House PoE CCTV in KL (8 Cameras)

A landed house has a perimeter, and a perimeter is what wireless systems handle worst. Gates, side lanes, back yards and car porches are exactly the places where Wi-Fi weakens and where you need footage that was still recording at 3am. A wired PoE system is built for that.

## Why PoE for a landed home

| | PoE (wired) | Wi-Fi (wireless) |
|---|---|---|
| Power and data | One CAT6 cable per camera | Mains socket per camera + Wi-Fi |
| Recording | 24/7 continuous to local NVR | Usually event clips to cloud |
| Reliability outdoors | Not affected by Wi-Fi range | Drops out at gates and rear boundary |
| Ongoing cost | None mandatory | Cloud plan usually needed |
| Retention | ~1 week on 2TB, expandable | Depends on plan |
| Install effort | Higher — cable routing | Lower |

Continuous recording is the practical difference. Motion-triggered clips routinely miss the two seconds before something happens, which is often the part you need.

## Typical 8-camera layout

| Camera | Position | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Main gate, facing in | Faces and plate numbers at the entry point |
| 2 | Car porch | Vehicles, and the most common approach path |
| 3 | Front door | Anyone at the door, close range |
| 4 | Left side lane | Boundary access |
| 5 | Right side lane | Boundary access |
| 6 | Rear door or back yard | The most common forced entry point |
| 7 | Rear boundary or back gate | Early warning before the yard |
| 8 | Interior — stairs or living hall | Movement inside if the perimeter is passed |

A single-storey terrace often needs 4–6 cameras rather than 8. A semi-D or bungalow with a larger perimeter may need more. The site survey sets the count; we quote what the property needs, not a fixed number.

## Mistakes that cost you footage

- **Mounting cameras too high.** At 4 metres you record the top of everyone's head. Around 2.5–3 metres captures faces.
- **One wide shot instead of one identification shot.** An overview camera tells you something happened; a tighter camera at the gate tells you who.
- **No camera on the NVR itself.** If the recorder is taken, so is the evidence. Keep it out of sight and ideally in view of a camera.
- **Never checking playback.** Test it monthly. A disk that failed six months ago looks identical to a working system on live view.
- **Letting the disk fill without overwrite set correctly** — see [CCTV not recording because storage is full](/problems/cctv-not-recording-storage-full).
- **Ignoring a camera that has gone offline** — see [CCTV not recording](/problems/cctv-not-recording).

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does 2TB store?

Roughly one week of continuous recording across 8 channels at 4MP. Bitrate settings and scene activity move that figure either way. A larger disk extends it.

### Do the cameras work at night?

Yes. 4MP bullet cameras have infrared night vision. Existing porch and boundary lighting improves colour detail, and we check night performance during commissioning.

### Can I view the cameras from overseas?

Yes, through the mobile app on iOS or Android. We test remote viewing on your phone at handover rather than leaving you to set it up.

### Is there a monthly fee?

No. Recording is local to the NVR. Cloud backup is optional if you want it.

### Can this be installed in a house that is already renovated?

Yes. Where ceiling voids are sealed, we use surface trunking or conduit routed along skirting or ceiling lines to keep it tidy.

### What if I want cameras covering the road outside?

Cameras that capture the public road or a neighbour's property raise privacy considerations. We angle to keep coverage on your own boundary wherever possible.

### Can I mix PoE and Wi-Fi cameras?

Yes, where a position has no practical cable route. Wired remains the default for perimeter cameras.
