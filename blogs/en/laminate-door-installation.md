# Laminate Door Installation in KL & Selangor

A laminate door (engineered door with an HDF skin and laminate face) is the usual bedroom and store-room leaf in Malaysian condos. It is lighter than solid meranti, stays more stable in air-conditioning, and gives a flat modern face that matches white or wood-look interiors.

**Direct answer:** KL Servis Rumah supplies and hangs laminate doors from **RM 250**, including fitting to the existing frame where it is sound, hinge work, and lining up the lock.

Choose a [solid timber door](/services/door/solid-timber-door-installation) when you want mass, a refinishable face, or a main-door feel. See the full [door repair and installation](/services/door) range for planing and fire-rated leaves.

## What you get at the starting price

From RM 250 typically covers:

- An HDF-skin engineered leaf with a laminate face, sized to the measured rebate.
- Hanging on the existing frame when the frame is reusable.

A new frame, architrave, glass insert, or a new lockset is quoted on top. Fire-rated openings need a certified fire door, not a laminate bedroom leaf.

## Why laminate doors suit condos

Klang Valley condos are air-conditioned by day and humid when the AC is off. Solid interior timber can swell and scrape. An engineered laminate leaf:

- Moves less across the seasons.
- Wipes clean — useful in kids’ rooms.
- Comes in consistent colours for a whole corridor of bedrooms.

The trade-off: you cannot sand the face like solid timber. A deep knock is a door replacement or a careful filler, not a full refinish.

## When not to use a laminate bedroom door

- **Wet bathrooms** with poor exhaust — edges can swell if water sits on the bottom rail.
- **Fire-rated openings** — kitchen / escape doors that must be a certified set.
- **Main entrance** if you want a solid security leaf and a deep mortice for a [deadbolt or smart lock](/services/locksmith).

If the only problem is scraping after new [flooring](/services/flooring), start with [door planing](/services/door) from RM 120, not a new leaf. Read [door scraping the floor](/problems/door-scraping-floor).

## How we install a laminate door

1. Measure the rebate, thickness and lock height.
2. Confirm colour and whether the existing frame and architrave stay.
3. Trim the leaf if needed (factory doors are often slightly oversize).
4. Hang on three hinges for a full-height leaf.
5. Fit or reuse the lock; test latch and privacy turn.
6. Check clearance over the finished floor.

## Common mistakes

- Buying a 30-inch “standard” door for a condo rebate that is not standard.
- Two hinges on a tall leaf, then blaming the door for sag.
- Planing the laminate edge without resealing, then watching it swell.
- Replacing the leaf when only the hinge screws have stripped the frame.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I keep my old frame and lock?

Usually yes. We measure the lock case. If the new leaf is a different thickness, the latch may need a different strike or a new lock.

### Are laminate doors hollow?

Many engineered doors have a honeycomb or tubular core with a solid lock block. They are not solid timber. That is why they are lighter and cheaper.

### How long does it take?

One or two doors on existing frames are often done in a single visit once the leaves are on site.

### Can you match an existing colour?

We match to common white and wood-look laminates with samples. An exact discontinued colour may not exist.
