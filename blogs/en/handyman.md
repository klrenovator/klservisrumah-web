## When to Call a Handyman

- You do not have the right tools.
- The job needs a second person (e.g. lifting a large TV).
- You want the job done safely and correctly the first time.
- The task is too small to justify a specialist contractor.

Attempting heavy mounting or electrical-adjacent work yourself can be unsafe and end up costing more.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you mount a heavy TV on a plasterboard wall?

Yes, provided the correct anchors are used. For plasterboard, we locate the studs or use suitable plasterboard anchors rated for the weight, and advise on the safest mounting.

### Do you assemble IKEA and Taobao furniture?

Yes. We assemble flat-pack furniture from IKEA, Taobao, and other brands, following the instructions carefully to avoid damage.

### Can you fix a door that is sticking?

Yes. Sticking doors are usually caused by misaligned hinges, swelling in humid weather, or paint build-up. We adjust or plane the door as needed.

### Do you provide the mounting brackets and screws?

We can supply suitable brackets and fixings or use ones you provide. We will advise on the correct type for your wall and TV.
