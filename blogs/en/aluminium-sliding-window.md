## Glass and frame choices that actually matter

**Glass**
- **5mm clear** — the standard infill for typical residential sliding panels.
- **Tempered** — required where panels are large, low to the floor, or in high-traffic areas. Breaks into blunt granules.
- **Laminated** — an interlayer holds the glass together. Better for street-facing windows and noise.
- **Tinted / low-E** — reduces heat gain on west-facing rooms. Useful in KL afternoons, priced higher.

**Frame**
- Powder-coated finish holds up better than anodised in coastal and high-rain conditions.
- Heavier frame sections resist flexing on wide openings; wide spans should not use the thinnest residential profile.
- Ask what the frame section is on the quote, not just the brand name.

## How we install

1. **Measure** the structural opening in three places, check for out-of-square, note the sill fall and wall build-up.
2. **Quote** with frame section, glass type, panel configuration and any masonry work itemised.
3. **Fabricate** — aluminium frames typically take 3–5 working days; glass 5–7 working days.
4. **Remove** the old window carefully. Steel and timber frames often bring plaster with them; we allow for making-good.
5. **Install** — set the frame plumb and square, pack correctly, never force it into a distorted opening.
6. **Seal** — flashing and weather sealant on the outside, backer where the gap is wide, neat internal finish.
7. **Test** — slide, lock, check drainage slots, and water-test before handover.

## Common mistakes

- Ordering from a rough tape measurement. Aluminium and glass cannot be trimmed after fabrication.
- Sealing over the frame drainage slots, so rainwater has nowhere to escape and backs up inside.
- No flashing at the head, so water tracks down behind the frame and stains the internal wall.
- Choosing the thinnest frame profile for a wide opening, then living with flex and rattle.
- Replacing a window when the leak is actually coming from the wall above. Check [wall dampness and efflorescence repair](/services/waterproofing/wall-dampness-and-efflorescence-repair).
- Removing a security grille without a plan to refit or replace it.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does installation take?

Fabrication is typically 3–5 working days for the frame and 5–7 for glass. Installation of a standard residential window is usually completed in one visit.

### Can you match my existing window colour?

Common powder-coat colours are readily available. Exact matching of an aged finish is not always possible; we will show the closest standard option at measurement.

### Do I need tempered glass?

For large panels, panels close to floor level and areas where impact is likely, yes. For a typical small bedroom window, 5mm clear is standard. We advise per opening.

### Can you install mosquito netting or grilles at the same time?

Yes, if specified upfront so the frame is ordered with the right provision. Retrofitting later is possible but less neat.

### Will a new window stop my rain leak?

Only if the leak is coming through the window. If water is entering through the wall, sill or above the frame, that needs waterproofing rather than a new window. We identify the source at measurement.
