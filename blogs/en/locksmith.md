## Mechanical lock vs smart lock

| | Mechanical mortice / deadbolt | Smart lock |
|---|---|---|
| Best for | Simple reliability, tenants, spare rooms | Main door, frequent guests, no-key living |
| Starting install | From RM 220 | From RM 420 |
| Failure mode | Worn cylinder, loose screws, misaligned strike | Flat batteries, warped door, poor Wi-Fi (if used) |
| After a move | Re-key from RM 30 | Delete old fingerprints / codes |

A smart lock on a door that does not latch will fail. We check door alignment before we cut a new mortice.

Brands we commonly fit include **Samsung, Yale, Igloohome, Solity, Häfele, Kaiser and Philips**, matching the lock to the door thickness and existing hole.

## When to replace, re-key, or just cut a key

- **Cut a key** when the lock is sound and you only need spares.
- **Re-key** when you have moved in, lost a key, or a contractor still has the old set — cheaper than a full lock.
- **Replace the lock** when the cylinder is worn, the latch is bent, or you want a different type (deadbolt, smart).
- **Reinforce the frame** when the strike plate is held by short screws in a soft frame — a lock is only as strong as the jamb.

## Emergency lockout

For landed houses in KL and Selangor we can often open the door the same day without drilling, subject to technician availability. We will ask for **proof of ownership** (IC, utility bill, or photo ID that matches the address) before we open anything. For condos, the management office often holds a master or can confirm the occupant — we can coordinate with them.

We do not advertise “silent entry” for third parties. No proof, no opening.

## How a lock job runs

1. **Assess** the door (solid, hollow, metal), existing hole, and what you need (keys, codes, fingerprints).
2. **Quote** the lock, parts, labour, and any door modification.
3. **Remove** the old lock and tidy the door face.
4. **Install** and test. Smart locks: app, fingerprints, codes, mechanical override.
5. **Handover** serial number, warranty card, and a short briefing on batteries and backup keys.

## What we check on site

- Door thickness and stile width — some smart locks need a minimum stile.
- Whether the door is warped or the strike is high (common after humidity or a dropped hinge).
- Existing hole pattern (condo doors often match Solity / Häfele cutouts).
- Need for longer strike screws into the stud.

If the leaf itself is the problem, we hand you to [door installation](/services/door) instead of forcing a new lock into a banana door.

## Common mistakes

- Buying a smart lock from a marketplace without checking door thickness.
- Installing on a door that does not latch, then returning the “faulty” lock.
- Leaving factory admin codes unchanged.
- Replacing the lock but leaving 15 mm screws in the strike plate.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which smart lock is best for a Malaysian condo?

There is no single best brand. Condo doors with a standard hole often take Solity or Häfele with less cutting. Landed main doors often take Yale, Samsung or Igloohome mortice models. The buying guide compares features; we confirm fit on site.

### Can you keep my lock and only cut keys?

Yes, if the cylinder is healthy.

### What if I am locked out of a condo?

Contact management first if they have access. We can still attend; we still need proof you have the right to enter.

### How long does a smart lock install take?

About 30–60 minutes on a matching hole; 1.5–2 hours if we cut a new mortice.
