# Old Condo That Needs a Full Refurbishment

**Question:** I have bought or inherited a 15-year-old condo. Paint is tired, tiles are dated, the kitchen and bathrooms are worn. Where do I start, and what does a full refurbishment actually include?

**Answer:** Start with a room-by-room survey, then fix waterproofing, electrical safety and plumbing before any finishes. Paint, flooring and tiles come second. Kitchen and wardrobes go last so they are not damaged by wet trades. A typical 3-bed programme is 6 to 10 weeks: one week hacking and waterproofing, three weeks tiling, plumbing and electrical, two weeks kitchen and carpentry, one week paint and handover. KL Servis Rumah handles the JMB deposit, permits, quiet hours, lift protection and rubbish removal as standard.

This is a **whole-unit planning** article, not a single-trade how-to. Individual problems such as [leaking bathroom](/problems/leaking-bathroom) or [yellowing white walls](/problems/yellowing-white-walls) still need their own diagnosis inside the programme.

## Why 15-Year Condos Feel Exhausted

- Decades of tenant or owner wear.
- Fixtures past service life.
- Aged waterproofing membranes in bathrooms and on the balcony.
- Original-build materials that were never premium.

## Suggested Sequence

1. Survey and itemised scope — you approve which lines proceed.
2. Waterproofing, electrical safety and plumbing first.
3. Tiles, flooring and wall finishes second.
4. Kitchen and wardrobes last.

Typical 3-bed schedule: 6 to 10 weeks as above. After the site visit a detailed itemised quote is issued with each scope line priced separately.
