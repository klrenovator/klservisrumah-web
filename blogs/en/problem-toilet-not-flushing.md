# Toilet Not Flushing Properly: Weak Flush or Keeps Running

**Question:** My toilet flushes weakly, does not clear the bowl in one press, or keeps running after I flush. Do I repair the cistern, or replace the whole suite?

**Answer:** A weak flush or a cistern that never stops is almost always a worn flapper or flush mechanism, clogged rim jets around the bowl edge, a partial block in the S-trap, or a faulty fill valve. Replacing the flapper or fill valve is a short job — published guidance is RM 150 to RM 250 including parts. Replace the whole suite when the ceramic is cracked or you have replaced parts repeatedly in the last two years.

This is a **cistern and flush-performance** problem. If the bowl is rising and will not go down, that is a blockage — see [blocked drain toilet](/problems/blocked-drain-toilet). If other fixtures are also slow, see [clogged drain](/problems/clogged-drain).

## Why Toilets Flush Badly in Malaysian Homes

- **Worn flapper or dual-flush cartridge** — the seal no longer holds water in the cistern, so the flush volume drops and the cistern keeps refilling.
- **Clogged rim jets** — hard water and scale choke the holes under the rim, so water does not wash the bowl from all sides.
- **Partial S-trap or waste-line block** — enough water leaves to look like a flush, not enough to carry solids.
- **Faulty fill valve (float assembly)** — the cistern overfills into the overflow or never reaches the correct level.

## Weak Flush vs Keeps Running

| Symptom | Likely part |
|---|---|
| Bowl does not clear in one press, cistern feels light | Low water in cistern / flapper leaking |
| Water trickles into the bowl between flushes | Flapper or flush-valve seal |
| Weak wash from the rim all around | Scaled rim jets |
| Needs two or three flushes, other drains fine | Partial S-trap block |
| Cistern never stops hissing | Fill valve |

## What You Can Check

1. Lift the cistern lid and watch one flush. If the water level is low before you press, the flapper is leaking.
2. Listen after the flush. A continuous hiss is the fill valve.
3. Wipe the rim jets with vinegar on a cloth if they are visibly furred — do not pour neat acid into the cistern.
4. Do not keep flushing a bowl that is already high — that is a block, not a weak mechanism.

## What a Professional Repair Involves

1. Replace the flapper, fill valve or dual-flush cartridge.
2. Descale the rim jets.
3. Snake the S-trap and waste line if the flush volume is fine but the bowl still does not clear.
4. Fit a full cistern rebuild kit on old suites, or replace the suite when the ceramic is cracked.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I repair or replace an old toilet?
If the ceramic is cracked, or parts have been replaced several times in two years, replacing the suite (RM 550 to RM 1,800 installed) is usually more cost-effective.

### Why does the toilet keep running?
Almost always a worn flapper or fill-valve seal. Both are quick fixes at RM 150 to RM 250 including parts.
