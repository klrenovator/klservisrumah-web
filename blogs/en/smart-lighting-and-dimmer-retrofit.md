## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Installing a dimmer on a non-dimmable LED

- Assuming every switch box has a neutral

- Overloading a smart module

- Making all basic lighting depend on one phone account


Do not bypass structural, electrical, waterproofing or product requirements to save preparation time. Stop work if hidden moisture, unsafe wiring, weak support or moving cracks are discovered; diagnose these conditions before covering them.

## Frequently Asked Questions


### Will any LED work with a smart dimmer?
No. The lamp and driver must explicitly support dimming and the dimmer type and load range must match.


### Do I need a hub?
Wi-Fi devices may not, while Zigbee and other systems commonly do. A hub can improve multi-device reliability.


### What happens after a power cut?
Behaviour is model-specific. Commissioning should test reconnection and the chosen power-restoration state.
