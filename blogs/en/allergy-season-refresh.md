This guide explains the [Allergy Season Refresh](/services/deep-cleaning/allergy-season-refresh) scope under [deep cleaning services](/services/deep-cleaning). It reduces allergens in fabrics and on surfaces; it treats the home environment, not the medical condition, so it complements — never replaces — a doctor's advice.

## When This Service Is the Right Choice

Malaysia has no four seasons, but allergens peak in familiar patterns. Haze periods push fine particles indoors, the monsoon raises humidity that dust mites thrive in, and air-conditioned bedrooms stay closed up for months. Book an allergy refresh when:

- Someone sneezes or gets sinus irritation mainly at home, especially in bed or on the sofa.
- Dust returns to surfaces within days of normal wiping.
- The haze season has ended and fine particulate has settled into fabrics.
- Mattresses, sofas and curtains have never been professionally treated.

If the trigger was an illness rather than allergens, the [Post-Illness Disinfection Clean](/services/deep-cleaning/post-illness-disinfection-clean) is the appropriate scope instead.

## Why Ordinary Vacuuming Is Not Enough

A standard vacuum without HEPA filtration picks up visible debris but blows the finest particles — the ones that penetrate deep into airways — straight back into the room through its exhaust. A HEPA-filter machine traps those fine particles inside. Dust mites, meanwhile, live inside mattresses and upholstered furniture, not on top of them, which is why the refresh combines thorough HEPA vacuuming with a dust-mite treatment applied to the fabric.

## What Should Be Checked Before Work

- **Priority rooms** — usually bedrooms first, then the living room sofa area.
- **Fabric types and condition** — delicate curtains, leather and loose covers are handled differently.
- **Existing stains or damage** on mattresses and sofas, recorded before treatment.
- **Washable items** — bed linen and removable covers are best hot-washed at home before the visit.
- **Occupants and pets** — plan which rooms are in use while others are treated and dried.
- **Ventilation and drying time** — treated fabrics need airflow before evening use.

## Typical Work Process

1. **Confirm priority rooms and pre-existing fabric condition.**
2. **HEPA-vacuum top-down** — curtains, pelmets, fans and surfaces — so fine dust is captured, not spread.
3. **Deep HEPA-vacuum mattresses and sofas**, including seams, tufts and undersides where accessible.
4. **Apply dust-mite treatment** to mattresses, sofas and curtains according to product directions.
5. **Damp-wipe hard surfaces**, then vacuum and mop floors last.
6. **Ventilate treated rooms** and agree re-use timing with the occupant.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Dry-dusting with a feather duster, which simply redistributes fine particles.
- Treating only the bed and ignoring curtains and the sofa, which hold the same allergens.
- Over-wetting fabrics so they stay damp in humid weather — damp fabric encourages mould, the opposite of the goal.
- Expecting one visit to solve a daily allergy permanently; fabrics re-accumulate allergens, so a quarterly or haze-linked rhythm works better.
- Skipping the hot wash of bedding — home laundering and the visit work as a pair.

## Safety and Ongoing Control

Products are used per label and rooms are aired before normal use; mention infants, asthma or sensitive skin at booking. Between visits, the highest-impact habits are washing bedding weekly on a hot cycle, using mattress and pillow protectors, keeping indoor humidity down with ventilation or dehumidification, and vacuuming with a HEPA machine rather than sweeping dry. If symptoms persist despite a clean environment, see a doctor — persistent allergy can have triggers outside the home.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How often should an allergy refresh be done?
A practical rhythm for most homes is quarterly, or after each haze season. Homes with strong sensitivities may prefer a tighter schedule; the quotation can price recurring visits.

### Is it safe for babies and people with asthma?
Yes, when rooms are ventilated and fabrics dry before use. Declare sensitive occupants when booking so product use and room order are planned around them.

### Does it remove pet dander?
HEPA vacuuming removes dander sitting on and in treated fabrics, which usually brings noticeable relief. Dander returns as long as pets live in the home, which is why a recurring schedule matters more than any single visit.
