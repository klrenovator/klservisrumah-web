# Autogate Not Closing or Motor Running Continuously

**Question:** My auto gate opens normally but will not close, or the motor keeps running without stopping. Sometimes it reverses halfway. Is the motor dying?

**Answer:** Not necessarily — and a gate that opens but will not close is usually a control or safety issue rather than a worn-out motor. The four most common causes are a faulty limit switch that no longer tells the motor where to stop, a blocked or misaligned obstruction sensor that keeps tripping the safety reverse, a failing motor capacitor that cannot drive the gate closed, or a remote or control-board fault sending the wrong signal. The fix is to test and replace the faulty component — often a small, inexpensive part — rather than replace the whole motor.

## Why an Autogate Stops Closing

| Cause | Typical clue |
|---|---|
| Faulty limit switch | Motor runs past the stop point, or never stops |
| Blocked obstruction sensor | Gate reverses as if something is in the way |
| Motor capacitor failure | Motor hums but lacks power to close |
| Remote or board fault | Gate ignores the close command intermittently |
| Misaligned safety beam | Gate reverses at the same point every time |

## What to Check Before You Call

1. **Look at the safety sensors** (photobeams) on either side of the gate. Dirt, spider webs or a knocked-out alignment will make the gate refuse to close.
2. **Listen to the motor.** A hum without movement, or slow travel, points to the capacitor.
3. **Try the manual release** so the gate can be opened and closed by hand while the fault is fixed — important for security.
4. **Note whether it happens every time or only sometimes** — intermittent faults often point to a board or remote.

## What a Technician Does

An autogate technician will typically:

- Test and replace the limit switch so the motor stops at the correct points.
- Clean and realign the safety sensors and photobeams.
- Test and replace the motor capacitor where the motor is weak.
- Reprogram or replace the control board if it is sending faulty signals.
- Service the motor and track, and check the hinges and wheels run freely.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you repair existing autogate motors?
Yes. We repair and service common autogate brands, including Autogate 2000, Nice and FAAC, plus many others.

### Do you install battery backup?
Yes. We recommend and install battery backup so the gate keeps operating during power outages.

### Why does my gate reverse when nothing is there?
Usually the safety sensors or photobeams are dirty, misaligned or failing, making the gate think something is in the way.

### Is it dangerous if the motor keeps running?
Yes — a motor that does not stop can overheat and damage itself or strain the gate. It should be switched off and checked promptly.

### Should I replace the motor or just the part?
Most "not closing" faults are a limit switch, sensor or capacitor — far cheaper than a motor. The motor is only replaced when it has genuinely failed.
