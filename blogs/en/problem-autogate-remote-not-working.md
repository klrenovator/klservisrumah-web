# Auto Gate Remote Not Working? Quick Checks and Fixes

**Question:** Why is my auto gate remote not opening or closing the gate?

**Answer:** The most common cause is a flat remote battery or a remote that needs reprogramming. If the wall button works but the remote does not, the issue is usually the remote, battery, antenna or receiver. If neither remote nor wall button works, the control board, motor, power supply or safety sensors may need repair.

## Start With These Checks

| Sign | Likely cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Remote LED is dim/off | Flat remote battery | Replace battery |
| Works close but not far | Antenna or range issue | Check antenna position |
| One remote fails, another works | Faulty remote | Reprogram or replace |
| No remote works but wall button OK | Receiver/aerial issue | Check receiver and wiring |
| Nothing works at all | Power, board or motor fault | Check breaker and call technician |
| Gate starts then reverses | Obstruction or safety sensor | Clear track and check sensors |

## Replace the Remote Battery

Use the correct battery type, usually a small lithium cell. After replacing it, test from the normal distance and close to the control panel. If the remote has been dropped or wet, buttons or internal contacts may be damaged.

## Reprogramming

Some remotes lose their code after a battery change, power surge or control-board reset. A new spare remote also needs to be learned by the receiver. Keep all existing remotes available when adding a new one, as some systems clear memory before relearning.

## Range and Antenna Problems

If the remote only works very close to the gate:

- Check that the receiver antenna is upright and not trapped in metal.
- Replace the remote battery.
- Check for new metal structures, vehicles or devices near the receiver.
- Avoid repeatedly pressing the button from far away; it can desync some systems.

## When the Motor or Board Is Involved

If the wall switch also fails, check:

- The main power supply and MCB/breaker.
- Whether the autogate motor is humming but not moving.
- Whether the gate is obstructed or off its track.
- Whether the manual release is engaged.
- Whether there was a recent lightning surge or flood.

Do not force the gate open or closed if the motor is locked; use the manual release according to the manufacturer's method.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why does my remote work only sometimes?
Usually battery, range or receiver antenna issues. Interference from nearby metal or electronics can also reduce reliability.

### Can I buy a universal autogate remote?
Some systems can accept compatible remotes, but frequency and coding must match. The receiver may need reprogramming.

### What if the gate opens but does not close?
Check for obstructions, safety sensors and the close-limit setting. If it reverses for no visible reason, a technician should inspect the board and sensors.
