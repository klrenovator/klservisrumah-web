# Grout Still Stained After a Deep Clean: What Next

**Question:** I paid for a professional deep clean, but the grout lines in my bathroom and kitchen are still dark and discoloured. Is there any way to get them clean?

**Answer:** Yes — but the method depends on why the grout is stained. Grout is porous, and some stains penetrate deep into it. Deep-set mold and mineral deposits, hard-water staining, or a previous poor seal all mean the discolouration is inside the grout, not on its surface, so no amount of surface scrubbing will lift it. The two durable answers are professional cleaning with steam and a grout cleaner followed by a penetrating sealer, or — where the grout is badly aged — re-grouting or colour sealing. Both restore a clean, even finish; the choice is about how far the damage goes.

## Why Grout Stays Stained

| Cause | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| Deep-set mold | Black or green staining in shower and basin areas |
| Hard-water mineral deposits | Chalky white or grey deposits |
| Old or porous grout | Stains soak in and never fully lift |
| Failed previous seal | Grout re-stains quickly after cleaning |
| Ground-in dirt in kitchens | Grease-darkened lines near cooking areas |

## Clean vs Re-Grout vs Colour Seal

| Method | When to use | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Deep clean + steamer + sealer | Surface and moderate staining | Restores original grout colour |
| Colour sealing | Evenly dark, intact grout | Uniform new colour, sealed |
| Re-grouting | Cracked, crumbling or badly stained grout | Brand-new grout lines |
| Combination | Mixed damage across a bathroom | Clean, re-seal, re-grout where needed |

For grout that is still structurally sound, [colour sealing](/services/tiling/tile-repair-and-re-grouting) is often the fastest path to an even finish. For cracked or crumbling grout, removal and re-grouting is the only lasting fix — see [cracked tile grout](/problems/cracked-tile-grout).

## What Each Approach Involves

### Deep Clean + Seal
A grout-specific cleaner and steam lift surface staining, then a penetrating sealer is applied so future staining stays on the surface and wipes off.

### Colour Sealing
The grout is cleaned, a pigmented sealant is brushed over the lines, and it is left to cure. This recolours and seals in one step and typically lasts two to four years with normal care.

### Re-Grouting
The old grout is raked out, the joints cleaned, and fresh grout applied and finished. This is the correct fix where grout is cracked or falling out, because sealant cannot repair a failing joint.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you restore grout colour without re-grouting?
Often yes. Professional cleaning plus colour sealing restores an even colour where the grout is still intact. Full re-grouting is only needed for cracked or crumbling grout.

### How long does colour sealing last?
Typically two to four years, depending on usage and the cleaning products used.

### Why did my grout stain again after a deep clean?
The grout was likely porous and unsealed, so staining soaked back in. A penetrating sealer after cleaning is what prevents rapid re-staining.

### Is re-grouting a big job?
For a single bathroom it is usually a one-to-two day job including removal, re-grouting and curing. The area must be kept dry while the new grout sets.

### Can I just bleach the grout?
Bleach lightens surface mold but does not remove mineral deposits or reseal the grout, and heavy use can weaken the joint. Professional cleaning and sealing lasts far longer.
