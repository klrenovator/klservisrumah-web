# Condo Move-Out Deep Clean in KL: RM 420 Checklist

Moving out of a rented condo in Kuala Lumpur usually ends with a walkthrough, a checklist and a conversation about the deposit. A move-out clean is aimed squarely at that walkthrough — the kitchen, bathrooms, window tracks and skirting that get looked at closely.

**Direct answer:** KL Servis Rumah performs move-out deep cleans on 1,000 sqft condos from **RM 420** across Kuala Lumpur and Selangor. A typical unit takes **2 cleaners for 4–6 hours**, and the work is covered by a **24-hour re-clean guarantee**.

## When to book it

- After your belongings are out. An empty unit cleans faster and better.
- Two to three days before the handover inspection, so there is time for a re-clean if the agent flags something.
- Before the electricity or water is disconnected — cleaning needs both.
- At month-end, book early. That is the busiest window in Klang Valley.

## How we work through the unit

1. **Scope check** — we walk the unit, note problem areas and confirm against the landlord checklist if you have one.
2. **Top-down** — fans, lights, high shelving, curtain rails first, so falling dust is captured later.
3. **Kitchen detail** — cabinets emptied and wiped inside, appliances degreased inside and out.
4. **Bathroom detail** — descaling needs dwell time, so bathrooms are started early and finished late.
5. **Windows, tracks and skirting** — the parts inspections always reach.
6. **Floors last** — vacuum, then mop or treat according to surface.
7. **Walkthrough** — you or your agent confirms, and we note anything to return for.

## Condo access on moving day

- Book the service lift for both the movers and, if needed, cleaning equipment.
- Confirm the management office's working hours; some buildings restrict contractor access on Sundays and public holidays.
- Register the cleaning team at the guardhouse in advance.
- Confirm that water and electricity remain connected on cleaning day.
- Arrange access if you have already handed back your card — a key handover or your agent being present.

## Common mistakes

- **Cleaning before the movers come.** Furniture removal marks floors and walls all over again.
- **Booking a regular clean.** Inspections check inside the oven, not just the counters.
- **Leaving mould until the last day.** Treatment needs dwell time; heavily mouldy silicone may need replacing, not cleaning. Related: [mould in the bathroom after cleaning](/problems/mold-in-bathroom-after-cleaning).
- **Expecting stained grout to look new.** Some grout is permanently discoloured — see [stubborn grout stains after a deep clean](/problems/stubborn-grout-stains-after-deep-clean).
- **Forgetting the balcony drain.** It is a common inspection point and a common miss.
- **Not accounting for renovation residue.** If the unit was recently renovated, book [post-renovation cleaning](/services/post-renovation-cleaning) instead.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does a move-out clean take?

A typical empty 1,000 sqft condo takes 2 cleaners about 4–6 hours. A furnished or heavily soiled unit takes longer, and we say so before starting.

### Will this guarantee my deposit is returned?

No cleaning company can guarantee a landlord's decision. We clean to the points landlords check and re-clean any flagged area within 24 hours.

### Can you clean if the unit still has furniture?

Yes, but access is limited and the result behind and under heavy items will be affected. Cleaning after removal is always better.

### Do you dispose of leftover rubbish?

Normal bagging and disposal to the building's bin area is included. Bulk items, mattresses and renovation debris are quoted separately.

### Can the agent be present at handover?

Yes. Many clients schedule the clean so the final walkthrough happens right after, which makes any re-clean easy to arrange.
