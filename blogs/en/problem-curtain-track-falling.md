# Curtain Track Coming Loose from the Ceiling

**Question:** My curtain track is drooping, the screws have pulled out of the ceiling, or the rail has partly fallen. Can it just be screwed back into the same holes?

**Answer:** Not into the same stripped holes. Tracks fail when screws were driven into plasterboard without anchors, when the curtain is heavier than the fixings, when water has softened the board, or when old rubber plugs have crumbled. The repair is to remove the old fixings, patch the plaster, then remount into a joist or with heavy-duty toggle bolts and stainless screws. Heavy blackout curtains also need intermediate wall brackets.

This is a **curtain-track fixing** job, not a failing ceiling structure. If the board around the track is soft or stained, treat the leak first — see [sagging plaster ceiling](/problems/sagging-plaster-ceiling).

## Why Tracks Pull Out

- Screws in plasterboard with no anchors.
- Track overloaded with lined or blackout fabric.
- Water damage weakening the plasterboard.
- Old rubber wall plugs disintegrating.

## Plasterboard or Concrete?

| Ceiling | Typical fixing |
|---|---|
| Plasterboard / gypsum | Heavy-duty toggle or spring anchors rated well above the curtain weight |
| Concrete slab | Hammer-drilled plated concrete anchors — about 30 minutes per window |
| Damaged plaster around old holes | Patch, prime, cure, then remount — often same day plus a 24-hour paint cure |

For blackout curtains, add intermediate wall brackets so the ceiling is not carrying the whole load.

## What the Job Involves

1. Remove old fixings and patch the plaster.
2. Locate the ceiling joist or set heavy-duty toggles.
3. Reinstall the track with proper anchors and stainless screws.
4. Add support wall brackets for extra-heavy curtains.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do you mount a track on plasterboard?
Heavy-duty toggle bolts or spring anchors rated for at least 4× the curtain weight. Blackout curtains also get intermediate wall brackets.

### Can you install on a concrete ceiling?
Yes — hammer-drill and plated concrete anchors. About 30 minutes per window.

### What if the ceiling is already damaged?
Patch the plaster, prime and let it cure, then install the new track — usually same-day with a 24-hour paint cure between steps.
