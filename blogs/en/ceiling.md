## Common Ceiling Problems in Malaysian Homes

- **Water stains and leaks** — after a roof or pipe leak.
- **Sagging plaster** — weakened ceiling panels that bulge downward.
- **Cracks at joints** — along the seams between plasterboard panels.
- **Mould and condensation** — black spots from humidity and poor ventilation.
- **Old or dated design** — wanting a modern flat or L-box ceiling.

If your ceiling is leaking after rain, the source is often the roof. See our [ceiling leak after rain guide](/problems/ceiling-leak-after-rain) to understand what may be happening before you call a repair team.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does ceiling work cost in KL & Selangor?

Plaster ceiling installation starts from RM 10 per sq ft, water-damaged ceiling repair from RM 220, and gypsum partition walls from RM 11 per sq ft. Final pricing depends on area, design, and site condition.

### Why is my ceiling sagging?

Sagging usually means the plasterboard has become water-damaged or the ceiling framework has failed. It needs inspection — and if the ceiling is heavy or sagging significantly, professional repair is important for safety.

### Can you repair a water-stained ceiling?

Yes, but we also need to find and fix the source of the water first, or the stain will return. See our guide on [water leaking from ceilings](/problems/ceiling-leak-after-rain).

### Do you install L-box ceilings with concealed lighting?

Yes. L-box and tiered designs with concealed light troughs are a popular feature we install as part of plaster ceiling work.
