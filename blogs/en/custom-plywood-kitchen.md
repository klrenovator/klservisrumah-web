# Custom Plywood Kitchen Cabinets in KL & Selangor

A custom plywood kitchen is built to the actual room: the wall that is 20 mm out of plumb, the beam that drops over the fridge, the pipe chase beside the sink. Instead of forcing factory box widths into the space, the carcass is cut, edged and assembled to fit.

**Direct answer:** KL Servis Rumah builds custom plywood kitchens from **RM 11,000**, with moisture-resistant plywood carcasses, soft-close hardware, custom doors, and full-height pantry units where the ceiling allows.

This is the better path when a [modular 10 ft L-shape](/services/kitchen-cabinet/modular-kitchen-cabinet-10ft-l-shape) would leave awkward gaps or cannot reach the ceiling.

## What a custom plywood kitchen includes

A typical custom quote covers:

- Site measurement of every wall, ceiling drop, window and service point.
- A plywood carcass cut to those dimensions (not a catalogue module width).
- Soft-close hinges and runners — Häfele or Blum on the quoted spec, or your supplied hardware.
- Custom door and drawer fronts (laminate, acrylic, spray paint, or a mix).
- Full-height pantry or tall units scribed to the ceiling.
- Installation, levelling, scribing and hardware tuning.

Countertops are usually a separate line — [quartz or granite](/services/kitchen-cabinet/quartz-granite-countertop) from RM 220 / linier ft — because stone is templated after the boxes are fixed.

## Why plywood instead of particleboard in KL

Malaysian kitchens sit in high humidity. Plywood (layered veneer) holds screws better, stays more stable when a sink leaks for a weekend, and can be repaired. Particleboard and cheap MDF swell at the sink base and at any unbanded edge.

That does not mean every plywood kitchen is automatically better. The board grade, edge banding, and how the sink base is detailed matter more than the word “plywood” on a quotation. Ask for:

- Moisture-resistant plywood for sink, hob and dishwasher bays.
- Full edge banding on every exposed edge.
- A removable sink-base back or service void so a plumber can reach the trap later.
- Adjustable legs and a plinth that can be taken off for cleaning.

## When custom plywood is worth the extra cost

Choose custom when you need any of the following:

- Full-height storage to a sloped or beamed ceiling.
- A run that must wrap a structural column or gas riser.
- An extra-deep or extra-shallow counter because the walkway is tight.
- A pantry that hides a washer, water heater cupboard, or broom slot.
- A landed-house kitchen where walls were never square after renovation.

If the room is a standard condo L-shape and you mainly want new doors, start with modular or a [doors-and-hardware makeover](/services/kitchen-cabinet) instead.

## Design choices that affect daily use

- **Drawer banks vs cupboards** — drawers waste less of the back of a deep base unit.
- **Pantry internals** — roll-out trays beat a single deep shelf you cannot reach.
- **Corner** — a blind corner wastes space; a carousel or magic-corner only helps if the opening is wide enough.
- **Tall unit next to the hob** — leave a heat gap and a filler, or the door finish will cook.
- **Under-cabinet lighting** — plan the [electrical](/services/electrical) point before the boxes go in.

Misaligned doors after a few months are often humidity plus weak hinges, not “the wood moved a little”. See [kitchen cabinet door misaligned](/problems/kitchen-cabinet-door-misaligned).

## How a custom kitchen is built

1. **Site visit and brief** — measure, photograph, and list what you store (pots, rice, mixer, kids’ plates).
2. **Design and quotation** — itemised carcass, doors, hardware, internals, and related trades.
3. **Workshop manufacture** — typically **3–4 weeks** after design lock: cut, edge, assemble, finish doors.
4. **Demolition and wall prep** — old kitchen out; tiling or waterproofing finished first if they are in scope.
5. **Install** — boxes are set, scribed, screwed to wall plugs (not just silicone), then doors hung and adjusted.
6. **Countertop and handover** — stone is templated on the finished boxes, then fitted, sealed and cleaned.

End-to-end time for a full custom kitchen is usually **6 to 10 weeks**, same family of timeline as a larger [kitchen cabinet](/services/kitchen-cabinet) project.

## Common mistakes with “custom” kitchens

- Calling painted particleboard “custom plywood”.
- Building boxes to a floor plan instead of the measured wall.
- Fixing the carcass only to tiles with silicone, then blaming the hinges when it drops.
- Installing before the [splashback tiles](/services/tiling) are finished.
- Forgetting a service void behind the sink.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is plywood always better than modular melamine?

Plywood is stronger and more repairable. A good modular melamine kitchen with proper banding and Blum hardware can still be the right choice for a standard room. Custom plywood wins on fit and long-term screw-holding.

### Can I supply my own handles or imported doors?

Yes. If you supply hardware, the hardware itself is outside our warranty, but workmanship on the assembly still stands. Confirm hole centres before the doors are drilled.

### Do you build around an existing stone top?

Sometimes. If the top is sound and the new boxes can be built to its height and L-corner, we can reuse it. Many older tops are out of level; we will tell you on site.

### How long is manufacturing?

Usually 3–4 weeks after you approve the drawing, then 1–2 days on site for the cabinet install.

### Do you work in landed houses and condos?

Yes, across Kuala Lumpur and Selangor. Condo jobs follow management hours, lift protection and debris rules.
