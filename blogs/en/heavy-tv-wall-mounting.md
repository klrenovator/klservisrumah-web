# Heavy TV Wall Mounting in KL & Selangor

Question: How do I mount a heavy TV on the wall safely?

Answer: Use a wall mount rated for the TV's weight, fix it into solid brick, concrete, or a stud (not just plasterboard), keep the TV level, and secure all cables. For large TVs, professional mounting is safer.

- **Safety** — a 40–70-inch TV falling off the wall can cause serious injury and damage.
- **Correct brackets** — the mount must be rated for the TV's size and weight.
- **Right fixings** — the correct anchors for brick, concrete, or plasterboard.

## Common Questions About TV Mounting

### Can any TV be wall-mounted?

Most TVs are wall-mountable, but you should check that the back of the TV has VESA mounting holes and that the wall can support the weight. Very old or very large TVs may need special consideration.

### Can you mount a TV on a plasterboard wall?

Yes, if the correct anchors are used or the TV is fixed into a stud. We will assess the wall and use the safest method.

### What height should a TV be mounted?

As a general guide, the centre of the screen should be at eye level when seated — usually around 42–48 inches from the floor. We will advise based on your room and viewing position.

### Do you hide the cables?

Where feasible, yes. We can organise cables neatly or, for a concealed run, the wall may need a channel chased and repainted (separate work).
