A floor is the hardest-working surface in a home. If it is laid over an unlevel base or with the wrong adhesive, tiles crack, hollow, or lift within a year. This guide explains what a professional floor tiling job includes and what affects the cost.

## The Floor Tiling Process

1. **Substrate inspection** — the installer checks the concrete or old screed for level, cracks, and moisture. Any significant unevenness is corrected before tiling begins.
2. **Levelling** — where needed, self-levelling compound is poured to create a flat base. This matters most for large-format tiles, which show even small slopes.
3. **Layout planning** — tiles are dry-laid across the room to plan the starting point and cuts. Cuts are placed along the least visible edges, and the layout is checked for awkward thin strips.
4. **Adhesive application** — the correct adhesive (based on tile type and size) is applied with a notched trowel. Large and heavy tiles may need back-buttering — adhesive on both floor and tile back.
5. **Tile fixing** — tiles are placed and tapped level, with spacers for even joints. For large-format slabs, levelling clips keep the surface lippage-free.
6. **Curing** — the floor is left to cure for the adhesive manufacturer's recommended time before grouting and foot traffic.
7. **Grouting** — grout is applied, joints are finished, and excess is cleaned. A sealer is applied where the tile type needs it.
8. **Final inspection** — every tile is checked for hollow spots, lippage, and uniform joints.

## Choosing the Right Floor Tile

- **Porcelain** — the best all-rounder for Malaysian homes: hard, low water absorption, and available in wood-look, stone-look, and plain finishes.
- **Homogeneous** — a dense tile with the colour running through the body; ideal for high-traffic areas and shops.
- **Ceramic** — a budget-friendly option for bedrooms and low-traffic rooms, but softer than porcelain.
- **Natural stone** — marble and granite add a premium look but need sealing and careful maintenance.
- **Large-format slabs (600×1200mm, 800×1800mm)** — fewer joints and a modern, seamless look; requires a very flat substrate and specialist handling.

**Size guide:** 600×600mm suits most rooms; 800×800mm and larger read as more modern in open-plan spaces; mosaics and small tiles are for feature areas, not whole floors.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does it cost to tile a floor in Malaysia?

Installation starts from RM 14 per square foot for standard tiles. For a 300 sq ft living room, labour is roughly RM 4,200 and up, excluding tiles. Large-format slabs and natural stone cost more.

### How long does a floor tiling job take?

A typical living room takes 2–4 days: preparation and levelling on day one, tiling over one to two days, then grouting and finishing. Adhesive and grout curing time is included.

### Can new tiles be laid over existing tiles?

Sometimes, but it is usually not recommended. Tiling over old tiles raises the floor height, can cause door clearance problems, and the old surface must be sound and clean. Removing old tiles is the safer, more durable route in most cases.

### Why do some floor tiles go hollow?

Hollow tiles usually mean insufficient adhesive coverage or a base that moved. Tapping a hollow-sounding tile is a sign the bond has failed. See our guide on [hollow floor tiles](/problems/loose-hollow-tiles) for what to do.
