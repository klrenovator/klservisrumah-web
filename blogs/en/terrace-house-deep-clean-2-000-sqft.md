# Terrace House Deep Clean in KL & Selangor (2,000 sqft)

A deep clean on a double-storey terrace house is a bigger job than the floor area suggests. There are two full bathroom sets, a staircase, usually a wet kitchen with years of accumulated grease, a garage, and outdoor areas that a condo simply does not have.

**Direct answer:** KL Servis Rumah deep cleans 2,000 sqft double-storey terrace houses from **RM 650** across Kuala Lumpur and Selangor. A typical house needs **3 cleaners for 6–8 hours**, and the work carries a **24-hour re-clean guarantee**.

## When to book

- **Moving in** — before your furniture arrives, while the house is empty and every surface is reachable.
- **Moving out** — after the movers leave, ahead of the handover walkthrough.
- **After a long tenancy** — when regular cleaning has not touched the wet kitchen or window tracks for years.
- **Before festive season** — Raya, Chinese New Year, Deepavali or Christmas, when the whole house is on show.
- **Reactivating an empty house** — a property that has been vacant for months needs mould treatment as much as dusting.

For ongoing upkeep after the reset, see [bi-weekly terrace house cleaning](/services/cleaning/bi-weekly-terrace-house-cleaning).

## What is covered

**Ground floor**
- Living, dining and any study or guest room
- Kitchen: inside cabinets, hob, oven, extractor exterior, fridge, sink
- Wet kitchen: degreasing of walls, hob area, sink and floor
- Downstairs bathroom in full, including descaling and grout scrubbing
- Skirting, doors, door frames, grilles and window tracks

**Staircase and first floor**
- Treads, risers, handrail, balustrade and the corners that collect dust
- Bedrooms: inside wardrobes, sills, skirting, ceiling fan blades, light fixtures
- Upstairs bathrooms in full
- Window interiors, tracks, grilles and reachable frames

**Outdoor and service areas**
- Porch and car park sweep and wash-down of reachable surfaces
- Garage floor sweep, cobwebs and clearing of debris
- Back yard, drying area and outdoor drainage points
- Gate area and entrance

**Not included as standard:** exterior glass at height, roof and gutter work, pressure washing of driveways, and renovation residue. For renovation residue, book [post-renovation cleaning](/services/post-renovation-cleaning/terrace-house-post-renovation-clean-2-000-sqft).

## How we sequence a two-storey house

1. **Walkthrough and scope** — note problem areas: mould, grease, stained grout, blocked outdoor drains.
2. **Upstairs first, top-down** — fans and lights, then wardrobes, sills, bathrooms.
3. **Staircase** — done after upstairs so falling dust is captured once.
4. **Ground floor** — living areas, then the main kitchen.
5. **Wet kitchen** — usually the longest single zone; degreasing needs dwell time.
6. **Outdoor** — porch, garage, back yard, drainage points.
7. **Floors last, then final walkthrough** with you.

## Common mistakes

- **Underestimating the wet kitchen.** In many terrace houses it needs more time than both bathrooms combined.
- **Booking on the same day as the movers.** Cleaning and moving in the same house at the same time undoes both.
- **Skipping outdoor drains.** Blocked drainage causes damp walls that no amount of cleaning fixes. See [wall dampness and efflorescence repair](/services/waterproofing/wall-dampness-and-efflorescence-repair).
- **Expecting mould to stay away.** Recurrence within days points to ventilation or waterproofing. See [mould in the bathroom after cleaning](/problems/mold-in-bathroom-after-cleaning).
- **Deep cleaning right after renovation.** Cement dust and grout haze need [post-renovation cleaning](/services/post-renovation-cleaning) — see [construction dust that keeps returning](/problems/construction-dust-returning).
- **Assuming stained grout will look new.** Some staining is permanent. See [stubborn grout stains after a deep clean](/problems/stubborn-grout-stains-after-deep-clean).

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does it take?

A typical 2,000 sqft double-storey house takes 3 cleaners about 6–8 hours. Larger or heavily soiled homes take longer, and we say so before starting.

### Do you clean the garage and porch?

Sweeping, cobweb removal and cleaning of reachable surfaces are included. Pressure washing of driveways and heavily stained outdoor tiles is a separate service.

### Should the house be empty?

For move-in and move-out cleans, yes — it is faster and the result is better. For occupied homes we work around furniture and move what is safe to move.

### Can you do a deep clean on the wet kitchen only?

Yes, single-zone deep cleans can be quoted. Send photos so we can scope the grease level accurately.

### Do you treat mould on ceilings and walls?

We apply anti-fungal treatment to affected surfaces. If the cause is a leak or lack of ventilation, mould returns — that needs a repair, not a clean.
