## How a Professional Tiling Job Is Done

1. **Substrate assessment** — the installer checks the concrete or screed for level, cracks, and moisture. An unlevel base is corrected before a single tile is laid.
2. **Surface preparation** — self-levelling compound levels the floor; for wet areas, waterproofing membrane is applied first.
3. **Layout planning** — tiles are dry-laid to plan cuts, starting points, and grout lines so cuts fall in the least visible places.
4. **Installation** — tiles are fixed with the correct adhesive and notched trowel for the tile type; spacers keep joints even. For large slabs, suction lifters and levelling clips prevent lippage.
5. **Grouting and sealing** — grout is applied and wiped clean; natural stone and porous tiles are sealed to resist stains.
6. **Clean-up and inspection** — the floor is cleaned and every tile is checked for hollow spots, lippage, and uniform joints.

Where needed, we use professional systems such as Schlüter DITRA decoupling membranes, Mapei self-levelling underlayments, and Raimondi lippage-tuning systems — the same tools and materials used in commercial-grade tiling.

## When to Repair Instead of Re-Tile

Not every tile problem needs a full re-tile:

- **Hollow tiles** — individual loose tiles can be lifted and re-fixed without touching the rest of the floor.
- **Cracked tile** — a single tile can be removed and replaced if you have a spare or can match the design.
- **Tired grout** — re-grouting and re-sealing restores a floor or wall at a fraction of the replacement cost.
- **Staining** — some stains on natural stone can be removed and the tile re-sealed.

Full re-tiling makes sense when the substrate is failing, the layout is being changed, or the majority of tiles are damaged.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does it cost to tile a house in Malaysia?

Installation labour starts from RM 14 per square foot for floors and RM 16 per square foot for walls, with tile supply separate. A typical living room of 300 sq ft costs roughly RM 4,200–5,500 in labour, depending on tile size and complexity.

### How long does tiling a room take?

A medium living room (about 300 sq ft) typically takes 2–4 days including preparation, tiling, and grouting. Large-format slabs and natural stone take longer because of the precision required.

### Do I need waterproofing under bathroom tiles?

Yes. In wet areas, a waterproofing membrane must be applied before tiling to stop water from seeping into the walls and floor slab. This is part of our bathroom tiling work — see our waterproofing services for more.

### What is tile lippage?

Lippage is the difference in height between adjacent tiles — a lipped floor is uneven and catches the eye. Large-format tiles need levelling systems to prevent it, which is standard in our large-format installations.

### Can you match tiles for a repair?

Often yes — we source matching tiles where available. If the exact tile is discontinued, we can recommend a close match or suggest re-grouting to unify the look.
