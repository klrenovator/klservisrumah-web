# Drain Unclogging & Cleaning Services in KL and Selangor

**Question:** Why is my drain slow, gurgling or smelly?

**Answer:** Most household drain blockages in Malaysia come from a small set of causes: cooking grease and food waste in kitchen sinks, hair and soap scum in bathroom floor traps, and unsuitable items flushed down toilets. A single slow fixture usually means a local blockage near that fixture; when several fixtures back up together or sewage smells spread through the house, the blockage is likely in a shared pipe or the main gully line — that needs professional clearing.

## Signs Your Drain Is Blocked

- Water drains slowly from the sink, shower or floor trap.
- Gurgling sounds from another fixture when water runs.
- Foul smells coming up from floor traps or sinks.
- Water pooling around the floor trap in the bathroom or wet kitchen.
- Toilet water rising high after flushing, then sinking slowly.
- Overflow at the outdoor gully trap when the washing machine drains.

## What Actually Blocks Malaysian Drains

| Location | Most common blockage material | Why it builds up |
|---|---|---|
| Kitchen sink | Cooking oil, grease, santan residue, food scraps | Grease cools and coats the pipe; debris sticks to it layer by layer |
| Bathroom floor trap | Hair, soap scum, dead skin | Hair mats together and traps soap residue at the trap bend |
| Toilet | Wet wipes, sanitary products, excessive tissue | These do not break down like toilet paper and snag at bends |
| Outdoor gully trap | Leaves, silt, kitchen grease from the sink line | Rain and silt wash in; grease from the kitchen line accumulates |
| Main sewer line | Combined buildup, sometimes roots near old joints | Affects every fixture at once |

Pouring strong chemical drain cleaner down a fully blocked pipe rarely reaches the actual blockage, can damage older pipes and gaskets, and makes the plumber's clearing work more hazardous. Use it, if at all, only as preventive maintenance on a healthy drain — not as a cure for a blockage.

## How Professionals Clear a Blockage

1. **Identify the blockage location:** Which fixtures are affected tells a lot. One slow sink is local; everything gurgling together points downstream.
2. **Open the trap:** Many kitchen and bathroom blockages sit in the bottle trap or floor trap itself and are cleared in minutes once opened.
3. **Auger or drain snake:** A flexible cable is fed into the line to break up and pull out hair and grease clogs beyond the trap — including around bends a DIY plunger cannot reach.
4. **Flushing and testing:** The line is flushed at full flow to confirm it clears completely, not just partially.
5. **Check for repeat causes:** If a line blocks repeatedly, the plumber looks for wrong pipe gradient, a dip or "belly" in the pipe, a collapsed or root-invaded section, or a gully trap that has lost its water seal.

A proper clearing job ends with confirmation that the pipe runs clear — not just that the water "eventually went down".

## When a Blockage Is Really a Pipe Problem

Recurring blockages in the same line usually mean something upstream of the trap:

- **Wrong gradient** — the pipe is too flat, so water and solids drain too slowly and settle.
- **Sagging section** — a dipped stretch of underground pipe collects debris.
- **Old or corroded pipes** — rough inner walls catch grease and hair faster.
- **Root intrusion** — trees near old outdoor lines find joints and grow into them.

These cases need a repair or pipe-replacement scope, similar to [water leakage diagnosis & repair](/services/plumbing/water-leakage-diagnosis-and-repair), rather than another round of snaking. ## Preventing the Next Blockage

- Scrape cooking oil and food scraps into the bin before washing; fit a sink strainer.
- Pour used grease into a container and bin it — never down the sink.
- Fit hair catchers over bathroom floor traps and clean them weekly.
- Flush toilets with paper only; bin wipes, cotton pads and sanitary items.
- Clear outdoor gully traps of leaves and silt after heavy rain.
- Run hot water through the kitchen sink after oily washing-up.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is a slow drain always a blockage?
Not always. A single slow floor trap is often just a hair-clogged trap. But if a toilet also flushes weakly or another fixture gurgles, the restriction is further down the shared line and needs equipment to clear.

### Can I use hot water and dish soap to clear a grease clog?
Hot water and detergent can shift light grease residue in a partially slow sink, but it will not clear a hardened grease plug or a hair mat set further down the line. If water is standing or backing up, stop adding liquids and get it cleared mechanically.

### Why does the same floor trap keep blocking every few months?
Repeated blockages in the same spot usually mean a trap that fills with hair faster than it is cleaned, a shallow belly in the pipe, or buildup already lining the pipe wall. A full clear-out plus a check of the pipe condition solves recurring cases more permanently than clearing the trap each time.
