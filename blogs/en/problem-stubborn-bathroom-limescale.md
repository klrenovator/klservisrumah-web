# Stubborn Bathroom Limescale and Water Stains: What Works

**Question:** How do you remove stubborn white limescale from bathroom glass, taps and tiles?

**Answer:** Limescale is calcium and mineral deposits left by evaporating water. Mild acid cleaners such as vinegar or citric acid dissolve fresh scale; bathroom descalers work on heavier buildup. Avoid scratching glass or metal with abrasive scrubbers. If scale has damaged grout, etched glass or blocked shower heads, replacement or professional restoration may be needed.

## Where Limescale Appears

- Shower glass and screens.
- Taps, shower heads and mixer surfaces.
- Wall and floor tiles, especially near wet zones.
- Toilet bowls, cisterns and bidet sprays.
- Glass doors and mirror edges.

## Safe Removal Methods

| Surface | Safe approach | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Chrome taps | Mild descaler, soft cloth | Steel wool, rough scourers |
| Shower glass | Vinegar/citric solution, squeegee | Abrasive powder |
| Ceramic tiles | Bathroom descaler and soft brush | Acid on marble/limestone |
| Shower head | Soak in descaling solution | Brushing coated finishes |
| Toilet bowl | Toilet descaler and brush | Mixing bleach and acid |

Never mix acid-based descalers with bleach or ammonia. The fumes can be dangerous. Ventilate the bathroom, wear gloves, and rinse surfaces thoroughly.

## Why It Returns Quickly

Malaysian tap water can leave mineral deposits, especially where water is allowed to air-dry. Squeegeeing shower glass after use, improving ventilation, and fixing dripping taps slows new buildup.

If the shower flow is weak and the head is crusty, see [low water pressure at home](/problems/low-water-pressure) — sometimes the heater inlet filter or shower head is scaled.

## When DIY Cleaning Is Not Enough

Professional help is useful when:

- Glass has become permanently cloudy or etched.
- Grout is stained and porous after years of scale and soap.
- Shower heads or taps are partially blocked.
- Scale covers silicone seals and mould is underneath.
- You are preparing a bathroom for move-out or handover.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is vinegar safe for bathroom glass?
Diluted vinegar or citric acid can work on light limescale, but rinse well and do not leave acid on metal fittings for too long.

### Can cloudy shower glass be restored?
Some cloudiness can be improved with specialised glass cleaning, but etched or damaged glass may need replacement.

### How often should a bathroom be descaled?
It depends on water quality and use. Light weekly cleaning prevents heavy buildup; a deep clean every few months keeps glass and grout manageable.
