# Post-Renovation Cleaning in KL & Selangor: Complete Guide

The contractor packs up, the plastic sheeting comes down, and the house looks finished until you run a finger along the top of a door frame. Renovation leaves behind fine cement dust that settles into every crevice, plus paint splatter, silicone smears, grout haze on new tiles and adhesive residue on glass. None of it responds to a normal mop and a bottle of floor cleaner.

## Why post-renovation cleaning is a different job

Deep cleaning is for lived-in homes: grease, limescale, dust, mould. Post-renovation cleaning is for construction residue, which is chemically and physically different.

| | Deep cleaning | Post-renovation cleaning |
|---|---|---|
| Target soiling | Grease, limescale, mould, household dust | Cement dust, paint, silicone, grout haze, adhesive |
| Tools | Microfibre, HEPA vacuum, scrub pads | Specialist solvents, plastic and metal scrapers, wet-vacuum, floor buffer |
| Risk | Low | High — new finishes can be scratched or etched |
| Typical start | From RM 420 (1,000 sqft condo) | From RM 650 (1,000 sqft condo) |
| Passes | One pass | Rough clean, then detail, then final polish |

Cement dust is abrasive. Dry-wiping it across a new window pane or a freshly painted wall grinds it into the surface. That is why the sequence matters as much as the products.

## When to book

- **3–7 days after the contractor finishes.** Paint, silicone and grout need to cure. Cleaning uncured silicone tears it out of the joint.
- **Before furniture delivery.** Every surface is reachable and nothing has to be protected or moved twice.
- **Before handover or listing photos.** Fine dust shows up badly in photographs, especially on dark floors and glass.
- **After a partial renovation too.** A kitchen-only or bathroom-only job still throws dust through the whole unit if doors were left open.

If your renovation is finished but you are still weeks from moving in, a post-renovation clean followed later by a light [deep clean](/services/deep-cleaning) before move-in day is the practical order.

## Surface safety rules we do not break

- **No metal scrapers on glass or aluminium.** Anodised aluminium scratches permanently; new glass can be left with fine tramlines visible in daylight.
- **No acidic cleaner on natural stone.** Marble, travertine and limestone etch on contact. Grout haze on stone needs a stone-safe product.
- **Test first on unusual finishes.** Microcement, limewash, textured paint and matt lacquer all react differently.
- **Coordinate with the contractor when finishes are under warranty.** Some suppliers specify approved cleaning methods, and using the wrong one voids cover.

## Common mistakes

- **Booking a normal maid service.** Household products do not remove grout haze or cured silicone, and the attempt often scratches new surfaces.
- **Cleaning too early.** Uncured silicone and grout get damaged, and the contractor may still be returning for touch-ups.
- **Skipping the ceiling and high surfaces.** Dust that stays on top of wardrobes and door frames falls back down within days — see [construction dust that keeps returning](/problems/construction-dust-returning).
- **Living in the unit during the clean.** Solvents need ventilation and floors need to stay clear.
- **Assuming cement splatter will just wipe off.** Dried cement on tiles needs a controlled acidic treatment — see [cement splatter on new tiles](/problems/cement-splatter-tile-clean).
- **Ignoring loose tiles found during the clean.** Hollow-sounding tiles are a laying defect, not a cleaning issue — see [loose and hollow tiles](/problems/loose-hollow-tiles) and raise it with the contractor while you still can.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does a post-renovation clean take?

A 1,000 sqft condo typically takes a team most of a working day. A 2,000 sqft terrace house usually needs a larger crew and can run into a second day if there is heavy residue or floors need buffing.

### Can you clean while my contractor is still working?

We can do an interim clean, but the final clean should happen after all trades are finished. Otherwise the next trade re-dusts everything you paid to clean.

### Will grout haze come off completely?

Usually, if it is treated before it hardens over months. Older haze on porous or textured tiles may need more than one pass, and we tell you before starting if we expect a residual film.

### Do you remove leftover renovation materials?

Yes, bagging and disposal of leftover debris is part of the standard clean. For large volumes we can coordinate a skip bin, quoted separately.

### What if I find a missed spot afterwards?

Tell us within 7 days and we return to re-clean the area under the spot-check guarantee.

### Do I need a deep clean as well?

Not immediately. A post-renovation clean covers everything a deep clean does plus construction residue. If you move in weeks later, a lighter clean before move-in day is enough.
