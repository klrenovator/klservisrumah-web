## When This Service Is the Right Choice

Book on-site repair when the damaged item is fixed in place or too awkward to remove: a gate leaf hanging off a cracked hinge, a rusted-through gate frame, a loose railing post, a cracked staircase bracket or a collapsed fence section. Two typical triggers:

- **[Loose metal gate hinges](/problems/loose-metal-gate-hinges)** that have progressed from wobble to cracked weld or torn plate.
- **[Rusting window grilles](/problems/rusting-window-grille)** where corrosion has eaten through bars or frames and patching beats full replacement.

When the damage is extensive or the design is simply at end of life, replacement fabrication — a [custom window grille](/services/welding/custom-window-grille) or a new [main gate](/services/welding/main-gate-fabrication) — is compared openly against the repair cost before you commit.

## What Should Be Checked Before Work

- **Metal type and thickness** — mild steel welds routinely; stainless or aluminium need different processes and are confirmed at quotation.
- **Rust extent** — surface rust cleans back to sound steel; rust-through means cutting out and plating, which changes price and method.
- **Load and stress** — a cracked hinge on a heavy gate leaf needs realignment as well as welding, or the crack returns.
- **Power access** — confirm a suitable supply or discuss arrangements when booking.
- **Surroundings** — vehicles, glass, plants and finishes near the weld zone are protected from sparks.
- **Approval** — condo and managed-building rules for hot work, working hours and access passes.

## Typical Work Process

1. **Photos first, then on-site assessment** of metal, rust and alignment.
2. **Make-safe** anything dangerous — a hanging gate leaf is supported before tools come out.
3. **Grind and clean** back to sound, bright metal; welding over rust and paint is how repairs fail fast.
4. **Weld, then grind smooth**; realign gates and moving parts so the weld is not immediately re-stressed.
5. **Prime and finish** — welded spots are protected against the weather that caused the last failure.
6. **Function test** — the gate is swung, the railing loaded, before handover.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Welding straight over rust and old paint — the weld bonds to contamination and cracks within months.
- Cosmetic patch welds over a structural crack without fixing alignment or support.
- Skipping primer after the weld, letting the fresh steel start rusting the same week.
- Ignoring hot-work safety: sparks near vehicles, dry leaves or stored paint are a fire risk, not a minor detail.
- Assuming stainless or aluminium can be handled like mild steel — confirm the metal before pricing.

## Safety and Maintenance After the Repair

Welding is hot work: the area is cleared, shielded and watched, and for managed buildings the required permissions are arranged before the visit. After the repair, keep welded joints painted and inspect them after the monsoon months — a chip touched up in minutes prevents the rust-under-paint cycle that destroys repairs from the inside.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you repair a gate hinge the same day?
Where the schedule allows, yes — hinge failures are treated as urgent because a dragging leaf is a safety and security issue. Photos sent ahead help the welder arrive with the right consumables.

### Will the repair last as long as the original?
With proper cut-back to sound steel, correct alignment and primed protection, a repair commonly outlasts the surrounding original metal — the weak point moves elsewhere, which normal inspection catches early.

### Do you weld stainless steel or aluminium on site?
Those metals need different processes and are confirmed case by case at quotation — the honest answer starts with identifying your metal from photos.
