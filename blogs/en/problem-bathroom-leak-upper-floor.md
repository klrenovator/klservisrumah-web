# Upper-Floor Bathroom Leak: Finding the Source Before You Hack

**Question:** The ceiling below my upstairs bathroom drips or stains every time someone showers. What is leaking, and do the tiles have to be hacked?

**Answer:** Water below an upstairs bathroom comes from one of four sources: a leaking pipe or fitting, a failed floor trap or waste connection, deteriorated tile grout and floor-wall joints, or a failed waterproofing membrane under the tiles. Not every case needs hacking. Pipe and trap leaks are fixed at the fitting. Slab cracks are usually treated with PU injection. Surface-level grout failure can often be resolved with a no-hack coating system. Full hacking and re-waterproofing is reserved for genuine membrane failure across the floor.

Getting the diagnosis right matters, because the four scenarios differ enormously in cost and disruption. Hacking a bathroom that only needed a trap reseal is an expensive mistake.

## Direct Answer: Which Repair Do I Need?

| What the evidence shows | Likely source | Typical repair |
|---|---|---|
| Drips when a tap or shower is running, even without floor washing | Pressurised pipe or fitting | Locate and repair the joint or pipe run |
| Drips after showering, worst near the shower area | Floor trap / waste connection | Reseal or replace the trap and its collar |
| Slow staining, worst after the floor is washed | Grout and floor-wall joints | Regrout plus no-hack surface seal |
| Wide, spreading damp patch on the ceiling | Membrane failure across the floor | Hack, re-lay membrane, re-tile |
| Damp along one crack line only | Concrete slab crack | PU high-pressure injection |

## How Professionals Trace the Source

A proper diagnosis is a process of elimination, not guesswork.

1. **Dry-run test.** The ceiling below is dried and monitored while no water is used upstairs. If it still wets, the leak is a pressurised pipe, not a drainage or surface issue.
2. **Fixture isolation.** Each fixture is used one at a time — basin, shower, WC, floor washing — with the ceiling checked between each. This alone identifies most trap and waste leaks.
3. **Ponding test.** The bathroom floor is plugged and flooded for a period. If the ceiling wets only under ponding, the membrane or floor joints are the source.
4. **Moisture mapping.** A moisture meter maps the wet zone in the ceiling. Because water travels along the slab and beams before dropping, the visible drip point is often not directly below the defect.
5. **Physical inspection of the wet area.** Grout condition, silicone at the floor-wall junction, hollow tiles, trap collar condition, and the state of the shower screen seal.

That last point matters: a leaking [shower screen](/problems/shower-screen-leaking) throws water past the wet zone entirely and can imitate a waterproofing failure.

## The Four Repair Methods Explained

### PU High-Pressure Injection
Polyurethane resin is injected under pressure into a concrete crack. On contact with moisture it expands and cures, sealing the crack path. Best suited to active leaks along a defined crack in a concrete slab. It is priced per injection point, and the point count depends on crack length and severity. KL Servis Rumah publishes [PU injection grouting](/services/waterproofing/pu-high-pressure-injection-grouting) from **RM 300 per point**.

### No-Hack Surface Waterproofing
A penetrating or coating system is applied over the existing tiled floor and joints, sealing the grout network without removing tiles. Suitable where the leak is through grout, hairline joints or minor surface defects and where the tiles themselves are sound. It is far less disruptive but is not a substitute for replacing a genuinely failed membrane.

### Full Hack and Re-Waterproofing
Tiles and screed are removed to expose the structural slab, a new membrane is applied and turned up the walls, protected, screeded, water-ponding tested, then re-tiled. This is the definitive fix for whole-floor membrane failure. ## Why the Ceiling Below Must Be Repaired Second

Never rebuild the ceiling before the leak is confirmed stopped. Plasterboard installed over a still-active leak stains within weeks and the work is wasted. Standard sequence:

1. Stop the water at source.
2. Allow the slab and ceiling void to dry — this can take one to two weeks in humid weather.
3. Replace soft or stained boards and re-tape joints.
4. Stain-block primer, then repaint the full ceiling plane.

## Cost Guidance for KL and Selangor

Published guidance for this problem spans **RM 250 to RM 9,000**, which reflects how different the four scenarios are. In practice:

- Trap reseal or fitting repair: the low end, a short plumbing visit.
- PU injection: priced per point from RM 300, so total depends on crack length.
- No-hack coating: mid-range, driven by floor area and joint treatment.
- Full hack and re-waterproof: the high end, because it includes demolition, membrane, screed, ponding test and re-tiling.

## Condominium-Specific Notes

In stratified buildings, a leak between units involves your neighbour and often the building management. Practical points:

- Access to the unit above is usually required to run the fixture isolation and ponding tests.
- Management corporations frequently require notification and a permit for wet-area hacking, and may restrict working hours.
- Establishing which party's defect caused the leak matters for cost apportionment, so keep dated photos and the inspection findings.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can an upstairs bathroom leak be fixed without hacking the tiles?
Often, yes. Pipe, trap and grout-level leaks are all repairable without hacking. Hacking is necessary when the waterproofing membrane under the floor has failed across the area.

### How long does the ceiling take to dry out?
Typically one to two weeks in Malaysian humidity, longer if the slab absorbed a lot of water. Rebuilding sooner risks trapping moisture behind the new board.

### Is PU injection permanent?
PU injection permanently seals the crack it fills. It does not waterproof the rest of the floor, so if the membrane is failing generally, more cracks can appear elsewhere later.

### Who pays when the leak is between two condominium units?
That depends on where the defect is and on the building's by-laws and management practice. A documented inspection identifying the source is the starting point for any discussion.

### Should I repaint the ceiling first to stop it looking bad?
No. Repainting before the leak is stopped wastes the work — the stain returns through the new paint.
