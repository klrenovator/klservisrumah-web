# Cracked or Discoloured Tile Grout

**Question:** The grout between my tiles is cracking, crumbling, or turning grey-black with mould. Can I just paint it?

**Answer:** Grout paint lasts 6 to 12 months and does not fix cracked or missing grout. Old cement grout is usually past its 5–8 year service life, or the substrate has moved. The proper repair is to grind the old grout out to a clean depth, clean the tile edges, pack premium epoxy or acrylic grout, and seal it. Epoxy grout is worth the extra cost in bathrooms and kitchens because it is waterproof, mould-resistant and typically lasts 15+ years versus 5–8 for cement.

This is a **grout-line** problem. Hollow or loose tiles are [loose hollow tiles](/problems/loose-hollow-tiles). Stains that remain after a deep clean are [stubborn grout stains](/problems/stubborn-grout-stains-after-deep-clean).

## Why Grout Fails in Malaysian Wet Areas

- Cement grout past its 5–8 year service life.
- Substrate movement stressing the joint.
- Damp bathrooms and kitchens feeding mould.
- Cheap unsealed grout used originally.

## When to Call

- Grout crumbles to powder when scratched.
- Water is leaking to the floor below — see [leaking bathroom](/problems/leaking-bathroom).
- Mould returns despite cleaning — see also [mould in bathroom after cleaning](/problems/mold-in-bathroom-after-cleaning).

## What a Proper Regrout Involves

1. Grind out the old grout to a clean depth.
2. Clean the tile edges.
3. Apply premium epoxy or acrylic grout.
4. Seal cementitious grout with a hydrophobic sealer.
