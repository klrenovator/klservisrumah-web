# IKEA & Taobao Furniture Assembly in KL and Selangor

**Question:** Is it worth paying a handyman to assemble flat-pack furniture?

**Answer:** For large or load-bearing pieces, usually yes. Flat-pack wardrobes, bed frames and tall cabinets fail not because of the product but because of assembly shortcuts: cam locks not fully seated, panels forced into misaligned dowels, hinges hung out of square, and — most dangerous of all — tall units never anchored to the wall. A professional assembles square and true the first time, anchors anti-tip hardware properly, and finishes in a fraction of the time a first-timer takes to decipher the manual.

## Items Commonly Assembled

| Item | Typical assembly traps |
|---|---|
| Wardrobes (hinged and sliding) | Panels forced out of square; sliding tracks misaligned; doors never levelled |
| Bed frames and storage beds | Bolts under-torqued = squeaks; slat supports fitted wrong |
| Display and storage cabinets | Tipping risk if not anchored; glass shelves set before alignment |
| Office desks and workstations | Cable cut-outs and return units assembled mirror-reversed |
| Kitchen island carts and trolleys | Castors cross-threaded; drawers inverted |
| Shoe cabinets and bookshelves | Back panels skipped (they carry the racking strength) |

Taobao-bought items add their own challenges: manuals in Chinese only, hardware sorted poorly, and occasional missing or wrong fixings — a professional sorts, checks and sources small replacements instead of forcing wrong-fit parts.

## What Professional Assembly Includes

1. **Inventory check** — all panels and hardware counted against the parts list before starting; missing pieces flagged immediately.
2. **Assembly sequence** — frames built square, cam locks fully engaged, back panels and braces fitted (never skipped — they stop the wobble).
3. **Levelling and alignment** — doors and drawers adjusted to even gaps; sliding tracks set and tested.
4. **Wall anchoring** — anti-tip brackets on tall wardrobes, bookshelves and display cabinets, fixed to solid wall material. A heavy unit on a gypsum partition needs different anchors than one on brick — the mount is matched to your wall type.
5. **Placement and cleanup** — the finished piece is positioned and cardboard/packaging consolidated for disposal.

## Safety: The Anti-Tip Anchor Is Not Optional

Flat-pack tall furniture is a genuine hazard in homes with children. Unanchored wardrobes and shelves tip when drawers are climbed or doors are swung, and the injuries are serious. IKEA supplies anti-tip hardware with tall pieces for exactly this reason — and it only works when it is actually fixed into the wall with the right anchor:

- **Concrete/brick:** expansion plugs or masonry anchors.
- **Gypsum partition:** the anchor must find a stud or use rated cavity fasteners — see how TV mounting handles the same wall-type problem in the [TV wall mounting safety guide](/blog/tv-wall-mounting-safety-guide) and why [TVs fall off walls](/problems/tv-fell-off-wall) when anchors are wrong.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you fix furniture I already half-assembled?
Yes. The usual approach is to partially disassemble, correct the misalignments (forced panels, skipped back panels, reversed parts), then re-assemble square. Salvage depends on whether particleboard cams or screw holes were damaged in the first attempt.

### Do I need to clear the room before assembly?
Not fully — just enough open floor space for the panels to lie flat during assembly (usually about the footprint of the finished piece plus walking space). Boxes should be in the room where the item will live; assembled wardrobes barely fit through door frames.

### Can you assemble brands other than IKEA and Taobao?
Yes — flat-pack assembly follows the same principles across brands sold in Malaysia, including furniture from Kaison, SSF, Ewin and online sellers. The parts-count-first process handles any manual language.
