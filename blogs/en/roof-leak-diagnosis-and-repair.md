# Roof Leak Diagnosis & Repair in KL & Selangor

Question: How do I find where my roof is leaking?

Answer: Roof leaks are often hard to find because water travels along rafters and appears far from the entry point. A professional diagnosis inspects the entire roof — tiles, ridge, flashing, and gutters — to locate the source before any targeted repair.

A leaking roof damages ceilings, walls, insulation, and even the structure over time. KL Servis Rumah provides professional roof leak diagnosis and repair across Kuala Lumpur and Selangor, from RM 380.

## Why Roof Leaks Are Hard to Find

- **Water travels** — water runs along rafters and beams, appearing far from the leak point.
- **Hidden entry points** — the damage may be under a tile or at a ridge.
- **Multiple sources** — an old roof may leak in more than one place.

For this reason, guessing where the leak is and patching the ceiling is rarely effective. A proper diagnosis is essential.

## How We Diagnose Roof Leaks

1. **Inspect the roof** — we check tiles, ridge, flashing, valleys, and gutters.
2. **Check the interior** — we trace the water path from the ceiling stain.
3. **Test in rain** — where possible, we verify during rain or use a hose test.
4. **Identify the source** — we pinpoint the exact point of water entry.

## Repair Options

- **Damaged or shifted tiles** — replaced and reseated.
- **Cracked ridge tiles** — re-bedded and re-pointed.
- **Failed flashing** — resealed or replaced.
- **Blocked gutters** — cleared and realigned.
- **Flat roof membrane failure** — repaired or waterproofed.

For ceiling stains inside, our [ceiling repair service](/services/ceiling/water-damaged-ceiling-repair) restores the interior once the leak is fixed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why can't I find my roof leak from inside?

Water travels along rafters and beams, so the stain inside may be far from the actual entry point. A professional inspection of the whole roof is the reliable way to find it.

### Can I patch the leaking spot from inside?

Patching the ceiling from inside stops the stain but not the leak — water continues to enter and spread. The source on the roof must be repaired.

### How long does a roof leak repair take?

A typical leak repair is completed within a day once the source is identified. Larger or multiple repairs take longer.

### Do I need to replace my whole roof?

Not usually. Most leaks come from a specific tile, ridge, flashing, or gutter that can be repaired directly. Replacement is only needed for extensive damage.
