# Door, Hinge & Lockset Repair in KL & Selangor

A sticking door, a squeaky hinge, or a stiff lock is a common and irritating household problem. KL Servis Rumah provides professional door, hinge, and lockset repair across Kuala Lumpur and Selangor, from RM 120.

This guide explains the common door problems we fix, what causes them, and what the repair involves.

## Common Door Problems We Fix

- **Sticking doors** — the door rubs against the frame or floor.
- **Squeaky hinges** — worn or dry hinges causing noise.
- **Dropped or sagging doors** — hinges have loosened or pulled out.
- **Faulty locksets** — stiff locks, keys that stick, or mechanisms that fail.
- **Misaligned strike plates** — the lock does not latch properly.
- **Swollen doors** — common in humid weather, causing the door to jam.

## What Causes Door Problems in Malaysia?

- **Humidity** — wooden doors swell and jam in humid conditions.
- **Worn hinges** — over time, hinge pins and screws wear or loosen.
- **Settling** — the house settling can pull door frames out of square.
- **Poor original installation** — a door hung incorrectly from the start.

## How We Repair Doors

1. **Diagnose the problem** — we identify whether it is the hinge, lock, or the door itself.
2. **Adjust or repair** — hinges are tightened, realigned, or replaced; locksets are adjusted or repaired.
3. **Plane or sand** — a swollen or sticking edge is planed or sanded.
4. **Test** — the door opens, closes, and locks smoothly.
5. **Clean handover** — the area is left clean and functional.

## When You Should Not DIY

- The hinge screws have stripped out of the frame.
- The door is heavy and misaligned.
- The lockset mechanism is damaged and needs replacement.
- You are not sure whether the frame has moved.

Attempting repairs on a heavy or badly misaligned door can make it worse.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is my wooden door sticking?

In Malaysia, high humidity causes wood to swell, making doors stick. The door edge may need planing or the hinges adjusting.

### Can you fix a door that won't lock properly?

Usually yes. A misaligned strike plate or a worn lockset can be adjusted or replaced so the door locks cleanly.

### Do you replace locksets?

Yes. If the lock mechanism is beyond repair, we can replace it with a suitable lockset.

### How long does a door repair take?

Most hinge and sticking-door repairs are completed within an hour. Replacing a lockset or planing a badly swollen door takes a little longer.
