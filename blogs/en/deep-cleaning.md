# Deep Cleaning & Move-In/Out Services in KL & Selangor

Deep cleaning is not a longer version of a weekly clean. It is a different job: everything a regular visit deliberately skips — inside the oven, behind the fridge, ceiling fan blades, window tracks, grout lines, limescale — done once, thoroughly, so normal cleaning can keep it that way.

**Direct answer:** KL Servis Rumah provides deep cleaning across Kuala Lumpur and Selangor from **RM 420** for a 1,000 sqft condo and **RM 650** for a 2,000 sqft terrace house. Work is covered by a **24-hour re-clean guarantee**.

## When people book a deep clean

- **Move-in** — you do not want to inherit the previous occupant's kitchen and bathrooms.
- **Move-out** — landlord inspection and deposit return depend on the state of the unit.
- **Post-illness** — after flu, COVID or a stomach bug in the household.
- **Allergy season** — dust-mite reduction on mattresses, sofas and curtains.
- **Quarterly refresh** — a reset for a home that is otherwise maintained.
- **Before or after guests** — festive season, extended family visits.

If renovation work just finished, this is not the right service. Cement dust, grout haze and paint splatter need [post-renovation cleaning](/services/post-renovation-cleaning).

## How we work

1. **Scope assessment** — home size, focus areas and specific concerns: mould, post-illness, allergy, move-in or move-out. For move-out we ask for the landlord's checklist if you have one.
2. **Itemised quote** — based on size, scope and crew size. A 1,000 sqft unit is typically 2 cleaners for 4–6 hours; a 2,000 sqft house is typically 3 cleaners for 6–8 hours.
3. **Top-down cleaning** — ceiling fans, light fixtures and high surfaces first, then walls, furniture, and floors last, so debris falls and is captured.
4. **Detail work** — inside cabinets and appliances, tracks, grout, descaling, mould treatment.
5. **Walkthrough** — we go through the property with you against the scope before leaving.

## Common mistakes

- **Booking a regular clean and expecting deep-clean results.** They are priced differently because they are different jobs.
- **Deep cleaning before the movers arrive.** Clean after the unit is empty, not before.
- **Assuming mould will not return.** If it comes back in days, the cause is ventilation or waterproofing. See [mould in the bathroom after cleaning](/problems/mold-in-bathroom-after-cleaning).
- **Using acid on natural stone.** Marble and some granite etch permanently. Tell us your surfaces.
- **Deep cleaning immediately after renovation.** Book [post-renovation cleaning](/services/post-renovation-cleaning) instead. See [construction dust that keeps returning](/problems/construction-dust-returning).
- **Leaving grout stains until move-out day.** Some staining needs longer dwell time than a single visit allows. See [stubborn grout stains after a deep clean](/problems/stubborn-grout-stains-after-deep-clean).

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does a deep clean take?

A 1,000 sqft condo is typically 4–6 hours with 2 cleaners. A 2,000 sqft terrace house is typically 6–8 hours with 3 cleaners. Heavily soiled properties take longer and we say so at quotation.

### Should the unit be empty?

For move-in and move-out cleans, an empty unit gives the best result and the fastest job. For occupied homes we work around furniture and clear what can be safely moved.

### Do you guarantee my deposit will be returned?

No cleaning company can guarantee a landlord's decision. What we can do is clean to the inspection points landlords actually check, and re-clean any area you flag within 24 hours.

### Can you remove all stains?

Not always. Etched marble, permanently discoloured grout, rust-stained enamel and heat-damaged surfaces are material damage, not dirt. We will tell you what is dirt and what is not.

### Do you clean air-conditioner units?

Filters and visible grilles are wiped as part of a deep clean. Full internal air-conditioner servicing is not part of this service.

### How far ahead should I book a move-out clean?

Book once your moving date is confirmed. Month-end and quarter-end are the busiest periods in Klang Valley.
