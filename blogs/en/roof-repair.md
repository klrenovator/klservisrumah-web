## Common Roof Problems in Malaysia

- **Leaks after heavy rain** — water entering through damaged or shifted tiles.
- **Damaged or cracked ridge tiles** — a common leak source at the roof ridge.
- **Blocked or leaking gutters** — causing water to overflow onto walls.
- **Aged waterproofing** — failing membranes on flat roofs.
- **Corrosion** — on metal roofs from years of exposure.

If your ceiling shows water stains after rain, the roof is often the source — see our [ceiling leak after rain guide](/problems/ceiling-leak-after-rain).

## When to Call a Roof Repair Professional

- Water stains appear on ceilings after rain.
- You can see damaged, cracked, or missing roof tiles.
- Gutters overflow or leak.
- The roof is old and its waterproofing has never been checked.

Attempting roof work yourself is dangerous and can make the leak worse. Professional roofers have the safety equipment and experience to do the job properly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I find a roof leak?

Water can travel along rafters and appear far from the actual entry point, so finding roof leaks is tricky. A professional inspection looks at the whole roof, including ridge tiles, flashing, and gutters, to identify the source.

### Can a leaking roof be repaired without replacing the whole roof?

Often yes. Many leaks come from a specific tile, ridge, flashing, or gutter that can be repaired directly. A full replacement is only needed when the roof is extensively damaged.

### How long does roof repair take?

A typical leak repair is completed within a day. Larger jobs like membrane waterproofing take longer depending on the area.

### Do you also fix the damaged ceiling inside?

We focus on the roof repair. For the ceiling stains inside, our [ceiling repair service](/services/ceiling/water-damaged-ceiling-repair) can restore the interior once the leak is fixed.
