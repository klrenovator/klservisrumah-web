## Glass, seal, hardware — which is failing?

| Symptom | Likely cause | Typical first fix |
|---|---|---|
| Cracked or shattered pane | Impact, stress, or tempered glass nick | Glass replacement |
| Rain water on the inner sill | Failed seal, clogged weep holes, or warped sash | Seal replacement + clear weeps |
| Fog between double-glazed panes | Failed insulated-unit seal | New glass unit, not a wipe |
| Hard to slide | Flat rollers or dirty track | Rollers + clean |
| Handle spins or will not lock | Worn gearbox or misaligned keep | Handle / lock repair |

Do not silicone the whole sash shut to “stop the leak”. That traps water in the frame and hides a seal you could have replaced.

## Safety: broken glass in a home

Broken window glass is both a weather and a safety problem — especially on upper floors and near children.

- For landed houses we can often replace **common 4 mm / 5 mm clear or tinted** sizes on the same visit.
- Custom sizes, **tempered** glass and double-glazed units are measured, boarded if needed, and fitted in about **2–5 working days**.
- Wet-area and door-adjacent glass should be tempered or laminated safety glass, not ordinary annealed glass.

Do not leave a taped star-crack on a high window through a storm weekend.

## How a window repair visit works

1. **Inspect** — frame condition, glass type, seal hardness, rollers, locks, weep holes.
2. **Quote** — written scope: glass only, seals, hardware, or a mix.
3. **Safe removal** — glass is taped, lifted, and carried out; not smashed in the room.
4. **Install** — new glass on setting blocks, new seals seated, hardware tested.
5. **Water check** — weeps open; casement closes on the new seal without forcing the handle.

## What we check before quoting a full window change

- Is the frame still rigid and square?
- Is the leak the glass gasket or the wall-to-frame silicone?
- For wall damp around a window, the issue may be [waterproofing](/services/waterproofing), not the pane.
- Can the sash be adjusted, or is the track bent from a previous forced close?

## Common mistakes

- Replacing glass but leaving the old hardened gasket.
- Painting shut a timber casement, then breaking the stay.
- Oiling a sliding track that is full of sand — it becomes grinding paste.
- Using annealed glass where tempered is required.
