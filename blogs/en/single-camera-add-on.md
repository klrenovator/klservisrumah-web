## What Should Be Checked Before Work

- **Recorder spare channels** — a 4-channel NVR with 4 cameras fitted has no room; an upgrade path is quoted honestly instead of forcing it.
- **System brand and format** — cameras are matched to the recorder's ecosystem and resolution support.
- **Cable route or Wi-Fi signal** — PoE add-ons need a cable run; Wi-Fi add-ons need reliable signal at the exact mounting point.
- **Power availability** — PoE draws from the recorder or switch; Wi-Fi cameras need a nearby socket.
- **Storage impact** — an extra camera shares the same hard disk, shortening total retention; see [CCTV not recording when storage is full](/problems/cctv-not-recording-storage-full).
- **App access** — pairing requires the admin account, not just a shared view login.
- **Mounting surface and weather exposure** — outdoor points get weather-sealed housings and drip loops.

## Typical Work Process

1. **Identify recorder model, free channels and app ecosystem.**
2. **Agree the camera position** — sightlines, height, tamper reach and light at night.
3. **Run cable or verify Wi-Fi at the exact point** before mounting.
4. **Mount, weather-seal and connect** the camera.
5. **Assign the channel or pair in the app**, then verify live view, recording and motion notifications from the owner's phone.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Buying a cheap mismatched camera first and discovering the recorder will not register it.
- Mounting before testing Wi-Fi signal at the exact spot — signal at the window is not signal at the soffit.
- Covering a new blind spot while creating glare into a bright light at night.
- Forgetting the hard disk: retention drops when another camera joins, and nobody checks until evidence is missing.
- Drilling through concealed wiring or into a weak substrate that will not hold screws long-term.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a different brand camera be added to my recorder?
Sometimes, when the recorder and camera share a compatible standard and app pathway. It is verified against your exact model first — the honest answer can be no, and that is better than a camera that never records.

### How much retention will I lose by adding a camera?
Retention is total disk space divided by all cameras' recording load. One extra camera on an 8-camera system trims retention modestly; on a nearly full disk it can matter. The estimate on site uses your actual disk size and settings.

### Can I view the new camera in the same app?
Yes when it joins the same ecosystem — that is the point of matching the system. If your current setup already mixes brands awkwardly, the visit will flag it.
