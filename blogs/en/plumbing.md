## Common Plumbing Problems in Malaysian Homes

- **Dripping or leaking taps** — wastes water and raises your bill.
- **Blocked sinks and toilets** — caused by hair, grease, and foreign objects.
- **Low water pressure** — often a blocked pipe, faulty pump, or pressure issue.
- **Hidden pipe leaks** — a sudden jump in your water bill is a common sign.
- **Leaking bathroom floors** — water seeping through to the floor below.
- **Burst pipes** — an emergency that needs immediate attention.

If your water bill has spiked but you cannot see a leak, the water may be escaping from a hidden pipe. Our [hidden leak detection guide](/blog/hidden-water-leak-detection-kl) explains how this is found without tearing up your home.

## When to Call a Plumber

Some small fixes (like replacing a tap washer) are DIY-friendly. Call a professional when:

- Water is leaking into walls, ceilings, or the floor below.
- Your water pressure suddenly drops.
- Drains are repeatedly blocked.
- There is a smell of sewage or signs of damp.
- A pipe has burst and water is escaping.

Attempting major repairs without the right tools can cause more damage and cost more in the long run.

### Can a leaking toilet be fixed without replacing it?

Often yes. Many leaks are caused by a worn valve, seal, or washer that can be replaced cheaply. If the ceramic or mechanism is damaged, replacement may be the better option.

### Why is my water pressure suddenly low?

Common causes include a blocked pipe or filter, a faulty pressure pump, a partially closed valve, or a hidden leak. A plumber can test the system and identify the cause.
