# Cement Splatter and Dust After Renovation: Cleaning Safely

**Question:** How do you remove cement splatter and renovation dust from tiles, floors and glass without damaging them?

**Answer:** Cement splatter and grout haze need careful removal using suitable tile cleaners, non-scratch pads and vacuuming. Strong acid can etch polished tiles, metal fittings and natural stone, so it should only be used by trained cleaners with the right dilution and rinsing. Heavy dust must be vacuumed before mopping, otherwise it becomes abrasive slurry.

## Common Post-Renovation Residue

| Residue | Where found | Risk if cleaned wrongly |
|---|---|---|
| Cement splatter | Tiles, glass, doors, frames | Scratching or acid etching |
| Grout haze | New floor/wall tiles | Dulling polished surfaces |
| Silicone smears | Glass, shower screens | Smearing if scraped improperly |
| Paint specks | Floors, skirting, switches | Damaging finish if scraped |
| Fine cement dust | Everywhere | Abrasive smears if wiped dry |
| Protective film | Cabinets, frames, appliances | Leaving sticky residue |

## Safe Cleaning Process

1. **Dry removal first.** Vacuum or sweep coarse dust and debris.
2. **Test a small area.** Confirm the cleaner does not mark tile, metal or glass.
3. **Use non-scratch tools.** White scrub pads, plastic scrapers and soft microfibre are safer than blades.
4. **Apply appropriate cleaner.** Cement residue may need a tile-safe acid-free or mildly acidic cleaner, correctly diluted.
5. **Rinse thoroughly.** Residue left on tiles can leave white film.
6. **Repeat carefully.** Heavy splatter may soften gradually rather than coming off in one scrape.

## What Not to Do

- Do not mop thick cement dust before vacuuming.
- Do not use razor blades on polished tiles or glass without training.
- Do not pour strong acid down floor traps or onto metal.
- Do not use the same dirty water across the whole unit.
- Do not ignore silicone or paint on new shower glass; it becomes harder to remove later.

For polished stone, marble, homogeneous tiles or special surfaces, always test first. Natural stone is especially sensitive to acid.

## After the Clean

- Change or clean air-conditioner filters if renovation dust was heavy.
- Wipe inside cabinets before putting items back.
- Check grout haze after the floor dries.
- Keep the unit ventilated during and after cleaning.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can vinegar remove cement splatter?
Not reliably on heavy cement. Acidic household products can also damage sensitive surfaces. A tile-safe cleaner and correct dwell time are safer.

### Will post-renovation cleaning remove all paint specks?
Most can be removed, but cured paint on delicate surfaces may need careful hand work and could still carry a small risk of surface marking.
