## Common Water Problems We Fix

- **Leaking bathrooms** — water seeping through to the room below.
- **Damp or stained walls** — rising damp or water penetration.
- **Efflorescence** — white salt crystals on concrete or brick walls.
- **Roof leaks** — water entering through damaged roof surfaces.
- **Basement or car park leaks** — water seeping through concrete.

## Why Waterproofing Matters in Malaysia

Malaysia's heavy rain and high humidity make waterproofing essential. Without it:

- Water damages concrete and masonry over time.
- Mould and mildew thrive in damp areas, affecting health.
- Structural cracks widen as moisture weakens the material.
- Repair costs grow the longer a leak is ignored.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does waterproofing cost in KL & Selangor?

PU injection grouting starts from RM 300 per point, bathroom & wet area waterproofing from RM 1,000, and wall dampness repair from RM 350. Final pricing depends on the area and method.

### What is PU injection grouting?

It is a method of stopping active water leaks in concrete by injecting polyurethane resin into the crack or joint under pressure. The resin expands and seals the leak from inside.

### Can you waterproof an existing bathroom without breaking tiles?

In many cases, leaks can be stopped with injection grouting or surface waterproofing without removing tiles. The best method depends on where the water is coming from.

### Do you also fix the source of the leak?

Yes. Waterproofing and leak repair go together — we find where the water enters and apply the correct system to stop it.
