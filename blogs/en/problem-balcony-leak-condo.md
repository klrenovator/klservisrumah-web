# Condo Balcony Leaking Into the Unit Below

**Question:** The neighbour downstairs has water dripping from their ceiling on the same footprint as my balcony. Am I liable, and do my balcony tiles have to come up?

**Answer:** Under most Malaysian JMB rules the strata unit owner is responsible for their own balcony waterproofing. The leak is usually a failed acrylic or cementitious membrane, cracked tile grout, a blocked balcony drain causing ponding, or hairline cracks in the slab. A ponding test confirms the path. With the downstairs owner’s permission, PU grouting from below often seals hairline cracks without touching your tiles. A full tile-hack and new membrane typically takes three to five working days for a standard balcony.

This is a **condo open balcony leaking to the unit below**. It is not a [swimming-pool or wet-deck balcony leak](/problems/swimming-pool-leak-balcony), and it is not an indoor [leaking bathroom](/problems/leaking-bathroom).

## Why Condo Balconies Fail in the Klang Valley

- Failed acrylic or cementitious membrane after years of UV and ponding.
- Cracked tile grout letting rain through to the slab.
- Blocked balcony drain so water sits against the door threshold.
- Concrete slab hairline cracks.

A JMB repair notice means treat it as urgent and keep written records of the repair for later claims.

## What to Check Before Anyone Hacks Tiles

| Check | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Does water sit after rain? | Ponding overloads a tired membrane |
| Is the drain grate packed with leaves? | Clearing it is the first cheap test |
| Does the drip match shower use or only rain? | Indoor wet area vs balcony |
| Has grout already been resealed? | If yes and it still leaks, the membrane or slab is next |

## What Repair Involves

1. Ponding test to confirm the leak path.
2. PU grout the slab from below if access is allowed.
3. Strip tiles, apply a new membrane and retile when the membrane has failed.
4. Clear blocked drains and add a drip edge where the detail is missing.

Full tile-hack + new membrane + retile is typically **3 to 5 working days** for a standard balcony. Waterproofing carries the published Up to 5-Year Written Leak-Free Guarantee.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Am I liable for the downstairs unit’s damage?
Under most Malaysian JMB rules yes — the strata owner is responsible for their own waterproofing. Fix urgently and document the repair.

### Can you PU grout from downstairs without hacking my tiles?
Often yes, with the downstairs owner’s permission. Success rate is high for hairline cracks.

### How long does a balcony re-membrane take?
Typically 3 to 5 working days for a standard balcony.
