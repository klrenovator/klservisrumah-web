# Water Heater Repair & Descaling in KL and Selangor

**Question:** Why is my water heater taking longer and longer to heat?

**Answer:** In most cases it is **limescale**. Mineral deposits in the water harden onto the heating element every heating cycle, slowly insulating it from the water it is supposed to heat. The heater then runs longer to deliver the same temperature — using more electricity, overheating the element itself, and often making popping or crackling noises as water boils through the scale layer. Descaling plus a check of the element, thermostat and (on storage tanks) the sacrificial anode rod usually restores normal behaviour without replacing the unit.

## Fault Map: Symptom to Cause

| Symptom | Most likely causes |
|---|---|
| No hot water at all | Dead element, failed thermostat, tripped breaker, faulty on/off switch |
| Slow heating or lukewarm water | Limescale on the element, failing element, wrong thermostat setting |
| Popping/crackling noises | Scale layer on element or tank bottom boiling trapped water |
| Water leaking from the unit | Failed gasket or valve, corroded tank (replacement usually) |
| RCCB trips when heating | Earth leakage in a failing element, moisture inside the terminal box |
| Hot water runs out fast | Scaled element, broken dip tube, undersized tank for the household |

## What Repair & Descaling Includes

The published scope starts at **RM 180** and covers diagnosis plus the common remedial work:

1. **Fault diagnosis** — element continuity, thermostat operation, wiring and connection points are tested before anything is opened.
2. **Descaling** — mineral buildup is removed from the element and (for storage tanks) flushed from the tank interior.
3. **Anode rod check** — storage tanks carry a sacrificial anode that corrodes so the tank doesn't; a consumed anode is noted and replacement advised before the tank itself starts rusting.
4. **Component repair/replacement** — elements, thermostats, gaskets, valves and switches replaced where diagnosed faulty.
5. **Electrical safety checks** — earthing verified, terminal box sealed, RCCB behaviour tested after work.
6. **Leak source assessment** — if the leak traces to a corroded tank body rather than a fitting, you are told plainly: fittings are repairable, tanks are not.

## Instant vs Storage: What Breaks Differently

- **Instant (tankless) heaters** fail mostly at the element, flow sensor and pressure activator — and are very sensitive to inlet conditions. If your pressure is marginal, a heater may cut out even with nothing wrong inside it; check [low water pressure](/problems/low-water-pressure) symptoms or consider [pump installation](/services/plumbing/water-heater-and-pump-installation).
- **Storage heaters** fail gradually: scale builds up, the anode is consumed, then the tank corrodes. Regular descaling and anode checks are what separates a tank that lasts from one that leaks onto your ceiling.

## Repair or Replace?

- **Element, thermostat, valve or gasket fault:** repair is usually economical — these are wear parts.
- **Scaled but otherwise healthy heater:** descale first; replacement can wait.
- **Corroded storage tank leaking from the body:** replace the unit — tank repairs do not hold.
- **Unit still failing after multiple repairs:** at some point a new [storage heater installation](/services/water-heater/storage-tank-heater-installation) (from **RM 420**) or [instant heater installation](/services/water-heater/instant-water-heater-installation) (from **RM 250**) beats chasing faults on an aged unit.

Honest advice on this call is part of the diagnosis visit — you should never pay repair prices for a heater at the end of its life.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How often should a storage water heater be descaled and checked?
It depends on water conditions and usage, but scale builds continuously with each heating cycle, so periodic descaling with an anode check is standard maintenance for storage tanks. If you hear popping noises or heating time has stretched noticeably, that is your schedule arriving early.

### Can I flush or descale the tank myself?
A basic flush through the drain point is possible on many tanks, but effective descaling of the element and an anode inspection requires draining, opening and electrical isolation of the unit — a job that combines plumbing and electrical safety. Getting it wrong beside 240V and hot water is how people get hurt.

### Why did my RCCB start tripping right after my heater problem started?
A failing element often develops small cracks that let moisture reach live parts — the RCCB detects that leakage current and trips, exactly as designed. This is a repair-trigger warning, not an electrical nuisance: see [RCCB keeps tripping](/problems/rccb-tripping-kl) and have the heater checked before continuing to rearm the breaker.
