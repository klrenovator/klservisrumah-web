# CCTV System Health Check and Relocation in Kuala Lumpur and Selangor

**Direct answer:** A CCTV health check is a systematic on-site inspection of every camera, the recorder, hard disk, cabling, power supplies and app configuration — with the option to relocate the whole system to a new address. The published starting price is **from RM 220**.

This guide explains the [CCTV System Health Check & Relocation](/services/cctv/cctv-system-health-check-and-relocation) scope under [CCTV and security camera installation](/services/cctv). A CCTV system that is never verified often fails silently — it looks fine on the phone app until the day footage is actually needed.

## When This Service Is the Right Choice

Book a health check or relocation when:

- You moved house or office and want the existing system reinstalled properly at the new address.
- You bought or rented a property that came with cameras and you do not know their condition, passwords or history.
- Renovation work has shifted cameras, cut cables or changed what angles make sense.
- There are symptoms — a dead camera, blurry image, or gaps in recording. If live view works but footage is missing, the causes overlap with [CCTV not recording](/problems/cctv-not-recording) and [full storage wiping old footage](/problems/cctv-not-recording-storage-full), both of which the health check diagnoses on site.

If the system is fine and you only need wider coverage, a [single camera add-on](/services/cctv/single-camera-add-on) is the smaller, cheaper scope.

## What a Health Check Actually Covers

- **Every camera** — image clarity, lens and housing cleanliness, night-vision function, mount stability and weather sealing.
- **The recorder** — firmware condition, fan/dust, error logs and correct time/date stamps (wrong timestamps make footage hard to use as evidence).
- **The hard disk** — health status, capacity used, and real retention days at current settings.
- **Cabling and connectors** — corrosion on outdoor joints, crushed cable runs and power adapters running hot.
- **The app and users** — who has access, password strength, and notification settings that match how you actually use it.
- **Recording verification** — playback pulled from each channel, not just a live view glance.

## Typical Work Process

1. **Record the current configuration** — camera map, recorder settings and user access.
2. **Inspect and clean each camera and the recorder**, testing image and night mode.
3. **Test the hard disk and playback** on every channel.
4. **Re-aim cameras** to the owner's current priorities and re-secure loose mounts.
5. **Update the app setup** — remove stale users, refresh passwords, verify notifications.
6. **For relocations:** de-install safely, transport, re-cable and re-mount at the new site, then repeat the full verification.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Assuming "shows live view" equals "recording properly" — they fail independently.
- Keeping the previous owner's app accounts and unknown passwords on an inherited system.
- Re-aiming cameras without confirming the new angle still records and still identifies faces at night.
- Relocating without planning cable routes at the new address, then drilling twice.
- Ignoring corroded outdoor connectors until a rainy-season short kills the channel.

## Safety, Privacy and Maintenance

Work at height on soffits and poles follows proper access practice. Privacy angles are rechecked when cameras are re-aimed or relocated. After the visit, the realistic maintenance rhythm is simple: wipe lenses when the image dulls, glance at playback monthly, and book a check after any renovation or lightning-related power event. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### I bought a house with CCTV already installed. Can you take it over?
Yes. The visit verifies what works, factory-resets what is needed, and moves app access and passwords to you so no previous occupant retains view access.

### Can recordings from before the health check be recovered?
Usually that is not the service scope and depends on disk condition — expect honest confirmation of what the disk still holds rather than promises of recovery.

### How long does a health check take?
A typical 4–8 camera home system takes a few hours including cleaning, re-aiming and app work. Relocations naturally take longer depending on the new cable runs.
