# Bi-Weekly Terrace House Cleaning in KL & Selangor

A double-storey terrace house has more floor area than a condo, more staircases, and an outdoor edge that a high-rise unit does not have — porch, car park, back yard and the dust that comes with them. Fortnightly is the schedule most landed-home owners in Klang Valley settle on.

**Direct answer:** KL Servis Rumah cleans double-storey terrace houses every fortnight from **RM 250 per visit** across Kuala Lumpur and Selangor, covering the ground floor, first floor, staircase and outdoor areas, with a **24-hour re-clean satisfaction guarantee**.

## Why fortnightly suits landed homes

- Terrace houses usually have more space than daily life fills, so grime spreads slower per square foot than in a compact condo.
- Most owners already do light tidying between visits.
- A fortnightly visit is longer and more thorough than a weekly one, which suits homes where the staircase, porch and back area need real attention rather than a quick pass.
- It costs less per month than weekly while still preventing build-up.

Move to weekly if you have young children, multiple pets, an elderly parent at home, or the house sits directly on a busy road.

## What a fortnightly visit covers

**Ground floor**
- Living and dining areas: dusting, surfaces, furniture wiping
- Kitchen: counters, sink, hob exterior, cabinet fronts, appliance exteriors
- Powder room or downstairs bathroom in full
- Sweep, vacuum and mop throughout

**Staircase and first floor**
- Staircase treads, risers and handrail
- Bedrooms: dusting, bed-making, sills and skirting
- Upstairs bathrooms in full
- Vacuum and mop throughout

**Outdoor areas**
- Porch and car park sweeping
- Front gate area, entrance and reachable outdoor surfaces
- Back yard or wet kitchen sweep and tidy

**Not included as standard:** inside oven and fridge, ceiling fan blades, behind heavy furniture, exterior glass at height, grout scrubbing, pressure washing of driveways. Those belong in a [deep clean](/services/deep-cleaning/terrace-house-deep-clean-2-000-sqft) or [one-off spring clean](/services/cleaning/one-off-spring-clean).

## Typical fortnightly routine

1. **Top-down start** — upstairs bedrooms and bathrooms first, so dust travels downward.
2. **Staircase** — treads, risers, handrail and the corners that collect hair and dust.
3. **Ground floor living areas** — surfaces, furniture, floors.
4. **Kitchen and wet kitchen** — the longest single zone in most terrace houses.
5. **Outdoor** — porch, gate area, back yard sweep.
6. **Final pass** — bins, mopping, quick walk-through against the checklist.

## Common mistakes

- **Treating the outdoor area as free extra work.** Porch and back yard take real time. Include them in the scope so they are actually done.
- **Expecting a fortnightly clean to remove years of grout staining.** Book a [terrace house deep clean](/services/deep-cleaning/terrace-house-deep-clean-2-000-sqft) first, then maintain.
- **Ignoring the wet kitchen.** It is the greasiest room in most landed homes and needs to be named in the checklist.
- **Cleaning around a leak.** Recurring damp patches are a building issue, not a cleaning one. See [wall dampness and efflorescence repair](/services/waterproofing/wall-dampness-and-efflorescence-repair).
- **Letting bathroom limescale build between visits.** See [stubborn bathroom limescale](/problems/stubborn-bathroom-limescale).

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does a fortnightly terrace clean take?

A maintained 2,000 sqft double-storey home typically takes several hours. Team size and duration depend on layout, number of bathrooms and how much outdoor area is included.

### Can you clean the driveway and outdoor tiles?

Sweeping and reachable surface cleaning are included. Pressure washing of driveways and heavily stained outdoor tiles is a separate service, quoted after we see photos.

### Do you clean the wet kitchen?

Yes, when it is in the agreed checklist. Wet kitchens usually need more time than the main kitchen because of grease build-up.

### Can I switch between weekly and fortnightly?

Yes. Many households run fortnightly most of the year and switch to weekly during festive periods or school holidays.

### Do you clean the outside of upstairs windows?

Interior glass and reachable frames only. Exterior glass at height is excluded for safety.
