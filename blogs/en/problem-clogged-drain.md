# Clogged Kitchen or Bathroom Drain: Slow Water and Backflow

**Question:** Water drains slowly, sits in the sink or shower tray, or backs up when I run more than one tap. Is this a local clog, or is the main line failing?

**Answer:** A single slow sink or shower is usually grease in the kitchen P-trap and horizontal run, or hair in the bathroom trap. Water bubbling up from a second drain when you flush, or several drains backing up together, points to a shared waste line or a sagging pipe that collects debris. Mechanical snaking plus enzyme treatment is the safe first fix. Strong caustic drain openers can damage older PVC joints and are unsafe in enclosed Malaysian bathrooms.

This page is about **kitchen and bathroom waste lines that drain slowly**. A toilet that will not clear the bowl is a different fault — see [toilet not flushing](/problems/toilet-not-flushing). A toilet that is fully blocked and rising is [blocked drain toilet](/problems/blocked-drain-toilet).

## Why Drains Clog in Malaysian Homes

- **Kitchen grease** cools in the P-trap and the long horizontal run behind the cabinet.
- **Hair** mats in bathroom traps and the first bend of the shower waste.
- **Wet wipes and sanitary products** in toilet lines — they do not break down.
- **A broken or sagging pipe** creates a low point where debris settles even after you clear the trap.

Cooking oil poured “just this once” is the most common kitchen cause. It does not stay liquid in a 27 °C pipe.

## Local Clog or Shared Line?

| Sign | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Only the kitchen sink is slow | Grease in that trap or run |
| Only the shower tray holds water | Hair trap or shower waste |
| A second drain gurgles or rises when you use the first | Shared line partly blocked |
| Several drains back up at once | Main waste or inspection chamber |
| Sewage smell indoors | Trap dry, or waste leaking |

## What You Can Safely Do

1. Remove the sink strainer and clear obvious hair or food.
2. Use a plunger on a single fixture after sealing the overflow with a wet cloth.
3. Run hot water after washing up — not as a substitute for keeping grease out.
4. Skip caustic “drain opener” bottles on older PVC. They can soften joints and splash in a small bathroom.

If multiple drains fail together, stop pouring water and call — you may be filling a blocked main.

## What Professional Clearing Involves

1. **Manual snaking / rodding** of the trap and the first run.
2. **Enzyme treatment** that is safe for pipes, used after mechanical clearing.
3. **High-pressure water jetting** for stubborn kitchen grease lines.
4. **Camera inspection and pipe repair** when the line is sagging or broken.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are chemical drain openers safe?
Strong caustic chemicals can damage older PVC joints and are unsafe in enclosed spaces. Mechanical clearing plus enzymes lasts longer and is safer.

### How much to unblock a kitchen drain?
Manual snaking RM 150 to RM 250. High-pressure jetting RM 350 to RM 700 for a full kitchen line.

### How do I prevent kitchen clogs?
Never pour cooking grease down the sink. Use a mesh strainer. Run hot water and a monthly enzyme treatment.
