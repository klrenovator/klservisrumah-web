# Move-Out Condo Deep Cleaning in KL: Handover Checklist

**Question:** What does a move-out condo deep clean include?

**Answer:** A proper move-out clean covers bathrooms, kitchen, built-in cabinets, windows, floors, walls, switches and appliances so the unit is ready for landlord or management inspection. It is more detailed than weekly cleaning because it must remove grease, limescale, grout darkening, dust inside cabinets and leftover renovation or moving dirt.

This article is about **handover/move-out cleaning**. For newly renovated units, see [cement splatter and dust after renovation](/problems/cement-splatter-tile-clean) and [post-renovation cleaning](/services/post-renovation-cleaning).

## What Landlords Usually Check

| Area | What is inspected |
|---|---|
| Bathrooms | Limescale, mould, grout, toilet bowl, glass screens |
| Kitchen | Grease on cabinets, hob, hood, sink and tiles |
| Built-in wardrobes | Dust inside shelves, tracks and drawers |
| Floors | Stains, sticky patches, leftover tape and marks |
| Windows | Glass, tracks, sills and frames |
| Walls | Scuffs, nail holes, heavy stains |
| Appliances | Fridge, washing machine, fans and filters if included |

## Move-Out Cleaning Scope

A deep clean should include:

1. **Kitchen:** degrease cabinets, hob, hood, splashback tiles, sink and taps.
2. **Bathrooms:** descale shower glass, taps, toilet, floor and grout.
3. **Cabinets:** wipe inside and outside wardrobes, kitchen cabinets and drawers.
4. **Windows:** clean glass, tracks, sills and accessible frames.
5. **Floors:** vacuum, mop and remove sticky residue.
6. **Walls and switches:** wipe light switches, door handles and reachable scuff marks.
7. **Fixtures:** clean ceiling fan blades, aircond surface/casing and lighting where accessible.

## What May Need Separate Work

Move-out cleaning is not the same as repair. The following may need a separate quote:

- Painting over scuffs, nail holes or wall damage.
- Repairing cracked tiles, hollow tiles or grout.
- Removing cement splatter and paint after renovation.
- Clearing large rubbish or unwanted furniture.
- Cleaning exterior windows beyond safe reach.
- Treating mould caused by an active leak.

If the unit was renovated before handover, book [post-renovation cleaning](/services/post-renovation-cleaning) first because cement dust, silicone residue and protective film need a different process.

## Timing

A typical 800–1,200 sq ft condo can take several hours for one cleaner and less time with a team. Furnished units, heavy kitchen grease, and multiple bathrooms add time. Booking before the final inspection leaves time to touch up missed areas.

## How to Prepare

- Remove all personal belongings before the cleaning team arrives.
- Keep electricity and water connected.
- Note stains or damage that may need repair rather than cleaning.
- Inform the cleaner whether appliances are included.
- Point out fragile items or surfaces requiring special care.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is move-out cleaning the same as regular cleaning?
No. Move-out cleaning is more detailed and targets grease, limescale, inside cabinets, windows, tracks and handover-ready surfaces.

### Can cleaning remove wall scuffs?
Light marks may wipe off, but deep scuffs, nail holes and paint damage usually need patching and painting, not cleaning.
