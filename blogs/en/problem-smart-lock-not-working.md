# Smart Lock Not Responding or Battery Draining Fast

**Question:** My digital door lock has stopped responding — the keypad is dead, the app will not connect, or the batteries drain within days. How do I know if it can be repaired?

**Answer:** Most smart-lock failures are recoverable and come down to four things: a low or faulty battery, a lost pairing between the lock and the app, a failed motor module, or an installation that is slightly out of alignment. The first two are often fixable by the owner in minutes; the last two need a locksmith. Before assuming the lock is dead, always check the battery and whether the lock works from its physical keypad or key override — that tells you whether the problem is power, electronics, or mechanics.

## The Quick Checks Before You Call

1. **Check the batteries.** A fading battery causes intermittent response before total failure. Replace with fresh batteries of the correct type first.
2. **Try the physical override.** Most smart locks have a mechanical key or emergency power port. If the manual method works, the lock's mechanics are fine.
3. **Test the keypad directly.** If the keypad unlocks the door but the app does not, the problem is pairing or connectivity, not the lock.
4. **Listen for the motor.** A whir without movement, or no sound at all when triggered, points to the motor module.

## The Most Common Causes

| Cause | Typical clue |
|---|---|
| Low or faulty battery | Intermittent response, then nothing |
| App pairing lost | Works from keypad, not from phone |
| Motor module failure | Keypad responds but bolt does not move |
| Misaligned install | Bolt binds or struggles to throw |
| Firmware or connectivity issue | App shows offline or won't pair |

## What a Locksmith Does

A locksmith will typically:

- Test the power supply and replace batteries or battery pack.
- Re-pair the lock with the app and re-check Wi-Fi or Bluetooth connectivity.
- Remove the lock body, inspect the motor and gearbox, and replace the module if it has failed.
- Re-align the strike and bolt so the lock throws cleanly without binding.
- Restore the key or emergency override so you are never locked out by a battery failure.

### Why does my smart lock battery drain in days?
Usually a failing battery, a lock that keeps retrying to connect, or a motor under strain from a misaligned bolt. A locksmith can measure the draw and find the cause.

### Can a smart lock be repaired, or must it be replaced?
Most are repairable. Battery, pairing and motor issues can all be corrected; the lock only needs replacing if the board or housing itself is damaged.

### What happens if the batteries die while I'm outside?
Use the mechanical key or emergency power port. It is worth testing this override when the lock is fitted so you are not caught out later.
