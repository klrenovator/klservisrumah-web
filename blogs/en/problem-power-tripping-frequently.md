# Power Tripping Frequently in the House

**Question:** The main breaker or an RCD trips several times a day, often when I turn on one appliance. Is the board broken, or is something in the house leaking current?

**Answer:** Frequent trips are usually a faulty appliance, an overloaded circuit, water on a socket or in the ceiling, or an aged DB with weak breakers. The RCD is protecting you — never bypass it. Isolate circuits, test appliances one by one, then redistribute loads or replace the faulty breaker. A smell of burning, an immediate re-trip, or trips only after rain need a same-day visit from an ST-registered wireman.

This page is **whole-house or circuit tripping** (overload, short, weak breaker). A residual-current device that trips without an obvious appliance is covered in more depth at [RCCB tripping](/problems/rccb-tripping-kl).

## Common Causes in Klang Valley Homes

- A faulty appliance drawing excess current or shorting.
- Too many high-load appliances on one circuit.
- Water ingress on a socket or in the ceiling after rain.
- An aged DB box with weak breakers.

## How to Read the Trip

| Pattern | Likely cause |
|---|---|
| Trips when one appliance starts | That appliance or its dedicated circuit |
| Trips when several things run together | Overload — redistribute |
| Immediate re-trip in seconds | Active short or earth leak — stop resetting |
| Only after rain | Water on a fitting or in the ceiling |
| Burning smell at a socket or the DB | Isolate power and call |

## What You Can Do Safely

1. Note which breaker moved and what was switched on.
2. Unplug the last appliance and reset once.
3. Do not keep resetting an immediate trip.
4. Do not replace an RCD with a plain breaker.

## What Diagnosis Involves

1. Isolate each circuit to find the source.
2. Test appliances one by one.
3. Redistribute high-load appliances across circuits.
4. Replace faulty breakers or upgrade the DB box. A DB can be upgraded in a day without rewiring the whole house.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is a tripping RCD dangerous?
The RCD is protecting you. Never bypass it. Get the cause diagnosed within 24 hours.

### Can I upgrade the DB without rewiring the house?
Yes. The DB, breakers and RCDs can be upgraded in a single day without rewiring.
