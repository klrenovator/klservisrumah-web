Whether you are adding a fan to a new room or upgrading an old one, this guide covers what a professional installation includes and how to prepare.

## Before Installation: What to Prepare

- **Choose the right fan size** for the room — 42–48 inch for bedrooms, 52–56 inch for living rooms, 56–60 inch for large halls.
- **Confirm the ceiling type** — concrete slab, plaster ceiling, or gypsum false ceiling. Each affects the mounting method.
- **Check for an existing fan hook** — a previous fan installation may have left a hook; it will be inspected and reused only if it is solidly anchored.
- **Decide AC or DC** — AC fans use a wall regulator; DC fans use a remote or app. Make sure the wiring and location suit your choice.

## What Installation Includes

1. **Hook inspection** — the installer checks whether the ceiling has a solid anchor point. If not, a heavy-duty fan hook is installed and anchored to the structure above — never into plasterboard alone.
2. **Wiring** — the fan is wired to the existing fan circuit with correct connections. For AC fans, the regulator is checked or replaced; for DC fans, the receiver unit is fitted in the canopy.
3. **Mounting** — the fan bracket and motor are mounted level, with all fasteners tightened correctly.
4. **Blades and fittings** — blades are fitted securely; glass shades and light kits (if included) are installed.
5. **Swing test** — the fan is run at full speed and checked for wobble, vibration, and noise. Minor imbalances are corrected by swapping blades or using balancing weights.

## Why the Fan Hook Matters

The fan hook carries the entire weight of the fan plus the forces of spinning blades. A fan bolted to a thin ceiling board or a poorly anchored hook can pull loose — a known cause of accidents in Malaysian homes. Professional installers anchor hooks into the concrete slab or a solid beam. In condominiums with gypsum false ceilings, a steel support frame is fitted over the ceiling to transfer the load to the slab.

If your ceiling has no hook or you are unsure of its condition, our installer will install or replace it as part of the job — always quote this in advance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you install a fan I bought from a shop?

Yes. We install fans from any retailer, and we also supply popular brands (KDK, Panasonic, Fanco, Khind, Acorn) if you prefer a one-stop quote.

### Do I need a wall regulator for a DC fan?

No. DC fans use a remote control or app and do not need a wall regulator. If you are replacing an AC fan that uses a regulator, the wiring may need a small adjustment — our installer handles this.

### How long does a new fan installation take?

About one hour for a straightforward job on a concrete ceiling with existing wiring. Allow longer for false ceilings, new hooks, or new wiring.

### What if my room has no wiring for a ceiling fan?

We can install a new fan point, running the wiring from the nearest circuit and adding a switch. This is quoted separately and combined with the installation for one visit.

### Is a swing test really necessary?

Yes. A fan that is not balanced can wobble, stress the hook, and produce annoying noise. The swing test confirms the fan runs smoothly at all speeds before we leave.
