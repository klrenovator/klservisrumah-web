# Leaking Bathroom: Wet Floor Below and Damp Walls

**Question:** The floor below my upstairs bathroom is wet or damp, or the wall next to the bathroom shows brown stains and bubbling paint. Do the tiles have to be hacked up?

**Answer:** Not usually. A leaking bathroom is one of four things: a cracked floor waterproofing membrane, failed silicone around the shower screen or bathtub, a concealed pipe inside the wall, or broken tile grout letting water into the slab. Pressure-testing isolates pipe versus slab. In most cases the slab can be sealed from below with high-pressure PU grouting — no tile damage. Full hacking and a new membrane is only needed when the membrane has completely failed.

This page is the **source diagnosis** for a bathroom that is leaking into the structure. ## How Water Leaves a Malaysian Bathroom

- **Cracked floor membrane** — the hidden sheet under the tiles has aged or been punctured.
- **Failed silicone** at the shower screen, bathtub or wall-floor junction.
- **Concealed pipe leak** inside a wet-wall (supply or waste).
- **Open grout lines** letting shower water into the screed and slab.

Active dripping onto the ceiling below is a high-urgency job. Published guidance for active leaks in the Klang Valley is same-day attendance, typically arriving within 45 to 90 minutes.

## Pipe Leak or Slab Leak?

| Sign | More likely |
|---|---|
| Drip continues even when nobody uses the bathroom | Concealed supply pipe |
| Drip only during or after a shower | Membrane, grout or silicone |
| Wall next to the bathroom bubbling, floor below dry | Wet-wall pipe or splash leak |
| Brown stain directly under the shower tray | Membrane or tray seal |
| Expanding wet patch week on week | Active leak — do not wait |

## What You Can Do Immediately

1. Stop using the upstairs shower if the ceiling below is dripping.
2. Photograph the wet patch at the same time each day so you can see whether it is growing.
3. Check shower-screen and bathtub silicone for gaps or black mouldy failure.
4. Do not keep repainting the downstairs ceiling — the water is still coming.

If several fixtures are involved or you smell sewage, treat it as a waste-line issue and see [clogged drain](/problems/clogged-drain) as well.

## What Diagnosis and Repair Involves

1. **Pressure-test the plumbing** to isolate a pipe leak from a slab leak.
2. **PU grout the concrete slab from below** when the leak is through hairline cracks or a local membrane breach.
3. **Re-silicone** shower screens and bathtub joints when that is the only path.
4. **Full membrane re-do with tile hacking** only when the membrane has failed across the wet area.
