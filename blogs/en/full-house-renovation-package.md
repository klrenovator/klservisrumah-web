# Full House Renovation Package in KL & Selangor

A full house renovation is a large project that touches every part of your home — structure, plumbing, electrical, tiling, painting, and cabinetry. KL Servis Rumah provides coordinated full house renovation packages across Kuala Lumpur and Selangor, from RM 22,000.

This guide explains what a full house renovation package includes and what to consider before you start.

## What a Full House Renovation Includes

A complete renovation package typically covers:

- **Planning and design** — scoping the work and layout.
- **Demolition and preparation** — removing old finishes and preparing surfaces.
- **Structural work** — walls, partitions, and openings as required.
- **Plumbing** — relocating or replacing pipes and fixtures.
- **Electrical** — wiring, points, and lighting.
- **Tiling and flooring** — floors and walls.
- **Painting** — interior finishes.
- **Cabinetry and carpentry** — kitchen, wardrobes, and built-ins.

## Key Considerations

- **Budget** — a full renovation is a major investment; define your budget clearly.
- **Timeline** — plan for a realistic duration, with allowances for contingencies.
- **Design** — decide on the layout and style before work begins to avoid changes.
- **Approvals** — check strata or authority requirements where relevant.
- **Coordination** — one responsible contractor coordinating all trades avoids delays.
