## Modern Design Profiles Available

1. **Horizontal Slat Minimalist:** Clean, horizontal flat bars or square hollow sections spaced 3.5 to 4 inches apart. Highly popular in modern Scandinavian and contemporary terrace homes.
2. **Vertical Slimline:** Classic vertical bars with concealed screw fixings matching window mullions.
3. **Geometric Grid:** Modern lattice or intersecting grid patterns for feature windows.
4. **Emergency Fire Escape Hatch:** Hinged opening panel with padlock bracket integrated seamlessly into the design to allow quick exit during a fire emergency.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is an emergency escape hatch required on window grilles?
Fixed grilles can trap occupants inside during a fire. Malaysian Bomba safety recommendations advise having at least one hinged emergency escape hatch with a padlock in every bedroom grille.

### How long does custom fabrication take?
From site measurement to final on-site installation typically takes 7 to 10 working days, including custom cutting, welding, and factory powder coating.

### Can you install grilles over existing aluminium sliding windows?
Yes, our grilles are custom fabricated to mount neatly inside the interior window reveal or on the external masonry face without interfering with existing sliding or casement windows.
