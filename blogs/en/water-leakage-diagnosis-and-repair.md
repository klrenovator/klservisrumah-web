Question: How do I find a hidden water leak without tearing up my house?

## Signs You Have a Hidden Water Leak

- **Higher water bill** — a sudden jump with no change in usage.
- **Damp or stained patches** on walls, ceilings, or floors.
- **Mould or a musty smell** in a specific area.
- **Water meter spinning** when all taps are off.
- **Peeling paint or bubbling wallpaper** near pipes.
- **Cracks in the floor** or a sagging area near a water line.

If you notice any of these, the sooner the leak is found, the less damage it causes.

## How Hidden Leaks Are Found Without Hacking

Modern leak detection avoids unnecessary damage:

1. **Pressure testing** — the system is pressurised to confirm a leak exists and isolate which zone it is in.
2. **Acoustic detection** — sensitive listening devices pick up the sound of water escaping through a pipe.
3. **Thermal imaging** — a thermal camera shows the cooler, damp area created by the leak.
4. **Dye testing** — for specific fixtures (toilets, overflow pipes), a safe dye confirms the source.
5. **Tracer gas** — in some cases, a trace gas is used to pinpoint the exact point.

Only after the location is confirmed do we open the affected area — keeping damage to the minimum.

## Repair Options

Once the leak is located, the repair depends on the cause:

- **Pinhole leak in a pipe** — the damaged section is cut out and replaced.
- **Leaking joint** — the joint is re-sealed or replaced.
- **Cracked pipe** — the cracked section is replaced.
- **Waterproofing failure** — the affected waterproofing layer is repaired or reapplied.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is my water bill high but I can't find a leak?

This is a classic sign of a hidden leak in the private pipework between the meter and your fixtures. Turn off all taps and check if the meter still moves; if it does, water is escaping somewhere in your system.

### Does finding a leak require breaking floors and walls?

Not usually. Modern detection methods locate the leak precisely first, so we only open the smallest area needed. This is the main advantage of professional detection over guess-and-dig.

### Can a leak damage the structure of my home?

Yes. Left untreated, water can rot timber, weaken plaster, encourage mould, and damage electrical fittings. Fixing leaks early saves money and protects your home.

### How long does leak detection take?

A straightforward leak can be located within a few hours. Repairs depend on the extent of the leak and access.
