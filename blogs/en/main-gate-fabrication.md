## Swing Gate vs Sliding Gate Fabrication

1. **Swing Gates (Single / Double Leaf):** Engineered with internal diagonal bracing to prevent leaf sag over time. Fitted with greasable heavy-duty ball bearing barrel hinges anchored securely into reinforced brick or concrete pillars.
2. **Sliding & Trackless Folding Gates:** Fitted with precision machined bottom grooved wheels with sealed bearings and top anti-sway guide rollers, engineered to interface smoothly with [sliding autogate motors](/services/autogate/sliding-autogate).

## Our Gate Fabrication Process

- **On-Site Pillar & Driveway Survey:** We inspect existing pillar structural integrity, measure gate opening widths with laser levels, and assess driveway slope clearances.
- **Structural Engineering:** Gate frames are welded using minimum 1.6mm to 2.0mm wall thickness steel tubing to eliminate bowing under wind load.
- **Zinc Phosphate Priming & Oven-Baked Powder Coating:** Complete degreasing, sand-blasting, and application of zinc-rich anti-rust primer before oven curing at 200°C.
- **Autogate Motor Bracket Pre-Welding:** We weld reinforced motor mounting plates directly to the gate frame in the workshop, preventing on-site paint burning during autogate motor installation.

## Common Gate Problems We Prevent

- **Gate Dragging or Scraping:** Prevented by using oversized bearing hinges and laser-aligned hinge pins.
- **Weld Seam Rusting:** Prevented by 100% full-penetration continuous MIG welds rather than spot welds.
- **Hinge Breakage:** See our guide on [loose metal gate hinges](/problems/loose-metal-gate-hinges).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How wide can a custom driveway gate be made?
We fabricate residential driveway gates ranging from 10 feet wide (standard terrace single porch) up to 24 feet wide (double porch or bungalow entrances), utilizing multi-leaf folding or heavy-duty sliding track designs.

### Will the new gate be ready for autogate installation?
Yes. Every gate we fabricate is designed with reinforced motor mounting plates, center locking brackets, and cable conduit sleeves ready for instant [single swing](/services/autogate/single-swing-autogate), double swing, or sliding motor attachment.

### How long does gate fabrication take?
Workshop fabrication, galvanising, powder coating, and on-site mounting typically take 10 to 14 working days from final site measurement.
