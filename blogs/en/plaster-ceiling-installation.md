# Plaster Ceiling Installation in KL & Selangor

A plaster ceiling gives a home a clean, modern finish while hiding wiring, ducts, and pipes. Whether you want a simple flat ceiling or a tiered L-box design with concealed lighting, KL Servis Rumah provides professional plaster ceiling installation across Kuala Lumpur and Selangor, from RM 10 per sq ft.

This guide explains the plaster ceiling options available, the installation process, and what affects the cost.

## Plaster Ceiling Options

- **Flat plaster ceiling** — a clean, smooth ceiling for a minimalist look.
- **Tiered & L-box ceiling** — layered designs that add depth and often include a concealed light trough.
- **Cove & curved ceiling** — softer, curved transitions for a premium feel.
- **Ceiling repair & re-skim** — resurfacing existing ceilings that are cracked or worn.

We work with gypsum board and plaster to create smooth, even surfaces ready for painting.

## The Installation Process

1. **Design and measurement** — we measure the area and finalise the design with you.
2. **Framework** — metal framing is installed at the correct level and spacing.
3. **Board installation** — gypsum boards are fixed to the framework.
4. **Jointing and skim** — joints are taped, filled, and skimmed smooth.
5. **Sanding and finishing** — the surface is sanded for a smooth paint-ready finish.
6. **Concealed lighting (optional)** — light troughs and wiring are prepared as required.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between a flat and L-box ceiling?

A flat ceiling is one smooth surface. An L-box (or tiered) ceiling has stepped layers, usually with a concealed trough for indirect lighting, adding depth and a modern look.

### Do you install the concealed lighting too?

We prepare the ceiling structure and troughs for concealed lighting. Electrical wiring for the lights is done by our licensed electrical team — see our [lighting design and installation](/services/lighting) service.

### How long does a plaster ceiling take to install?

A standard living-room ceiling is usually completed within a few days, depending on area and design. Drying and finishing times depend on humidity.

### Can a plaster ceiling hide wiring and pipes?

Yes. One of the main advantages is concealing wiring, ducts, and plumbing above the ceiling while keeping access points where needed.
