# CCTV Camera Not Recording or Footage Missing

**Question:** My cameras show a live picture, but when I try to play back there are no recordings, or files are missing and corrupted. What is going wrong?

**Answer:** When live view works but recording does not, the cameras and power are usually fine — the fault is in the recording side. The most common causes are a failed or failing hard disk drive (HDD), a recording schedule that was switched off or overwritten, wrong retention settings deleting footage too early, a network fault that drops the feed to the recorder, or camera firmware that has stopped writing correctly. The fix is to check the HDD health, re-confirm the recording schedule and retention, and test each camera's recording path — work a CCTV technician can usually complete in one visit.

## Why a System Stops Recording

| Cause | Typical clue |
|---|---|
| HDD failure | No playback, or recorder reports a drive error |
| Recording schedule off | Live view fine, but nothing ever records |
| Retention set too short | Older footage missing, recent clips present |
| Network fault | Some or all cameras drop out of the recorder |
| Power supply to recorder | Recorder restarts or loses recordings |
| Camera firmware issue | A single camera stops recording while others work |

## What to Check Before You Call

1. **Confirm the recorder is on** and its power supply is healthy — a restarting recorder loses recordings.
2. **Check the recording schedule** in the recorder or app. A wrong time or an accidentally disabled schedule is a common silent cause.
3. **Look at the HDD status.** Most recorders show a drive health or error indicator.
4. **Test playback from one camera** to see whether the fault affects all cameras or just one.
5. **Note when it started** — after a power cut, a firmware update, or a network change.

## What a CCTV Technician Does

A technician will typically:

- Test the HDD and replace it if it has failed, transferring or attempting recovery of important footage first.
- Reconfigure the recording schedule and retention so footage is kept for the intended period.
- Update camera and recorder firmware where that is the cause.
- Check network connections and power to the recorder.
- Verify every camera is actually recording by playing back a test clip from each.

### Why does live view work but nothing records?
The cameras are fine; the recording side is at fault — usually the HDD, the recording schedule, or retention settings. A technician can isolate which in one visit.

### How long should a CCTV system keep footage?
That depends on HDD size, camera count and resolution, but most homes keep between two weeks and a month of footage. Too-short retention often explains "missing" older recordings.

### How do I know if my HDD is failing?
Recorders usually show a drive error, missing playback, or beeping. A technician can run a drive health check to confirm.
