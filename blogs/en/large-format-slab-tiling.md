# What Makes Large-Format Tiling Different?

**Direct answer:** Large-format tiles need a flatter substrate, fuller adhesive coverage and more controlled handling than standard tiles. Levelling clips can align neighbouring edges, but they cannot correct a wavy floor or replace correct trowelling, back-buttering, movement joints and safe slab lifting.

## Practical Planning Notes

A quotation should define the measured work area or number of points, the preparation included, who supplies the finish product or equipment, and how concealed defects will be handled. Product names alone are not a complete specification: model, dimensions, colour or output, compatible accessories and installation method all affect the result. For **large-format slab tiling in kl & selangor**, the survey should record substrate flatness and strength, tile dimensions and layout, adhesive coverage and open time, and movement joints edges and access.

Sequence matters when several trades share the area. Complete leak repairs and wet work before sensitive finishes; coordinate framing, wiring, pipework and access panels before surfaces are closed; and protect completed work from following trades. A sample, layout drawing or one-point trial is useful when appearance, alignment, brightness, texture or control behaviour cannot be judged from a catalogue.

The handover should be functional, not visual only. The installer should inspect fixing or adhesion, edges and penetrations, operation under normal use, and any serviceable components. Keep the product model, colour or batch reference and care instructions. These records make future replacement or local repair more accurate.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Laying slabs over an uneven screed

- Dot-and-dab adhesive leaving voids

- Starting without planning cuts at doors and drains

- Walking or grouting before adhesive cures


Do not bypass structural, electrical, waterproofing or product requirements to save preparation time. Stop work if hidden moisture, unsafe wiring, weak support or moving cracks are discovered; diagnose these conditions before covering them.

## Frequently Asked Questions


### Why do large tiles crack or sound hollow?
Voids, movement, weak substrate or inadequate coverage can leave unsupported areas. Diagnosis should distinguish bond failure from substrate cracking.


### Are levelling clips enough?
No. They reduce edge lippage between adjacent tiles but do not flatten the substrate.


### Can large slabs be installed in a bathroom?
Yes, with suitable waterproofing, falls, drainage cuts and slip-conscious tile selection.
