# Hairline Cracks on Painted Walls: Settlement or Something Worse?

**Question:** Fine, spidery cracks have appeared on my painted walls — often around window frames, door frames or the ceiling-wall junction. Does this mean the house is structurally damaged?

**Answer:** Almost never. Cosmetic hairline cracks are normal building settlement and paint-film movement, especially in the first two to five years and during Malaysian humidity swings. Real structural cracks are usually much wider (5 mm+), diagonal across a large area, and getting worse. The lasting repair for hairlines is to open the crack slightly, fill with flexible acrylic filler, add fibreglass mesh on larger lines, skim, prime and repaint with a flexible acrylic — not rigid putty that pops out in months.

This article is about **cracks in the painted wall film and skim**. It is not [peeling paint](/problems/peeling-paint-malaysia), not [peeling skim coat](/problems/peeling-skim-coat), and not [cracked ceiling joints](/problems/cracked-ceiling-joints).

## Why Hairlines Appear in Malaysian Homes

- **Normal settlement** is worst in the first 2–5 years as the frame and brickwork find their load path.
- **Skim coat applied too thick or too fast** shrinks and crazes under the paint.
- **Temperature and humidity movement** opens the same lines every monsoon and closes them in dry spells.
- **Underlying plaster shrinkage** shows first at openings — windows, doors and AC box-outs.

## Cosmetic vs Structural — a Quick Read

| Sign | Usually means |
|---|---|
| Thinner than a credit card, random or following the skim | Cosmetic hairline |
| Around frames and ceiling junctions only | Movement at a joint |
| Same line reopens after a rigid-putty fill | Wrong filler, not a bigger problem |
| Wider than 1 mm and growing | Needs a proper crack repair, not just paint |
| 5 mm+, diagonal, stepped through brick or lintel | Structural — inspect before decorating |

Diagonal, progressive cracks across a large wall should be inspected before anyone skims over them.

## What You Can Safely Check

1. Measure width against a coin edge. Hairlines are typically under 1 mm.
2. Mark the ends with a pencil and date. Recheck after the next heavy rain week.
3. Press along the line. Hollow or loose skim is a skim failure, not just a hairline — see [uneven wall surface](/problems/uneven-wall-surface-skim).

Do not force rigid cement putty into a moving joint. It will crack again and look worse under a new topcoat.

## What a Proper Repair Involves

1. Widen the crack slightly so filler can key.
2. Fill with **flexible acrylic filler**, not rigid polyfilla alone.
3. Embed **fibreglass mesh tape** on longer or repeating lines.
4. Skim a thin plaster coat over the repair so the mesh does not telegraph.
5. Prime and repaint with a flexible acrylic topcoat.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do hairline cracks mean structural damage?
Almost never. Cosmetic hairlines are settlement and film movement. Structural cracks are usually 5 mm+, diagonal and progressive.

### Can I fill them with regular putty?
Not for a lasting result. Rigid putty pops out within months. Flexible acrylic filler plus mesh stays sealed as the wall moves.

### How much to fix hairlines in a whole room?
Typical room-level crack repair + skim + primer + repaint runs RM 500 to RM 1,200 depending on wall area and paint grade.

### Will they come back after monsoon?
They can if the filler is rigid. A flexible system is designed to move with humidity.
