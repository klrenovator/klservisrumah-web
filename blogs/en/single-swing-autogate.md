## Why Choose a Single Swing Autogate?

- **Space-Efficient:** Perfect for homes with a single car porch bay or asymmetric gate layout (e.g. 1 large motorized leaf + 1 small manual pedestrian gate).
- **Lower Upfront & Maintenance Cost:** With only one motor arm, replacement and service costs are substantially lower than dual-leaf systems.
- **Smooth & Silent Operation:** Modern 24V DC screw-drive motors operate with minimal mechanical vibration compared to older AC systems.
- **Reliable Emergency Power:** The DC actuator operates efficiently on a 12V 7AH backup battery during TNB blackouts.

## Common Issues with Single Swing Gates

Because a single wide gate leaf concentrates all torque on one pillar, proper installation is critical:

- **Hinge Sagging:** Wide single leaves exert heavy leverage. If the pillar hinge weakens, the gate drags on the floor. Our team reinforces hinge brackets with [welding services](/services/welding).
- **Motor Overload:** Incorrect motor arm bracket geometry causes strain on the linear screw. We calculate exact mounting pivot geometry before drilling.
- **Sensor Misalignment:** Rain or garden growth blocking optical sensors. Check our guide on [autogate not closing](/problems/autogate-not-closing) for quick troubleshooting.
