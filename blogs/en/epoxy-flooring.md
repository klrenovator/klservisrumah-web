## Where Is Epoxy Flooring Used?

- **Garages and car porches** — tough and easy to clean.
- **Balconies** — seamless and water-resistant.
- **Offices and showrooms** — a clean, modern finish.
- **Warehouses and workshops** — heavy-duty protection.
- **Retail and food areas** — hygienic and easy to maintain.

## Why Choose Epoxy Flooring?

- **Durable** — resists wear, impact, and chemicals.
- **Seamless** — no joints for dirt and water to collect.
- **Easy to clean** — a smooth surface that wipes down easily.
- **Water-resistant** — protects the concrete beneath.
- **Customisable** — available in many colours and finishes.

## The Installation Process

1. **Surface preparation** — the concrete is cleaned, ground, and any cracks repaired.
2. **Primer** — a primer is applied to help the epoxy bond.
3. **Epoxy application** — the epoxy is applied by trowel, roller, or self-levelling.
4. **Broadcast or topcoat (as chosen)** — flake or quartz is broadcast, or a topcoat is applied.
5. **Curing and finishing** — the floor is allowed to cure for a hard, durable finish.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is epoxy flooring suitable for outdoor use?

Epoxy is best used on protected or covered areas such as car porches and balconies. For areas exposed to direct sunlight and heavy rain, we recommend systems suited to UV and weather.

### How long does an epoxy floor last?

A properly installed epoxy floor lasts many years, depending on traffic and maintenance. Heavy-duty systems last longer in demanding areas.

### Can epoxy be applied over old concrete?

Yes, if the concrete is cleaned, sound, and properly prepared. We grind and repair the surface before applying epoxy.

### How long does installation take and how long to cure?

A standard floor is usually installed within a few days. The epoxy needs time to cure fully before heavy use — we will advise on the timeline.
