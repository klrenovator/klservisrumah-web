# Weekly Condo Cleaning in KL: What RM 180 Covers

A 1,000 sqft three-bedroom condo is the most common unit type in Kuala Lumpur, and weekly cleaning is the most common schedule for it. Here is exactly what that looks like, what it costs, and when weekly is more than you need.

Each visit covers the kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms and common areas, performed by the same background-checked cleaner on a fixed day, with a **24-hour re-clean satisfaction guarantee**.

## Is weekly right for your unit?

Weekly makes sense when:

- Two or more adults work full time and nobody has a free half-day.
- There are young children, or pets that shed.
- The unit faces a main road or nearby construction and dust settles fast.
- Floors are light-coloured tile or vinyl that shows every footprint.
- You entertain often, or work from home and cannot live with clutter.

Fortnightly is usually enough for a single occupant, a couple who tidy daily, or a unit that is empty on weekdays. If you are unsure, start weekly for a month, then step down — it is easier than starting behind.

## What a weekly visit covers

**Kitchen**
- Counters, sink and tap, hob exterior and splashback
- Cabinet fronts, appliance exteriors, dining table
- Bin emptied and liner replaced

**Bathrooms**
- Toilet, basin, taps, mirror
- Shower area and screen, floor scrubbed
- Restock and tidy of visible items

**Bedrooms and living areas**
- Dusting of surfaces, sills, skirting and reachable fixtures
- Beds made, linen changed if you leave a fresh set out
- Vacuum and mop throughout
- Sofa cushions straightened, visible clutter grouped neatly

**Not included as standard:** inside oven and fridge, ceiling fan blades, behind heavy furniture, exterior glass, grout scrubbing, hard-water descaling. These sit in a [deep clean](/services/deep-cleaning) or a [one-off spring clean](/services/cleaning/one-off-spring-clean).

## Common mistakes

- **Booking weekly cleaning to fix built-up grime.** Weekly maintains a clean home; it does not reverse years of limescale. Start with a deep clean, then maintain.
- **Not clearing surfaces.** A cleaner who has to move forty items off a counter is spending your paid time on tidying, not cleaning.
- **Skipping the checklist conversation.** The unspoken expectation is the one that gets missed.
- **Expecting bathroom mould to disappear permanently.** If mould returns within days, the cause is ventilation or waterproofing. See [mould in the bathroom after cleaning](/problems/mold-in-bathroom-after-cleaning).
- **Booking a normal clean when moving out.** Landlord inspections need a [condo move-out deep clean](/services/deep-cleaning/condo-move-out-deep-clean-1-000-sqft).

For a maintained 1,000 sqft unit, a standard visit is typically a few hours with one cleaner. The first visit usually takes longer.

No. Most clients arrange a key, access card or family member. We confirm the access method before the first visit.

### Can I change my regular day?

Yes, with reasonable notice. We will confirm availability for your assigned cleaner on the new day.

### Can you do laundry and ironing?

These can be added as agreed extras. They take time away from cleaning, so we scope them into the quote rather than squeezing them in.

### What happens if I travel for a month?

Pause the schedule and tell us your return date. Many clients book a single catch-up clean on return rather than restarting cold.
