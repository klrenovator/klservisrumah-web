# DB Box Upgrade & Rewiring in Kuala Lumpur & Selangor

A DB box (distribution board) upgrade starts from RM 2,200 in KL and Selangor, and a full house rewiring is quoted after a site visit. The upgrade replaces old fuse boxes with a modern MCB + RCCB board that protects your family from electric shock and your home from electrical fires.

Many homes in Kuala Lumpur and Selangor still run on 20-to-40-year-old electrical systems: rewireable fuse boards, ungrounded two-pin sockets, and circuits sized for a time before air conditioners, instant water heaters, and induction cookers were common. This guide explains when an upgrade is needed, what rewiring involves, and how to plan the work.

## When Do You Need a DB Box Upgrade?

Consider upgrading if your home shows any of these signs:

- **The DB box uses fuse wire** — rewireable fuses provide no protection against earth leakage and react slowly to overloads.
- **No RCCB or ELCB** — without an earth-leakage device, a fault in an appliance can deliver a lethal shock instead of tripping the circuit.
- **Tripping under normal load** — if the main breaker trips when the kettle and air conditioner run together, the board or wiring is undersized. See our guide on [RCCB tripping in KL homes](/problems/rccb-tripping-kl).
- **The box is warm, buzzing, or discoloured** — these are signs of loose connections or overloaded terminals.
- **You're renovating or extending** — adding circuits for a new kitchen, air conditioners, or an EV charger is the right time to upgrade the board.

## What a DB Box Upgrade Involves

A professional upgrade includes:

1. **Load audit** — your electrician calculates the total connected load, plans circuit distribution, and selects correct MCB and RCCB ratings.
2. **Itemised quote** — the scope, breakers, box, and labour are confirmed in writing before work starts.
3. **Power coordination** — for landed homes, TNB or a registered supply contractor isolates the incoming supply; for condominiums, building management coordinates the shutdown.
4. **Board replacement** — the old fuse board is removed and the new MCB + RCCB board is installed, with each circuit labelled.
5. **Testing** — insulation resistance, earth-loop impedance, and RCCB trip time are tested, and you receive a written test record.

## Full vs Partial Rewiring

If the wiring itself is old, damaged, or undersized, a DB box upgrade alone is not enough.

**Partial rewiring** covers specific circuits — for example, re-running the kitchen circuit to handle an induction cooker, or adding a dedicated circuit for an air conditioner. It is a good option when the rest of the installation is sound.

**Full rewiring** replaces the wiring throughout the property, including socket and lighting circuits, and is recommended when:

- The cables are fabric-insulated or in poor condition (common in pre-1970s homes).
- The property has no earth wiring in socket circuits.
- You are doing a full renovation and the walls will be hacked or skimmed anyway.
- Multiple circuits are overloaded and failing regularly.

## Planning Tips for Rewiring

- **Do it with renovation** — if you are renovating, rewire before plastering, painting, and tiling. Cutting into finished walls costs more and makes a mess.
- **Phase it if you stay at home** — full rewiring in an occupied home can be phased room by room so the family still has lighting and power at night.
- **Plan for the future** — while the walls are open, add circuits for appliances you may buy later, such as an EV charger or extra air conditioners.
- **Keep records** — a labelled DB box and a written test record make future work faster and help when selling the property.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between MCB and RCCB?

An MCB (miniature circuit breaker) protects a circuit from overload and short circuit. An RCCB (residual current circuit breaker) detects earth leakage — current flowing through a person or a fault — and cuts the power in milliseconds. Modern boards use both: RCCB for shock protection, MCB for overload protection.

### How long does a DB box upgrade take?

A straightforward board swap is usually a one-day job. Full rewiring of a terrace house typically takes several days to a few weeks depending on scope and whether the home is occupied.

### Do I need approval to upgrade my DB box?

In condominiums, yes — building management usually requires an electrical contractor's approval form. For landed homes, the work itself is carried out by a registered wireman; major supply alterations are coordinated with TNB.

### Can I stay in the house during rewiring?

Yes, with proper phasing. We plan circuit-by-circuit so essential services — lighting and a few sockets — are restored each evening.

### Will rewiring raise my electricity bill?

No. Rewiring does not change consumption; it makes the installation safer and more reliable. You may even notice fewer trips and steadier voltage on dedicated circuits.
