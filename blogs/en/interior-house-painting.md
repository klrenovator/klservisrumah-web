# Interior House Painting in KL & Selangor

Interior painting is one of the fastest, most cost-effective ways to refresh your home. KL Servis Rumah provides interior house painting across Kuala Lumpur and Selangor, covering living rooms, bedrooms, hallways, and ceilings with low-odour, washable paint.

In Malaysia's humid climate, interior walls are prone to stains, mould, and peeling — especially in kitchens, bathrooms, and air-conditioned rooms. A proper interior paint job includes preparation and sealing so the fresh colour lasts.

## What Is Included in Interior Painting

Our interior service covers:

- **Walls** — living rooms, bedrooms, corridors, and feature walls.
- **Ceilings** — repainting or freshening up ceilings and cornice areas.
- **Wardrobes and doors (optional)** — on request, matching internal woodwork.
- **Skirting and architraves** — crisp, even edges with quality masking.

We use low-odour, low-VOC washable paints (for example Nippon Odour-less, Nippon Spot-less, or Dulux EasyClean), which dry quickly in high humidity and wipe clean easily — ideal for homes with children or pets.

## The Interior Painting Process

1. **Masking and protection** — furniture, floors, and switches are covered so nothing gets painted accidentally.
2. **Surface preparation** — cracks, nail holes, and marks are filled and sanded smooth.
3. **Primer or sealer** — applied to problem areas (stains, patches, and new plaster) for good adhesion.
4. **Two topcoats** — even, rich coverage with professional rollers and brushes.
5. **Clean handover** — covers are removed, the space is vacuumed, and we inspect together.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you paint over existing paint?

Yes. If the existing paint is sound, we clean, sand lightly, and repaint. If it is peeling or chalky, we remove the loose paint and prepare the surface first — see our [peeling paint guide](/problems/peeling-paint-malaysia).

### Is the paint safe for a home with children?

Yes. We offer low-odour, low-VOC washable paints that are safe for families and dry quickly.

### Do you move furniture?

We cover and protect furniture in place. For larger jobs, we can advise on moving large pieces before we start.

### Do you paint ceilings as well?

Yes. Ceiling repainting is available and often done together with walls for a consistent finish.
