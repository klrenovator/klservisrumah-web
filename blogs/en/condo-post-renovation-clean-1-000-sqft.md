# Condo Post-Renovation Clean in KL (1,000 sqft)

A condo renovation is contained but not clean. Even a kitchen-only job pushes fine cement dust through the air-conditioning, into wardrobe interiors and along every window track in the unit. Add grout haze on the new backsplash, silicone smears around the sink, and paint flecks on skirting, and a normal clean will not get you to move-in condition.

**Direct answer:** KL Servis Rumah cleans 1,000 sqft condos after renovation from **RM 650** across Kuala Lumpur and Selangor. Book **3–7 days after your contractor finishes**, before furniture arrives. The work carries a **7-day spot-check free re-clean guarantee**.

## When to book

- **After a kitchen or bathroom renovation** — the two jobs that generate the most tile dust and silicone residue.
- **After a full unit refit** — flooring, carpentry, painting and rewiring together.
- **Before furniture and appliance delivery** — cabinets and wardrobes can be cleaned inside while empty.
- **Before handover to a tenant** — an ex-renovation unit photographs and inspects badly with residue left on glass.
- **3–7 days after the contractor finishes**, not the same day. Paint, grout and silicone need to cure.

## What is covered

- Construction debris, offcuts, packaging and protective film bagged and removed
- Industrial HEPA vacuum through floors, skirting, cabinet interiors, wardrobes and window tracks
- Paint splatter removed from floors, skirting, glass and fittings
- Silicone smears scraped and dissolved around sinks, counters, showers and window frames
- Grout haze on new tiles treated with pH-balanced acidic cleaner
- Sticker and adhesive residue removed from glass, aluminium and appliances
- New aluminium windows, tracks, sliding doors and balcony glass cleaned
- Kitchen: inside cabinets and drawers, hob area, backsplash, sink and tapware
- Bathrooms: new tiles, glass screen, sanitary ware, fittings and floor traps
- Switches, sockets, light fixtures and air-conditioning fascias wiped
- Floors buffed according to material, then a walkthrough with you

**Not included as standard:** exterior glass beyond arm's reach from inside, facade work, skip bin rental, and repairs to defects found during the clean.

## Working inside a condo

Condo access adds steps that a landed job does not have:

1. **Management approval.** Most buildings need a work or cleaning permit and advance registration of the crew.
2. **Service lift booking.** Debris removal usually has to go through the service lift within permitted hours.
3. **Debris disposal rules.** Many managements will not accept renovation debris in the standard bin room, which is when a skip bin or a licensed disposal run becomes necessary.
4. **Working hours.** Weekday and Saturday windows are common; Sunday and public holiday work is often restricted.

Tell us your building name when you request a quote and we will plan the visit around its rules.

## Common mistakes

- **Cleaning before the contractor's final touch-ups.** You pay twice.
- **Letting anyone dry-wipe cement dust off new glass.** It scratches. See our [glass and aluminium service](/services/glass-aluminium) if damage has already happened.
- **Using household descaler on new tiles.** The wrong acid strength etches grout and can dull polished porcelain — see [cement splatter on new tiles](/problems/cement-splatter-tile-clean).
- **Ignoring dust in the air-conditioning path.** Fine dust keeps circulating and resettling — see [construction dust that keeps returning](/problems/construction-dust-returning).
- **Moving furniture in on cleaning day.** Nothing gets finished properly.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does a 1,000 sqft condo take?

Most units take a team the better part of a working day. Heavy residue or extensive floor buffing can extend it.

### Do you handle the management permit?

We provide the crew details and scope you need for the application. The unit owner or tenant normally submits it, since management deals with the registered occupant.

### Will you remove my renovation debris?

Normal leftover debris is bagged and disposed of. Large volumes need a skip bin or a licensed disposal run, quoted separately.

### Can you clean only the renovated rooms?

Yes, but dust travels. A kitchen-only clean often leaves visible residue in adjoining rooms, so we usually recommend at least a light pass through the rest of the unit.

### What if the contractor damaged something?

We will point out what we find — scratched glass, chipped tiles, hollow-sounding floors — with photos, so you can raise it while the contractor is still accountable.
