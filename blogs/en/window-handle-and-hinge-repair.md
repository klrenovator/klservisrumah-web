## What Should Be Checked Before Work

- **Window type, opening direction and hardware system.**
- **Handle spindle, gearbox and locking points.**
- **Hinge or stay corrosion and sash alignment.**
- **Seal compression, drainage and frame condition.**

## Compare the Available Approaches

| Approach | When it makes sense |
|---|---|
| **Adjust and lubricate** | hardware is sound but misaligned or dry. |
| **Replace hardware** | handle, stay, hinge or gearbox is worn. |
| **Replace window section** | frame or sash is badly distorted or corroded. |

Choosing the least expensive option without checking its limits can cause repeat work. The quotation should explain why the selected approach suits the substrate, dimensions and intended use.

## Typical Work Process

1. **Identify system and inspect all moving parts**.
2. **Support the sash and test alignment without forcing the handle**.
3. **Source compatible dimensions and fixing centres**.
4. **Replace or adjust hardware and clean contact surfaces**.
5. **Test locking, opening restriction, seals and safe closure**.

Preparation and sequencing matter. Existing defects, unstable supports, incompatible materials or damaged substrates should be corrected before they are concealed. Protect occupied rooms, common corridors and completed finishes throughout the work. At handover, test operation and inspect edges, fixings, joints and access points—not appearance alone.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Forcing a handle until the gearbox breaks.
- Replacing only the handle while lock points remain misaligned.
- Removing a stay without supporting the sash.
- Using the wrong screw or drilling into glass edges.

A competent inspection should distinguish a serviceable component from a system that has reached the point of replacement. Ask for assumptions and exclusions to be written down, especially where concealed conditions cannot be confirmed until work opens the area.

Do not bypass manufacturer instructions, building management requirements or safe access arrangements to save time. Keep product references and care instructions after handover. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why does the handle close only when the window is open?
The sash or locking points are likely misaligned under frame compression. Do not force it; alignment and hinges need checking.

### Can corroded hinges be repaired?
Light corrosion may be cleaned and adjusted; pitted, weak or seized parts should be replaced with compatible hardware.

### Does new hardware stop rain leaks?
Only when leakage is caused by poor sash compression. Failed seals, drainage or wall joints require separate repair.
