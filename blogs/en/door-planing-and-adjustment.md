# Door Planing and Adjustment in Kuala Lumpur and Selangor

**Direct answer:** A scraping door does not always need planing. Loose hinges, a twisted frame, floor movement or moisture swelling can produce the same symptom. The cause should be identified first; remove only enough material when the door itself is oversized or swollen.

This guide explains the [Door Planing and Adjustment](/services/door/door-planing-and-adjustment) scope under [door services](/services/door). The published starting price is **RM 120**. ## What Should Be Checked Before Work

- **Where the door rubs and whether the gap changes around the frame.**
- **Loose screws, worn hinges and frame movement.**
- **Moisture swelling, delamination or rot at edges.**
- **Floor finish changes and latch alignment.**

## Compare the Available Approaches

| Approach | When it makes sense |
|---|---|
| **Adjust hinges** | door has dropped but leaf dimensions remain correct. |
| **Plane and reseal** | leaf is sound but locally oversized or swollen. |
| **Repair/replace** | rot, delamination or frame distortion is substantial. |

Choosing the least expensive option without checking its limits can cause repeat work. The quotation should explain why the selected approach suits the substrate, dimensions and intended use.

## Typical Work Process

1. **Inspect gaps, hinges, floor and moisture signs**.
2. **Tighten or repair hinges before marking any cut**.
3. **Mark the minimum material to remove and protect finishes**.
4. **Plane evenly, smooth the edge and restore edge sealer**.
5. **Rehang and test swing, floor clearance and lock operation**.

Preparation and sequencing matter. Existing defects, unstable supports, incompatible materials or damaged substrates should be corrected before they are concealed. Protect occupied rooms, common corridors and completed finishes throughout the work. At handover, test operation and inspect edges, fixings, joints and access points—not appearance alone.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Planing before tightening a dropped hinge.
- Removing too much and leaving an uneven visible gap.
- Leaving a freshly cut timber edge unsealed.
- Ignoring the leak or humidity that caused swelling.

A competent inspection should distinguish a serviceable component from a system that has reached the point of replacement. Ask for assumptions and exclusions to be written down, especially where concealed conditions cannot be confirmed until work opens the area.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Will planing fix a door after new flooring?
Often, if the new floor has reduced clearance and the door and hinges are otherwise sound. Measure the required gap before cutting.

### Why did the door swell again?
The moisture source or unsealed edge may remain. Planing treats clearance, not an active leak or persistent humidity.

### Can laminate doors be planed?
Sometimes, within the construction limits. Excess removal can expose a weak core or damage the laminate, so the door must be inspected.
