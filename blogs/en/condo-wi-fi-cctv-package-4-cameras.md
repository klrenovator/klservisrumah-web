## Practical constraints in a condo

1. **Power, not just Wi-Fi.** Every camera needs mains power. If there is no socket within reach, an [electrical](/services/electrical) point has to be added first.
2. **Wi-Fi coverage at the camera, not at your phone.** A service yard behind a concrete wall may show full bars on a phone in the living room and drop out at the camera. We test before mounting.
3. **Management rules.** Cameras inside your unit are your business. Anything pointing into a corridor, lift lobby or another unit's door usually needs management approval, and some buildings prohibit it outright.
4. **Router capacity.** Four cameras streaming to the cloud add continuous upload traffic. Older ISP routers sometimes need a channel change or a mesh node.
5. **Cloud retention.** Free tiers are often only a few days of event clips. Decide what retention you actually need before buying the hardware.

## Common mistakes

- **Buying the cameras first, then asking about installation.** Positions and power availability should shape the purchase, not the other way round.
- **Pointing a camera straight at a bright balcony.** Backlight turns anyone entering into a silhouette.
- **Leaving default passwords.** Change them at handover and keep the admin credentials yourself.
- **Motion alerts on everything.** Untuned zones create so many notifications you stop reading them, which defeats the purpose.
- **Assuming footage will still be there next week.** Check the retention window — see [CCTV not recording because storage is full](/problems/cctv-not-recording-storage-full).
- **Ignoring cameras that stop uploading.** A camera that shows live view but never saves clips is a common failure — see [CCTV not recording](/problems/cctv-not-recording).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need management approval?

Not for cameras inside your unit. Cameras covering the corridor, lift lobby or common areas normally do, and often must be installed by a management-approved contractor.

### Can I take the system with me when I move?

Yes. Wireless cameras are the practical choice for tenants. Relocation and reconfiguration in the new unit starts from RM 220.

### How long is footage kept?

It depends on the cloud plan you choose. We explain the retention on each tier before you commit rather than after.

### What happens when the internet goes down?

Live view and cloud upload stop. Some models continue recording to a local microSD card, which we will point out if that matters to you.

### Can I add a fifth camera later?

Yes, from RM 420 per camera, subject to Wi-Fi coverage and power at the new position.

### Do the cameras have audio?

Most 4MP Wi-Fi models do. Recording audio of other people raises privacy considerations, so we set it according to your instruction.
