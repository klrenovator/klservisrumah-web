# Water Heater Not Working: Diagnostic & Safe Repair Guide

Question: Why is my water heater running cold, failing to turn on, or tripping the circuit breaker whenever I turn on the power switch?

Answer: The most common reasons a water heater fails in Malaysia are a burned-out copper heating element, a tripped internal thermal safety cut-out, a melted 20A double-pole (DP) wall switch terminal, or an active current leakage triggering the Earth Leakage Circuit Breaker (ELCB/RCCB). When an instant heater leaks water internally or trips power immediately, power must be cut at the DB box before professional inspection.

Water heaters combine high-voltage 230V electricity with pressurized water. Faulty wiring or compromised grounding poses severe electrical shock risks.

## 4 Common Water Heater Symptoms & What They Mean

### 1. Water Runs Cold but Power Light Is ON
The internal copper heating coil (element) has burned open, or the thermal cut-out switch tripped due to overheating (often caused by running the heater with weak water flow).

### 2. Power Indicator Light Is Completely OFF
No electrical supply is reaching the heater unit. This is frequently traced to a burned 20A DP wall switch with neon light, a blown fuse, or a tripped [RCCB / ELCB breaker](/problems/rccb-tripping-kl) in your main distribution board.

### 3. Circuit Breaker Trips Immediately When Heater Turns ON
The heating element insulation has cracked, allowing live 230V current to leak directly into the shower water, instantly triggering the unit's built-in 10mA ELCB or the main house 30mA residual current device.

### 4. Water Temperature Fluctuates from Scalding to Icy Cold
Caused by low municipal water pressure triggering the internal reed flow switch on and off. Check our guide on [low water pressure in KL](/problems/low-water-pressure-kl).

## Instant Water Heater vs Storage Tank Heater Repair

- **[Instant Water Heaters](/services/water-heater/instant-water-heater-installation):** Compact, tankless units (3.0kW to 3.8kW). Common repairs involve replacing the flow sensor, PCB controller, or upgrading to a new inverter pump model (e.g. Panasonic, Alpha, Joven, Rubine).
- **[Storage Tank Heaters](/services/water-heater/storage-tank-heater-installation):** Centralized overhead tanks (25L to 100L, 1.5kW to 3.0kW). Repairs involve replacing the immersion heating element, replacing the sacrificial magnesium anode rod to prevent tank rust, and resetting the dual thermal thermostat.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to use a water heater that occasionally trips the power?
No. An occasional trip indicates an intermittent current leak or an overheating terminal. Continued use can lead to sudden electrical shock or an electrical fire inside the wall switch.

### How long does a typical instant water heater last in Malaysia?
Most instant water heaters have a service lifespan of 5 to 8 years. If your unit is older than 7 years and starts experiencing internal electrical faults or tank leaks, replacing the entire unit is safer and more cost-effective than repeated component repairs.

### What size wiring is required for a water heater?
Under Suruhanjaya Tenaga (Energy Commission) regulations, instant water heaters must be powered by dedicated 2.5mm² or 4.0mm² cable directly from the DB box, protected by a 10mA RCD and controlled via a 20A double-pole switch with indicator.
