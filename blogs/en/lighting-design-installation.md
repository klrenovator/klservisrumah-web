Good lighting changes how a space feels: a bright, layered kitchen for cooking, warm dimmed light in a living room, and focused light over a dining table. This guide explains the main lighting types, how to plan a lighting layout, and what professional installation involves.

## Types of Lighting We Install

### LED downlights

The workhorse of modern Malaysian homes and offices — recessed ceiling lights available in various sizes (typically 3, 4, and 6 inches), colour temperatures, and beam angles.

- Clean, minimal look for living rooms, corridors, and kitchens.
- COB LED options give even, flicker-free light.
- Requires a ceiling void for recessed mounting.

### Pendants & chandeliers

Statement fixtures over dining tables, kitchen islands, and entryways.

- Mounted on reinforced hooks with safety cables for heavy fixtures.
- Available in single, cluster, and linear styles.

### Cove & strip LED lighting

Hidden LED strips in ceiling troughs or behind panels to create indirect ambient light.

- Mounted in aluminium profiles with diffusers for an even glow.
- Adds depth and a modern feel to living rooms and bedrooms.

### Track lighting

Directional spotlights on a track, popular for feature walls, galleries, and retail displays.

### Smart lighting & dimming

Wi-Fi, Zigbee, or DALI switches and dimmers that pair with Google Home, Alexa, and other platforms.

- Dimming scenes, schedules, and app control.
- Compatible with brands like Philips Hue and Tuya-based devices.

## How to Plan a Lighting Layout

A good layout layers three types of light:

1. **Ambient light** — general illumination from downlights or cove LED, giving the room its base brightness.
2. **Task light** — focused light where you work: under-cabinet strips in the kitchen, a pendant over the island, reading lights beside the sofa.
3. **Accent light** — directional light on artwork, shelves, or a feature wall to add depth.

Practical starting points:

- **Living room** — mix of downlights on a dimmer plus cove LED for evenings.
- **Kitchen** — brighter, cooler light over worktops; warm light over dining.
- **Bedroom** — dimmable warm light with separate reading fixtures.
- **Corridors** — spaced downlights or a continuous cove strip.

For larger projects, our team can calculate the number and placement of fittings using lux requirements and provide a layout before installation.

## What Installation Includes

1. **Layout planning** — positions for each fitting are confirmed, including new ceiling points where needed.
2. **Wiring** — new lighting points are wired from the nearest circuit; existing points are reused where possible.
3. **Ceiling work** — recessed downlights need a clean cut-out; cove LED needs the aluminium profile fixed and the driver concealed.
4. **Fitting installation** — fixtures are mounted securely — pendants and chandeliers get reinforced hooks and safety cables.
5. **Testing** — every fitting is tested, and dimmers or smart controls are paired and demonstrated.

All work is done by ST-registered wiremen and carries a 12-month lighting installation warranty.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does it cost to install a downlight in Malaysia?

A single LED downlight point starts from RM 120 in KL and Selangor, including the cut-out, wiring, and installation. Doing several at once lowers the per-point cost.

### Can existing light points be reused for new fittings?

In most cases yes. Recessed downlights can usually replace a central pendant if the ceiling has a suitable void; otherwise a new point is needed. The installer confirms this during the site check.

### What colour temperature should I choose?

Warm white (2700K–3000K) suits bedrooms and living areas; cool white or daylight (4000K–5000K) suits kitchens, bathrooms, and workspaces. Many homes now use adjustable fixtures to switch between the two.

### Do you do smart lighting with voice control?

Yes. We install Wi-Fi, Zigbee, and DALI smart switches and dimmers compatible with Google Home, Alexa, and Tuya-based apps, and we set them up before handover.

### Can lighting be installed in a condominium?

Yes. We regularly install lighting in condominiums across KL and Selangor, coordinating cut-out work and approval with building management where required.
