## Advantages of a Sliding Autogate System

- **Zero Driveway Intrusion:** Gate moves sideways along the fence line, allowing you to park cars right behind the gate line without obstruction.
- **Superior Wind & Weight Resistance:** Heavy sliding gates resist strong gusty monsoon winds far better than swing arms because the gate is firmly anchored in ground rails and top guide rollers.
- **High Security:** The heavy-duty pinion gear locks into the steel rack when stopped, making it virtually impossible for intruders to force open by hand.
- **Handles Heavy Gates:** Ideal for wide, heavy mild steel or wrought iron gates weighing upwards of 600kg to 1,000kg.

## Common Sliding Autogate Problems We Repair

- **Gate Stops Midway:** Debris, sand, or stones wedged inside the ground rail; worn roller bearings; or magnetic limit switch misalignment.
- **Loud Grinding Noise:** Gear rack out of pitch alignment with the motor pinion gear, causing tooth binding.
- **Motor Runs But Gate Does Not Move:** Manual clutch key is unlocked, or internal motor drive pin is sheared from high impact.
- **Remote Failure:** Module receiver failure from water ingress. Learn more at [autogate remote not working](/problems/autogate-remote-not-working).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What happens if small stones get stuck in the sliding track?
Stones can cause the gate rollers to jam or trigger the motor's anti-crush safety sensor to stop. We recommend sweeping the floor rail periodically. For low-maintenance setups, we can install protective rubber scraper flaps on gate ends.

### Can a sliding gate work if my driveway is on a slight slope?
Yes, but motor sizing and mechanical brake strength must be calculated carefully to prevent runaway sliding under gravity. Our technicians calculate slope forces during the site survey.

### Do you replace worn sliding gate wheels and rollers?
Yes, we provide on-site roller replacement using heavy-duty stainless steel bearing wheels to eliminate squeaking and ensure effortless rolling.
