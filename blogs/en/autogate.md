## What Our Autogate Installation Includes

Every full autogate installation by KL Servis Rumah follows a standard technical procedure:

1. **On-Site Assessment & Sizing:** We measure your gate width, calculate weight distribution, and inspect post hinge bearings to ensure smooth manual operation before motor mounting.
2. **Dedicated Electrical Connection:** Proper 230V AC connection with an outdoor weatherproof isolator, RCBO breaker protection, and earthing to prevent control board lightning damage.
3. **Control Board & Receiver Setup:** Microprocessor control panel setup inside a weatherproof IP65 enclosure with surge suppression and backup battery (12V/7AH) for power outages.
4. **Safety Sensor Integration:** Infrared photocell safety beams mounted across the gate line to automatically stop or reverse the gate when vehicles, children, or pets cross the path.
5. **Remote & Keypad Pairing:** Supply and programming of 2 to 4 high-frequency wireless remote controls, plus manual emergency release keys in case of total electrical failure.
6. **Testing & Handover:** Cycle testing for soft-start, soft-stop slowdown speeds, anti-crush sensitivity, and manual release operation.

## Common Autogate Problems We Fix

If your existing autogate is acting up, we diagnose and resolve issues such as:

- **[Autogate not closing or opening](/problems/autogate-not-closing):** Often caused by misaligned safety sensors, blocked ground tracks, or damaged control board relays.
- **[Autogate remote not working](/problems/autogate-remote-not-working):** Receiver frequency drift, weak remote batteries, or lightning surge damage to the antenna receiver module.
- **Gate opening slowly or grinding loudly:** Worn motor arm internal gears, dry screw jack threads, or rusted roller bearings.
- **Motor running continuously:** Limit switch failure or board relay stuck in the closed circuit position.
- **Gate dragging on driveway:** Post hinge wear or pillar structural sagging requiring [welding repair](/services/welding).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What happens to my autogate during a power outage (TNB blackout)?
All our autogate packages come with a 12V 7AH backup battery that powers 10 to 15 gate cycles during an outage. In addition, every motor arm includes a manual mechanical clutch key so you can unlock the motor and push the gate manually.

### Why does my autogate control board keep getting damaged after lightning?
Malaysia has one of the highest lightning strike densities in the world. Unprotected autogate boards connected to long outdoor cable runs absorb high-voltage transients. We install dedicated surge protectors and ensure proper grounding to safeguard the control board.

### Can you install an autogate on an old existing iron or wooden gate?
Yes, provided the gate structure is sound and hinges swing freely without excessive friction. If hinges or gate brackets are weak, our [welding and metal fabrication team](/services/welding) can reinforce the structure prior to motor mounting.

### How often should an autogate system be serviced?
We recommend an annual maintenance check to lubricate linear drive screws, clear debris from photocell lenses, test battery backup capacity, and inspect wire terminations for ant or insect infestation.
