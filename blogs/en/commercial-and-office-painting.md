# Commercial & Office Painting in KL and Selangor

**Question:** How do you paint an office or shop without disrupting the business?

**Answer:** By planning the job around operations: phasing the space into zones, working after office hours or on weekends where required, using low-odour water-based paint for occupied areas, and protecting floors, furniture, glass shopfronts and equipment so the workspace is usable again the next morning. A clear scope and agreed schedule matter more than the paint brand.

## When Businesses Repaint

- **New tenancy or fit-out** — repainting before moving furniture and staff in is far faster and cheaper.
- **End of lease** — many KL and Selangor tenancy agreements require the unit to be reinstated to its original condition; repainting is usually the core reinstatement item.
- **Rebranding** — changing feature-wall and accent colours to match a new brand palette.
- **Wear in high-traffic zones** — reception desks, corridors, lift lobbies and stairwells scuff faster than any home wall.
- **Mould and water stains** — after a plumbing or roof leak. The leak must be repaired before repainting, or the stain will bleed through again.
- **Retail refresh** — shops repaint more often than offices because appearance directly affects sales.

## What a Commercial Painting Job Includes

A properly scoped commercial repaint normally covers:

1. Site walkthrough and measurement of all wall and ceiling areas.
2. Surface preparation: crack filling, patching holes, sanding and spot-skimming damaged plaster.
3. Sealer or primer on new plaster and moisture-exposed areas.
4. Two topcoats of the agreed paint system.
5. Protection of flooring, workstations, IT equipment, glass panels, signage and air-conditioning grilles.
6. Daily cleanup so the premises can reopen each morning.
7. Final joint inspection with touch-ups before handover.

Major renovation work, electrical relocation and ceiling replacement are separate scopes, not part of a standard painting job.

## Keeping Disruption Under Control

| Method | How it helps |
|---|---|
| Zone-by-zone phasing | Staff move to a cleared zone while another is painted |
| After-hours and weekend work | Critical for retail units and clinics that cannot close |
| Low-odour, low-VOC paint | Occupied areas stay comfortable while work continues |
| Dust control and masking | Protects equipment, servers, stock and display shelving |
| Daily cleanup commitment | Premises reopen safely every morning |

Many office buildings in Kuala Lumpur require contractor registration, work permits, or specific lift and loading-bay bookings before work can start. Confirm these requirements with the building management when planning the schedule — they often determine the actual start date.

## Paint Selection for Commercial Spaces

- **High-traffic walls:** washable, scuff-resistant finishes (typically satin or soft-sheen) that tolerate repeated cleaning.
- **Retail and reception:** exact brand-colour matching from physical swatches; screen colours are not reliable for matching.
- **Pantries and washrooms:** moisture-tolerant coating systems.
- **Ceilings:** flat, non-reflective finishes that hide unevenness and resist staining.

## Process

1. **Walkthrough** — measure areas, photograph defects, confirm constraints.
2. **Scope and quotation** — paint system, protection level, schedule and phasing.
3. **Colour confirmation** — from physical catalogues, approved in writing.
4. **Protection and masking** — floors, furniture, glass, electrical points.
5. **Preparation** — crack filling, sanding, sealer.
6. **Topcoats** — two coats with drying time between them.
7. **Cleanup and joint inspection** — snagging list cleared before handover.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Painting over damp or leak-stained walls without fixing the source first.
- Approving colours from a phone screen instead of a painted test patch.
- Scheduling work before confirming building management permits.
- Choosing the cheapest chalky paint for a rental unit — it scuffs within months and costs more at the next reinstatement.
- Leaving protection of epoxy floors, glass shopfronts and signboards out of the written scope.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can painting run while the office or shop stays open?
Yes, in most cases. Work is phased by zone and low-odour water-based paint is used for occupied areas. Strong-smelling preparation work, such as heavy sanding, is scheduled outside operating hours.

### Can you match our exact brand colours?
Colours are matched from official physical colour catalogues and test patches. For critical brand walls, a sample patch is painted and approved before the full coat proceeds.
