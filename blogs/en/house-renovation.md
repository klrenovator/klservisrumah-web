## Planning a Renovation in Malaysia

1. **Define your budget and scope** — decide what you want to change and what you can spend.
2. **Consider the approval requirements** — strata properties (condos) may require management approval for certain works.
3. **Get a clear quote** — an itemised quote covering works, materials, and timeline.
4. **Plan the disruption** — renovation affects your daily routine; plan around it.
5. **Allow for contingencies** — unexpected issues (e.g. old wiring) can arise.

## What a Professional Renovation Involves

A typical renovation includes planning, demolition or preparation, structural and finish work (plumbing, electrical, tiling, painting, cabinetry), and a final handover. Coordination between trades is key to a smooth project.
