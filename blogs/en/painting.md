## Why Surface Preparation Matters in Malaysia

Skipping preparation is the most common reason paint fails in Malaysian homes. Before we paint, we:

1. **Protect and mask** — furniture, floors, switches, and windows are covered with drop sheets and quality masking tape for a zero-mess handover.
2. **Prepare the surface** — cracks, nail holes, and indents are filled with high-grade plaster and sanded smooth.
3. **Prime and seal** — a premium sealer or undercoat protects against moisture seepage and ensures the topcoat adheres.
4. **Apply two topcoats** — two coats of premium Nippon Paint or Dulux for rich, even coverage.
5. **Inspect and clean** — we remove all covers, vacuum the space, and do a joint inspection with you.

This is why a professional finish lasts years longer than a quick repaint over an unprepared wall.

## Signs Your Walls Need Repainting

- **Fading or chalking** — rubbing the wall leaves white powder on your hand.
- **Peeling or bubbling** — often caused by moisture behind the paint.
- **Cracks and flaking** — especially near windows, skirting, and bathroom edges.
- **Mould or mildew spots** — common in bathrooms, kitchens, and near air-conditioners.
- **Stains and marks** — that do not wash off with normal cleaning.

If you can see any of these, a repaint will not fix the cause unless the underlying surface is repaired and sealed first. Our [peeling paint repair guide](/problems/peeling-paint-malaysia) explains the common causes and the correct fix.

## Choosing the Right Paint for Malaysian Weather

- **Interior:** low-odour, low-VOC washable paints (e.g. Nippon Odour-less, Nippon Spot-less, Dulux EasyClean) are easy to clean and dry quickly in high humidity.
- **Exterior:** weatherproof, anti-fungal, and UV-resistant coatings (e.g. Nippon Weatherbond, Dulux Weathershield, Jotun) handle sun and rain best.
- **Colour:** light neutral tones brighten compact spaces, while dark shades on west-facing walls fade faster under direct sun.

Our team brings colour catalogues to your site inspection so you can choose with confidence.

## Tips to Make Your Paint Job Last

- Fix moisture problems before painting — paint will not stop a leaking pipe or rising damp.
- Allow enough drying time between coats, especially in humid weather.
- Clean walls gently with a damp cloth; avoid harsh scrubbing on matte finishes.
- Touch up small scratches early to prevent them from spreading.
