# Kitchen Cabinet Doors Sagging or Misaligned? What to Check

**Question:** Why is one kitchen cabinet door drooping, rubbing against another door, or not closing flush?

**Answer:** A sagging kitchen cabinet door is most often caused by loose hinge screws, a worn soft-close hinge, a pulled-out mounting plate, or a door that is too heavy for its hinge count. A carpenter can usually adjust or replace the hinges; carcass or plywood damage may need reinforcement or door replacement.

This article is about **kitchen cabinet doors**. If a [wardrobe door jams or will not close](/problems/wardrobe-door-jamming), the track, hinge and sliding hardware are often different.

## What Causes Cabinet Doors to Sag?

### Loose cabinet hinges
Standard concealed hinges have adjustment screws and mounting screws. After years of opening and closing, screws can loosen, especially on particleboard or MDF cabinets where the thread no longer grips tightly.

### Worn soft-close mechanism
A hinge that no longer holds its adjustment, slams shut, or feels floppy may be worn out. Replacement hinges are more reliable than repeated tightening.

### Pulled-out mounting plate
When screws strip out of the cabinet box, the mounting plate shifts and the door drops. A longer screw, timber insert, dowel repair or relocated hinge may be needed.

### Too few hinges for the door height
Tall or heavy doors need at least three hinges. Two hinges on a very tall door can slowly bend under the weight.

## Signs It Is a Hinge Problem, Not a Carcass Problem

| Sign | Likely issue | Typical fix |
|---|---|---|
| Door tilts at one corner | Loose top or bottom hinge | Adjust and tighten |
| Gap uneven along the edge | Hinge adjustment screw | Reset alignment |
| Screw spins without tightening | Stripped hole | Repair hole or move hinge |
| Door feels heavy/loose when lifted | Worn hinge or plate | Replace hinge |
| Cabinet side is bulging or wet | Carcass damage | Reinforce/replace section |

Also check for water damage under the sink. Persistent dampness can swell particleboard and make hinges pull out. If the cabinet is wet, inspect for a [leaking bathroom or kitchen pipe](/problems/leaking-bathroom) before repairing the woodwork.

## Can You Adjust It Yourself?

Concealed hinges usually have three screws:

1. The mounting screw attaches the plate to the cabinet.
2. The side-to-side screw moves the door horizontally.
3. The depth or height screw brings the door flush.

Make small turns and test the door after each one. If a screw will not tighten, stop — forcing it can enlarge the hole and make the repair bigger.

## What Professional Repair Involves

1. Remove the door and inspect the hinge, plate and board.
2. Tighten or relocate loose fixings.
3. Repair stripped holes with suitable inserts or timber support.
4. Replace damaged hinges or mounting plates.
5. Rehang, align and test the soft-close action.

## When Repair Is Not Enough

Consider cabinet or door replacement when:

- The cabinet box is swollen from water.
- Multiple hinges repeatedly pull out.
- Doors are warped beyond adjustment.
- The laminate or plywood is delaminating.
- You are already planning a kitchen makeover.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a sagging cabinet door be fixed without replacing it?
Usually yes. Tightening, adjusting or replacing hinges solves most cases. Replacement is mainly needed when the board is stripped, swollen or damaged.

### Why do hinge screws keep coming loose?
The screw hole may be stripped, especially in MDF or particleboard. A carpenter can repair the hole, add an insert, move the hinge, or use a longer fixing into solid timber.
